GTM & Sales Leadership Metrics Cheat Sheet

Every formula that matters in sales and GTM leadership — CAC payback, magic number, quota attainment, Rule of 40, and more — defined with benchmarks on one printable page.

What's inside

  • Pipeline & funnel metrics: coverage ratio, win rate, sales cycle length, ASP, stage conversion
  • Efficiency metrics: CAC, CAC payback, magic number, LTV:CAC, burn multiple
  • Growth & health metrics: Rule of 40, ARR growth rate, gross margin
  • Retention & expansion metrics: GRR, NRR, logo churn, expansion rate, net dollar retention by cohort
  • Productivity metrics: quota attainment %, rep productivity, ramp time, activity-to-outcome ratios
  • Forecasting metrics: forecast accuracy, commit-to-actual variance, slip rate, pipeline creation rate
  • A one-line "good/warning/bad" benchmark range attached to every formula

One formula per line. Benchmarks are directional (B2B SaaS-oriented) — recalibrate to your own segment and stage after 3-4 quarters of your own data.

Cold Email Copy Review Scorecard

A 5-criteria, 1-to-5 rubric — clarity, relevance, CTA, personalization, brevity — for grading a rep's cold email before it ships, with a concrete description for every score on every criterion.

What's inside

  • Clarity: 1–5 rubric with descriptions per level
  • Relevance: 1–5 rubric with descriptions per level
  • CTA: 1–5 rubric with descriptions per level
  • Personalization: 1–5 rubric with descriptions per level
  • Brevity: 1–5 rubric with word-count anchors per level
  • Scoring bands (/25): Ship / Revise / Rewrite
  • Reviewer instructions for consistent scoring across reps

Score every draft against all 5 criteria before you talk to the rep about it. Cite the specific sentence responsible for the score on each criterion — "it felt generic" isn't feedback a rep can act on.

1. Clarity (1–5)

ScoreDescription
1Reader can't tell what's being offered or asked, even after re-reading
2Message is present but buried in throat-clearing or jargon
3Clear on a second read
4Clear on first read, single throughline, no competing ideas
5Clear on first read AND a non-expert could explain what it's asking in one sentence

2. Relevance (1–5)

ScoreDescription
1Could be sent to anyone at any company
2Vague industry reference only ("companies like yours")
3References the specific company or segment by name
4References a specific trigger or context for this account
5References a specific trigger AND ties it directly to a stated priority of this persona

3. CTA (1–5)

ScoreDescription
1No clear ask
2Ask is vague ("let me know if you're interested")
3Ask is clear but high-friction ("book 30 minutes on my calendar")
4Ask is clear and low-friction (a direct yes/no question)
5Ask is a single low-friction question AND makes it easy to say no without ending the conversation

4. Personalization (1–5)

ScoreDescription
1No personalization — pure copy-paste
2Name/company merge-fielded only
3One specific detail about the recipient or account
4Detail is meaningfully tied to the offer, not just trivia
5Detail shows real research (recent post, hire, launch, earnings call) tied directly to the ask

5. Brevity (1–5)

ScoreDescription
1Over 200 words / multiple paragraphs
2150–200 words
3100–150 words
475–100 words
5Under 75 words, one idea per sentence

Total score: ___ / 25

RangeVerdict
20–25Ship it
15–19Revise the specific criteria that scored below 4 before sending
Under 15Rewrite from scratch — patching a low-scoring draft rarely lifts it past a 15

Reviewer instructions

  1. Score independently, criterion by criterion, before forming an overall impression.
  2. For every score below 5, write the one sentence in the email that caused the deduction.
  3. Review with the rep criterion by criterion, not as one blended "this needs work" comment.
  4. Track scores over time per rep — a rep who's consistently weak on the same criterion needs coaching on that specific skill, not another round of edits on this specific email.

How to use it

Score the draft cold and independently against each of the 5 criteria before discussing it with the writer, cite the exact sentence that earned or lost the points, then total the score to decide ship, revise, or rewrite.

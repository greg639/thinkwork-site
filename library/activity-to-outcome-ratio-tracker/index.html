Activity-to-Outcome Ratio Tracker

A month-by-month tracker that turns your raw call, email, and meeting counts into the ratios that actually predict quota attainment: so you know the real "cost" of one closed deal before the quarter ends, not after.

How to use it

Copy the tables into a spreadsheet, log activity weekly rather than from memory, and let the ratio formulas run automatically: review the rolling trend and diagnostic questions once a month, and the quarterly roll-up at quarter close.

What's inside

  • Monthly raw activity input log (calls, emails, meetings, opportunities, closed-won)
  • Core ratio formulas: Connect Rate, Call-to-Meeting, Email/LinkedIn Reply Rate, Show Rate, Meeting-to-Opportunity, Win Rate
  • The Blended Activity-to-Close Ratio ("cost of one deal" number) with a worked example
  • 3-month rolling trend table with trend-direction column
  • Benchmark reference ranges by funnel stage
  • Diagnostic questions triggered by a >15% ratio swing at each stage
  • Quarterly roll-up summary block

Track the real ratio of activity to outcome, month over month, so you always know how many calls, emails, and meetings it takes you specifically to land one closed deal, and can catch a slipping ratio while there's still a quarter left to fix it.

How to use this tracker

  1. Copy the Monthly Input Log table into a spreadsheet (one row per month).
  2. At the end of each week, log your raw counts. Don't wait for month-end, a ratio built from memory is a guess.
  3. Let the Ratio Calculations section run off your raw numbers (formulas included).
  4. Compare against the Benchmark Reference Ranges.
  5. Any ratio that moves more than 15% against your own 3-month average triggers the Diagnostic Questions for that stage.

2. Ratio Calculations (apply per month)

RatioFormulaWhat it tells you
Connect RateCalls Connected ÷ Calls MadeHow much of your dial time is reaching a live person
Call-to-Meeting RatioMeetings Booked ÷ Calls ConnectedHow persuasive your live-call ask is
Email Reply RateEmails Replied ÷ Emails SentWhether your subject line / opener is working
LinkedIn Reply RateLinkedIn Replies ÷ LinkedIn Touches SentWhether your social outreach is landing
Show RateMeetings Held ÷ Meetings BookedWhether your booked meetings actually happen
Meeting-to-Opportunity RatioOpportunities Created ÷ Meetings HeldWhether your meetings are qualifying real pipeline
Win RateDeals Closed-Won ÷ Opportunities CreatedWhether pipeline you create actually closes
Blended Activity-to-Close Ratio(Calls Made + Emails Sent + LinkedIn Touches Sent) ÷ Deals Closed-WonThe number that matters most: how many total touches it took to close one deal this month

Worked example: 220 calls + 340 emails + 60 LinkedIn touches = 620 touches. 1 deal closed. Your Blended Activity-to-Close Ratio is 620:1 for that month. If your quota needs 4 deals a month, you need to be running roughly 2,480 touches a month at this efficiency, or you need to improve the ratio.

4. Benchmark Reference Ranges (general B2B mid-market guide: recalibrate to your own 3-month average as the real baseline)

StageTypical range
Connect Rate (outbound cold call)5–15%
Call-to-Meeting Ratio1 meeting per 8–15 connects
Cold Email Reply Rate1–3% for pure cold, 8–15% for warm/referral
LinkedIn Reply Rate10–25% (higher-touch, lower-volume channel)
Show Rate70–85% (below 70% signals a qualification or confirmation-process problem)
Meeting-to-Opportunity Ratio30–50%
Win Rate20–30% for competitive B2B SaaS; varies heavily by deal size and segment

These are directional, not prescriptive, a founder-led motion into a warm network will beat these; a pure cold-outbound SDR motion into an enterprise segment may run below them. Your own trailing 3-month average is the real benchmark. Use the published ranges only to sanity-check whether a stage is structurally broken (e.g., a 2% show rate is a process failure, not a bad month).

5. Diagnostic Questions (run these when a ratio moves >15% against your own average)

Connect Rate dropped:

  • Am I calling the same list/segment, or has my target list shifted (worse data, wrong persona, wrong time-of-day)?
  • Has my calling window shifted away from when this persona actually picks up?

Call-to-Meeting Ratio dropped:

  • Am I still opening with a reason-to-meet that's specific to this account, or has my opener gone generic?
  • Am I asking for the meeting directly, or hinting at it?

Email/LinkedIn Reply Rate dropped:

  • Has my subject line / opening line gone stale (same one for more than 3 weeks)?
  • Am I personalizing to a trigger event, or sending the same template to everyone?

Show Rate dropped:

  • Am I sending a confirmation + reminder sequence, or just booking and hoping?
  • Is there real value promised in the invite, or just "quick intro call"?

Meeting-to-Opportunity Ratio dropped:

  • Am I qualifying (budget/authority/need/timeline) in the meeting, or just presenting?
  • Are meetings being booked with the wrong persona (no real problem to solve)?

Win Rate dropped:

  • Is this a deal-quality issue (wrong-fit opportunities getting created) or a deal-execution issue (right opportunities, losing them late)?
  • Cross-reference against the Deal Slippage Analysis Template to find the root cause.

What it changes

Every pound a player owes you, paid up front or on the list at the gate.

  • Saturday
    Empty places show on Monday, not on Saturday. Every running day shows booked against capacity, and a player can book at eleven on a Friday night from the pub. No account, no app.
  • No-shows
    A deposit or the full game fee is taken when they book, and whatever they paid is yours. What is still due sits against the booking, so nobody at the gate is guessing.
  • Parties
    An enquiry does not hold a slot. A deposit does. Quote, deposit, balance, played: one list in the console, nothing on your phone at nine at night.
  • The gate
    Waivers are signed on the player's own phone before the day. Reception shows red for anyone who has not, and nobody is checked in until they have.
  • Your money
    Card money is paid into your Stripe account, not ours. AOC keeps 2% of each online booking and nothing else. Bookings you key in at the desk cost nothing.
Where the money moves

Not described. Shown.

Every screen below is the real console, running on a demonstration site with sample bookings. Nothing is a mock-up, and every number on a screen is a sample number.

01 · Booking on a phone

Paid when they book, not when they turn up.

  • Only the days you run are open, with places left on each. A full day says sold out and what to do instead.
  • Deposit or the full fee by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe. Walk-ons can pay at the gate where you allow it.
  • Rental kit per player, or everyone at once. The booking says who hires, so you know how many rifles to have ready.
  • Under-18s sign with a parent or guardian. Anyone under your minimum age is refused by the server, whatever the page says.
  • The confirmation by email, with a pass to scan at the gate and a link for mates to sign their own disclaimer before the day.
02 · Money

Your Stripe account. 2% to us, the rest is yours.

  • Card money is yours: it is paid to your own Stripe account at the moment of booking and paid out to your bank on Stripe's schedule. It never sits with us.
  • Every booking with what was paid online and what is due at the gate, searchable by name or reference.
  • Refunds from the booking, with the amount and the method shown before you confirm, back the way it came, inside the refund window you set.
  • Phone bookings and walk-ups added by hand, cash or transfer recorded, under the same rules as the site. No 2% on those.
03 · Groups and parties

A deposit holds the date. An enquiry does not.

  • Take the enquiry on the screen: organiser, occasion, session, date, how many. It goes on the list, and the slot is not held until the deposit is in.
  • Send the quote from the console, with the deposit worked out from your percentage.
  • Deposit and balance recorded against the party however they were paid: card, bank transfer or cash. The balance shows until it is in.
  • Every parent gets a disclaimer link. Check the whole party in at once on the day, then mark it played.
04 · Reception

Signed at home, so the gate moves.

  • One card per player for the day. Tap to check in. Green is in. Red has not signed the disclaimer: sign it on the tablet, there and then.
  • Scan the pass from the confirmation email and the booking opens. No name to spell at the window.
  • Medical notes and an emergency contact show on a tap, to the people who should see them, and are deleted after the game day on a timer you set.
  • Kit issued in one press for the session, and a printable day sheet for the marshals.
05 · Calendar

See the empty places on Monday, not Saturday.

  • Month and week views built from your running days and your events, with places booked over places available on each one.
  • A day panel for any day: sessions, players booked, checked in, disclaimers outstanding, kit to issue, money taken online.
  • Needs attention names the things to do: a medical note to read, a quote to send, a deposit to chase.
  • Capacity per session and per area, with a one-day override for a big game.
06 · Members

Renew the whole membership in one email.

  • Your plans, a year or a month, with what each one saves a member at the checkout.
  • Everyone on a plan with visits this year, saved so far and status. Add a member at the desk in four fields.
  • Renew all by email in one run when the season turns. Who has paid, who has not, who has lapsed.
07 · Settings

Change a price once. It changes everywhere.

  • Sessions, prices and places on one screen. A price change is on the site and in the checkout at once.
  • Pay online or on the day for walk-ons, your call. Deposit percentage and refund window per brand.
  • Brands and venues: more than one brand on one account, each with its own name, colours and booking page.
  • Email from your name, and on Expert from your own domain. The console carries your name and your colours.

Questions

The ones every site owner asks.

When does the card money reach my bank?

It is paid into your own Stripe account at the moment the player pays, and Stripe pays it out to your bank on Stripe's payout schedule. It never passes through us. Our 2% is taken at the same moment, so there is no invoice for it and nothing to reconcile.

What does it cost to take money?

Two per cent of each online booking, and Stripe's card fee. Bookings you key in at the desk cost nothing. Monthly plan prices are on request.

Do players pay a deposit or the whole thing?

Your choice. A deposit or the full game fee online, and walk-ons can pay at the gate where you allow it. Parties pay a deposit to hold the date, at the percentage you set, and the balance before the day.

What happens when someone does not turn up?

Whatever they paid when they booked is already in your Stripe account, and the booking shows what was still due. A refund is your call, inside the window you set in days before the game day: from the booking, with the amount and the method shown before you confirm, and the money goes back the way it came. A cash or bank-transfer payment is recorded as refunded.

How do waivers work?

The person booking signs the site's disclaimer in the flow. Everyone else on the booking signs their own, by the link in the confirmation email or on the tablet at reception. Reception shows who has not signed in red, and nobody is checked in until they have. Each player is asked whether there is anything the marshals should know; the answer and an emergency contact are stored apart from the booking, shown to the people who need them, and deleted after the game day on the timer you set.

What about under-18s?

You set the minimum age. Below it, the booking is refused by the server, whatever the page says. Between the minimum and 18, a parent or guardian signs with the player, in the flow or at the desk. The emergency contact is required for them.

I run more than one brand, or more than one site.

More than one brand on one account is in Pro: each brand has its own name, colours, booking page and email voice, and reception can show one brand or all of them. More than one venue, each with its own sessions and capacity, is in Expert.

Do we need a new website?

No. The booking flow is one script tag on the site you have, and it opens as a full-screen sheet on a phone inside your page. If you want a new site, ThinkWork builds airsoft and adventure sites with the flow built in.

Can emails come from our own domain?

On Expert, yes. Name the domain in Settings, add the records we show you, press check, and every booking email and campaign is sent from your address. On Lite and Pro, emails are sent from AOC in your name, with replies going to you.

Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Template

A fillable worksheet for defining firmographic, technographic, and behavioral fit criteria before reps ever qualify a lead — so qualification starts from a documented standard, not gut feel.

What's inside

  • Firmographic fields: industry, company size, revenue band, geography, growth stage
  • Technographic fields: required stack, integration dependencies, disqualifying tech
  • Behavioral/trigger fields: buying triggers, existing tool pain signals, content engagement patterns
  • Organizational fields: typical buying committee, economic buyer title, average deal size and cycle length
  • Anti-ICP section — explicit disqualifiers
  • Tiering section: Tier 1 (best-fit) vs. Tier 2 (workable) vs. out-of-ICP
  • Validation step: cross-check against your last 20 closed-won deals

Fill in every field before this ICP is used to score inbound leads.

Firmographic

  • Target industries: _______________
  • Company size (employees): _______________
  • Revenue band: _______________
  • Geography served: _______________
  • Growth stage (startup / scaling / mature): _______________

Technographic

  • Required existing tools/stack: _______________
  • Key integration dependencies: _______________
  • Disqualifying tech (incompatible or competitive lock-in): _______________

Behavioral / trigger signals

  • Buying triggers that predict fit (e.g., recent funding, new leadership hire, compliance deadline): _______________
  • Pain signals from their current tools (e.g., manual workaround visible in job postings, public complaints): _______________
  • Content engagement pattern of a good-fit lead (pages, assets, time-to-convert): _______________

Organizational

  • Typical buying committee (roles/titles involved): _______________
  • Economic buyer title: _______________
  • Average deal size for this ICP: _______________
  • Average sales cycle length: _______________

Anti-ICP (explicit disqualifiers)

  • Company size below: _______________
  • Industries excluded: _______________
  • Geography not served: _______________
  • Any other hard disqualifier (e.g., no budget authority reachable, incompatible procurement process): _______________

Tiering

  • Tier 1 (best-fit): matches all firmographic + technographic + at least one trigger signal.
  • Tier 2 (workable): matches firmographic but missing a trigger or technographic fit.
  • Out-of-ICP: fails 2+ firmographic criteria or hits an anti-ICP disqualifier.

Validation step

Pull your last 20 closed-won deals. What percentage match this ICP as written? If fewer than 70% match, the ICP is miscalibrated — revise firmographic bands before rolling this out to the qualification scoring model.

How to use it

Complete every field with your leadership team, validate it against your last 20 closed-won deals, then hand the Tier 1/Tier 2/Out-of-ICP definitions to marketing and SDRs as the standard for lead scoring.

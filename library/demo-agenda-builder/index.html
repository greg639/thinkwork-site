Demo Agenda Builder

A manual, step-by-step agenda-generation tool: fill in your discovery notes, run them through the role-to-segment lookup table and time-budget formula, and get a stakeholder-mapped agenda ready to send.

How to use it

Fill in the stakeholder worksheet from your discovery notes, run each attendee's role through the lookup table to get their segment and time share, apply the time-budget formula for your meeting length, then drop the results into the agenda template and send it before the meeting.

What's inside

  • Step 1: stakeholder input worksheet (name, role type, priority/pain, attending, time allocated)
  • Step 2: role-to-agenda-segment lookup table for 5 common buyer roles
  • Step 3: time-budget formula and percentage table for 30/45/60-minute meetings
  • Step 4: fill-in-the-blank agenda output template
  • A fully worked example: 45-minute demo, 4 stakeholders
  • A send-ready pre-meeting agenda email template

How this tool works

This is a 4-step manual builder. Run your discovery notes through the steps in order and you'll produce a tailored, stakeholder-mapped agenda: no software required, just this worksheet.

Step 2: Role-to-segment lookup table

For each role type present, this table tells you what agenda segment to build for them, how much time it needs, and which outcome lens to lead with.

Role typeAgenda segment to includeTime shareLead with
Economic Buyer"Business case" segment: ROI, cost of status quo, timeline to value15-20%Results (revenue, cost, risk)
Champion/UserCore walkthrough tied directly to their stated pain40-50%Role outcomes (their day-to-day gets easier)
Technical EvaluatorArchitecture/security/integration segment, ideally with your SE15-20%Feasibility and risk-of-adoption
End UserHands-on segment: let them see or touch the actual workflow15-20%Day-to-day usability
Influencer/SkepticDirect address of their known objection, named explicitly10-15%Whatever removes their specific blocker

If a role isn't attending, redistribute its time share proportionally across the roles that are.

Step 3, Time-budget formula

Formula: Segment minutes = (Total meeting minutes − Buffer) × Segment %

Buffer = opening (intros/agenda-setting) + closing (next steps), reserve this off the top before allocating segment time.

Meeting lengthBuffer (open+close)Time available for segments
30 min6 min24 min
45 min8 min37 min
60 min10 min50 min

Apply the Step 2 percentages to the "available" column, not the total meeting length.

``` DEMO AGENDA, [Company] × [Buyer Company] Date/Time: [ ] Duration: [ ] min Attendees: [ ]
  1. [Champion/User segment name] [X] min

```

Worked example: 45-minute demo, 4 stakeholders

Attendees: VP Sales (Economic Buyer), RevOps Manager (Champion), IT Security Lead (Technical Evaluator), Senior Rep (End User).

Buffer: 8 min. Available: 37 min.

Segment%Minutes
Champion (RevOps pain: manual reporting)45%17
Economic Buyer (ROI on rep time saved)20%7
Technical Evaluator (SSO + data residency)20%7
End User (rep's daily workflow)15%6

Final agenda: Open (4) → RevOps walkthrough (17) → Business case (7) → Security/architecture (7) → Rep hands-on (6) → Close (4) = 45 min.

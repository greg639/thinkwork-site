Manager Skill-Observation Scorecard

A structured behavior checklist for live call and deal shadowing that ties every rating to a named skill and a direct quote, replacing gut-feel scoring with evidence a manager can coach from and a rep can act on.

How to use it

Print or duplicate one scorecard per shadowed call, fill in evidence for every score in real time, and deliver the SBI-framed debrief within 24 hours; log section totals into the trend tracker so the rep's skill grade reflects a pattern, not a single call.

What's inside

  • Pre-call setup checklist (what to review before you shadow)
  • 6 skill sections: Opening, Discovery, Qualification, Value/Positioning, Objection Handling, Next-Steps/Close
  • 28 named behavior items, each tagged to a specific skill and scored on a 4-point scale
  • Evidence field requiring a direct quote/timestamp for every score of 1 or 4
  • Post-call debrief script for delivering feedback within 24 hours
  • Trend-tracking row for logging scores across a rep's last 5 observed calls
  • Manager self-calibration note for cross-checking scores against a peer manager
  • Guidance on cadence (minimum observations per rep per month)

Use this on every live call or deal you shadow. Score behaviors, not vibes: every rating needs a quote or a timestamp behind it, or it doesn't count.

Rating scale: 1 = Not observed/Missing · 2 = Developing · 3 = Proficient · 4 = Strong (worth using as a peer example)

Pre-Call Setup

  • I reviewed this rep's last 2 observation scorecards before this call
  • I know what stage this deal is in and what the rep's stated goal for the call is
  • I have a notepad ready for direct quotes: I am not typing a summary, I am capturing evidence

Post-Call Debrief (deliver within 24 hours)

Use SBI for every point of feedback:

  1. Situation: "On the call with [account] at [timestamp]..."
  2. Behavior: "...when [specific observed behavior]..."
  3. Impact: "...the effect was [specific consequence for the deal/conversation]."

End every debrief with one question, not one instruction: "What would you try differently next time?", let the rep name the fix before you name it for them.

Cadence & Calibration

  • Minimum cadence: 2 observed calls per rep per month, calendarized (not "whenever I have time")
  • Calibration: once a quarter, score the same recorded call independently alongside a peer manager and compare: if your scores differ by more than 1 point on any item, discuss what you each counted as evidence before your next observation cycle

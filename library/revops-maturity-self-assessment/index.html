RevOps Maturity Self-Assessment

25 questions across five pillars that place your revenue operation on a 4-stage maturity curve and flag your single biggest leak.

What's inside

  • Five pillar structure — Data, Process, Technology, People & Enablement, Forecasting & Reporting
  • 25 scored questions, five per pillar
  • 0–3 scoring key applied to every question
  • Four maturity stage bands — Ad Hoc, Defined, Managed, Optimized
  • Pillar-level score-and-rank breakdown table
  • Biggest-leak detection rule (lowest-scoring pillar, not overall score)
  • Stage-specific next actions for wherever you land

Purpose: 25 questions across the five pillars that make up a functioning revenue operation. Answer honestly — score based on what's actually true today, not what's on the roadmap — to place your organization on a 4-stage maturity curve and find your single biggest leak.

How to score

For each question, score:

  • 0 — Not true / doesn't exist
  • 1 — Exists informally, inconsistently applied
  • 2 — Exists formally, applied most of the time
  • 3 — Exists formally, applied consistently, and is measured/reviewed

Pillar 1 — Data

  1. We have one declared system of record for every core revenue field (no field is "owned" by two systems). ___
  2. Our CRM data (contacts, accounts, opportunities) is deduplicated and cleaned on a defined schedule. ___
  3. We can trace any dashboard number back to its source records without manual reconciliation. ___
  4. New leads/accounts are validated and enriched automatically, not manually. ___
  5. We have a documented data governance owner (a person, not "the team"). ___

Pillar 1 subtotal: ___ / 15

Pillar 2 — Process

  1. Every pipeline stage has written, objective exit criteria (not "rep's judgment"). ___
  2. Lead routing (marketing → sales) is automated and rule-based, not manual triage. ___
  3. We have a documented, current sales process that new hires are trained on in their first month. ___
  4. Deal handoffs (SDR→AE, AE→CS) follow a defined checklist, not tribal knowledge. ___
  5. We review and update our sales process at least twice a year based on data, not just intuition. ___

Pillar 2 subtotal: ___ / 15

Pillar 3 — Technology

  1. Every tool in our stack has a named business owner (see the RevOps Tech Stack Audit Checklist). ___
  2. Our core systems (CRM, MAP, billing, CS platform) are integrated with monitored, healthy syncs. ___
  3. We've audited our tech stack for redundant tools in the last 12 months. ___
  4. Admin access and security review happens on a defined schedule, not reactively. ___
  5. Adding or removing a tool from our stack follows a review process, not ad hoc procurement. ___

Pillar 3 subtotal: ___ / 15

Pillar 4 — People & Enablement

  1. New reps hit full productivity (ramped quota attainment) within a defined, tracked timeframe. ___
  2. We have a defined onboarding curriculum, not "shadow a senior rep for two weeks." ___
  3. Comp plans are reviewed against the actual behavior they incentivize at least annually. ___
  4. Managers run structured deal reviews (not just pipeline list reviews) on a regular cadence. ___
  5. We track and coach against a defined set of skill/competency areas, not just quota attainment. ___

Pillar 4 subtotal: ___ / 15

Pillar 5 — Forecasting & Reporting

  1. We measure forecast accuracy by rep and by category (see the Sales Forecast Accuracy Scorecard), not just at the team level. ___
  2. Our forecast roll-up process takes less than a day of manual spreadsheet work. ___
  3. Leadership trusts the CRM-generated forecast enough to not need a "shadow" spreadsheet forecast. ___
  4. We can answer "which funnel stage is our biggest leak" with data, not opinion. ___
  5. Our dashboards are self-serve — reps and managers can answer their own questions without asking RevOps for a custom report. ___

Pillar 5 subtotal: ___ / 15

Total score: ___ / 75

Find your biggest leak

  • Calculate each pillar's subtotal as a percentage of its max (15 points)
  • The pillar with the lowest percentage score is your single biggest leak — fix that pillar before investing further in the others, since a strong Forecasting pillar built on a weak Data pillar will not hold up

Next actions by stage

  • Stage 1 (Ad Hoc): Start with Data — declare a system of record for your 10 core fields before building any new process or dashboard on top of unreliable data.
  • Stage 2 (Defined): Focus on consistency — pick your lowest-scoring pillar and turn one informal practice into a documented, enforced one this quarter.
  • Stage 3 (Managed): Focus on measurement — instrument the pillar you can't currently put a number on (usually Forecasting or People).
  • Stage 4 (Optimized): Focus on cadence — formalize the review rhythm (quarterly stack audit, monthly hygiene audit, monthly forecast scorecard) so maturity doesn't silently decay.

How to use it

Score all 25 questions honestly in one sitting, total each pillar, and use the lowest-scoring pillar — not the overall stage label — to decide what to fix first.

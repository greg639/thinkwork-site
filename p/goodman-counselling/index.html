How the work goes

No commitment until you have heard my voice.

The basis of my work is person-centred, which means it has a core of respect for you and a belief that you have the capacity for growth, well being and integrity. I also draw on many other counselling approaches, so the process can be one that feels right for you.

First, a phone call
A free initial 20 minute meeting to explore what it is you would like to look at, and whether we both feel we could work together effectively on it. There is also the option of a longer first meeting and first session in one, charged at the standard rate, if you would rather make a proper start straight away.
Then, an hour a week
I usually work in weekly sessions of 50 minutes, at the same time each week. In some circumstances sessions may be longer or shorter, and are charged accordingly.
For as long as it helps
Short term and longer term work, depending on how you want to work and what you feel you would benefit from working on. That is decided with you, not for you, and it can change.
In the room or on a screen
I recommend working face to face wherever possible. Where that is not practical I also work by phone and online using FaceTime, Teams. There are daytime and evening appointments.
Confidential
What you say in the room stays in the room. It is a special relationship, and the content of it is yours.

About Robert

Qualified, and doing this work since 1999.

I am an experienced and qualified counsellor and psychotherapist working in Eastbourne, Hastings and East Sussex. I have a warm, welcoming and empathic approach which is non-judgemental and moves at a pace appropriate for each client.

In his own words

Three pieces Robert has written, which say more about an hour with him than any description of it could.

You have retired. Congratulations. Err, now what?

When overworked, many of us dream of the life that retirement will bring. We often overlook how it might feel to not have a daily purpose, a role that defines us and sometimes gives us status.

Counselling provides the time and space to explore who we are without fear of hurting others or needing to live up to other people's expectations. Sometimes, people surprise themselves.

Robert Goodman

Mother of all days

As a professional therapist, I find that most clients explore their relationship with their mothers at some point. For some it is the burning core around which all other life events revolve, for others it is an appreciation of lessons learnt or lessons rejected.

Even if our mothers have long since died, therapy can help nourish our inner relationship with them, making ourselves the people, or parents, we really need to be.

Robert Goodman

Love is not always a bed of roses

Try to communicate from the position of I rather than you. That might mean saying "I need to feel more heard" instead of "you never listen".

Put some time aside to discuss regular areas of difficulty when you are feeling warm and kind towards each other, rather than in the middle of it.

Robert Goodman

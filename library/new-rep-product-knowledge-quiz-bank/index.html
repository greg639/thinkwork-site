Scoring logic

``` Module weights (points available): Module 1: Core Product & Platform 20 pts Module 2: ICP & Use Cases 20 pts Module 3: Competitive Positioning 20 pts Module 4: Pricing & Packaging 20 pts Module 5: Objection-Handling Knowledge 20 pts Module 6: Technical & Integration Basics 20 pts (use your own 6 questions, same 3.33 pt weighting)

Total possible 120 pts

Final score % = (Points earned ÷ 120) × 100

Pass threshold: 80% or higher overall AND no single module scoring below 60% (prevents a strong overall score from masking a real gap in, e.g., competitive positioning or pricing authority: both are high-risk areas if a rep gets them wrong live in front of a prospect) ```

Retake policy: if overall score is below 80%, or any module is below 60%, the rep restudies the specific module(s) that failed (not the whole bank) and retakes only those modules within 5 business days. A full-bank retake is only required if the rep fails the same module twice.

SDR Outbound Ramp Skills Checklist

The exact skill milestones a new SDR should be able to demonstrate by week 4, week 8, and week 12 of outbound ramp — so ramp reviews are based on observed skill, not tenure.

What's inside

  • Week 4 milestones: Foundation (8 checkable skills)
  • Week 8 milestones: Independent Execution (7 checkable skills)
  • Week 12 milestones: Full Ramp (7 checkable skills)
  • Milestones grouped by ICP knowledge, messaging, tools, cadence, objection handling, and pipeline
  • Cross-references to the Cold Email Copy Review Scorecard as an objective gate

Use this at each ramp checkpoint as an observed-skill audit, not a self-report. Every item should be verifiable against something you watched, read, or scored — a live call, a sent sequence, a scorecard result.

By Week 4 — Foundation

  • Can explain the ICP (firmographic + persona) without notes
  • Can name the top 3 problems the product solves, in customer language, not feature language
  • Has built and sent a first sequence that passed manager review
  • Can build a prospect list in the CRM/sales engagement tool unsupervised
  • Has shadowed 5+ calls (live or recorded)
  • Has completed first 50 dials
  • Can handle the 3 most common objections using a scripted response (reading from a cheat sheet is fine at this stage)
  • Understands cadence spacing rules and doesn't need reminding

By Week 8 — Independent Execution

  • Writes first-touch emails from scratch that score 18/25+ on the Cold Email Copy Review Scorecard
  • Runs 2+ live sequences independently with only spot-check manager review
  • Personalizes outreach using account triggers (funding, hiring, news) without being prompted
  • Handles the top 6 objections live on calls without a script
  • Books meetings at 50%+ of the full-ramp target
  • Logs activity accurately without manager follow-up
  • Has generated at least one self-sourced qualified opportunity

By Week 12 — Full Ramp

  • Hits 100% of assigned activity and meeting targets independently
  • Can build a channel mix plan (email/LinkedIn/call/video) for a new segment without a template
  • Mentors a newer SDR on at least one specific skill
  • Adapts messaging per persona without needing manager review first
  • Consistently scores 20/25+ on the Cold Email Copy Review Scorecard
  • Contributes to sequence or messaging improvements based on their own results data
  • Meets or exceeds team-average self-sourced pipeline contribution

How to use a gap: an unchecked item at any checkpoint is the specific coaching focus for the next two weeks — not a flag to extend ramp generally. Name the skill, not the tenure, in the follow-up conversation.

How to use it

Review the relevant week's checklist against direct observation (live calls, sent sequences, scorecard results) at each ramp checkpoint, and treat any unchecked item as the specific coaching focus for the following two weeks rather than a general performance concern.

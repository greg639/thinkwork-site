Champion Tracker Template

A fill-in tracker for who your internal champion actually is, what they personally gain, what they need from you to sell it upstairs, and when they last went dark — because 'we have a champion' isn't verified until this template is filled in.

What's inside

  • Champion name, title, and account/deal field
  • Personal-benefit field — what the champion gains if the deal closes
  • What-they-need-from-you field — the internal pitch materials the champion requires
  • Delivered-internal-pitch tracking field with date
  • Internal approval-chain field (who they still need to convince)
  • Political-capital rating (Low/Medium/High)
  • Last two-way contact date field, defined strictly as their reply, not your outbound
  • Trend field (warming/steady/cooling)
  • Backup champion field
  • Risk-flags field
  • A fully filled-in worked example row

Use one row per champion — a deal can and often should have more than one.

Instructions

  1. One row per champion, not per deal — a deal with two genuine champions should have two rows.
  2. Update "last two-way contact" only on replies, never on your own outbound — an unanswered email is not contact.
  3. Trend is a judgment call, but justify it in one line each time you update it — "steady" without evidence is just optimism restated.
  4. No backup champion identified is itself a red-flag — cross-reference with the companion Deal Risk Red-Flag Checklist (single-threaded contact).
  5. Review every entry before a deal review — if you can't fill in "what they need to sell it upstairs," you don't yet have a real champion, you have a friendly contact.

The single most important question this template forces

"What does this person personally get if the deal closes?" If you can't answer it — money, promotion, credit, solved pain, political win — you likely have an information source, not a champion. Downgrade your confidence in the deal accordingly and go find the real one.

How to use it

Add a row for every internal advocate on an active deal, fill in what they personally gain and what they need from you to pitch it upstairs, and revisit the 'last two-way contact' and 'trend' fields weekly to catch a champion going quiet before it costs you the deal.

Selling Style / Sales Archetype Quiz

Most reps have a natural selling instinct they fall back on under pressure, it's rarely a conscious choice. This quiz uses 12 real scenario choices covering prospecting, discovery, objections and closing to identify which of the four classic selling styles is yours by default, then tells you honestly where that style wins, where it costs you deals, and how to sharpen it.

Your numbers

Method & assumptions

  • Method: 12 forced-choice scenarios across prospecting, discovery, objections and closing, each answer maps to one of four recognised selling styles; your archetype is whichever you chose most often.
  • This is a self-report snapshot of natural instinct, not a fixed label, most experienced reps blend styles and consciously flex by deal type.
  • Loosely grounded in established sales-methodology research (Challenger Sale, consultative/SPIN selling, relationship-selling and transactional-closing models), simplified into a practical self-assessment, not a validated psychometric instrument.
  • Use it alongside real deal data: compare your natural style against which deal types you actually win, not just which you enjoy most.

How to use it

Answer all 12 scenarios with whichever option is closest to your gut instinct, not what you think you're supposed to say, your result updates instantly as you answer. There's no "best" style: use the strengths, watch-outs and best-fit deal types to understand where your natural instinct already wins, and the growth list to shore up where it costs you deals. If you score a close hybrid or a balanced spread, that's a real and common result, it means you flex by situation rather than defaulting to one mode.

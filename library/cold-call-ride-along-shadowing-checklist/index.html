Cold Call Ride-Along / Call Shadowing Checklist

A before/during/after checklist for shadowing a rep's live cold calling block: what to listen for in each phase of the call, and the rules for delivering feedback without shutting the rep down mid-session.

How to use it

Print this or keep it open on a second screen while you silently listen in on a rep's full dial block, checking items off in real time rather than trying to reconstruct the call from memory afterward.

What's inside

  • Before-session checklist: consent, block length, muting, scorecard-in-hand rules
  • Phase-by-phase listening checklist for the opener, discovery, objections, and next-step securing
  • Whole-block checklist: energy drop-off after rejection, warm-up time, timestamped moments worth replaying
  • After-session feedback rules: timing, sequencing, and the one-moment discipline
  • A short Do / Don't list for shadow etiquette

Use this every time you silently join a rep's live dial block: as a manager, peer mentor, or enablement lead.

Before the session

  • Rep knows this is developmental, not a performance review, and was told in advance: never surprise a rep with a silent shadow
  • Confirmed which block you're joining (minimum 45 minutes / a full dial session, not 2 cherry-picked calls)
  • Your line is fully muted; confirmed the rep can't hear you breathing, typing, or moving
  • Call Monitoring & Coaching Scorecard is open and ready: not filled in from memory afterward
  • Decided in advance whether you're giving live micro-feedback via chat, or saving everything for after (default: after, unless both agreed this is a live-coaching session)

During: Opener phase (per call)

  • Did the opener sound conversational, or read off a script?
  • Did the rep adapt when the first 5 seconds didn't go as expected?
  • Noted the exact prospect pushback line used (for the objection-handling review later)

During: Discovery phase

  • Counted how many open-ended questions were asked before any pitch
  • Did the rep let a silence sit, or fill it immediately?
  • Did the rep reference something the prospect said earlier in the same call?

During, Objection handling

  • Did the rep acknowledge before responding?
  • Did tone shift (defensive, rushed) after pushback?
  • Was the objection actually addressed, or talked past?

During: Next steps

  • Was a specific day/time asked for, or left vague?
  • Did the rep confirm the meeting before hanging up?

During, Across the whole block

  • Did energy or quality drop after a rejection or two in a row?
  • How many calls before the rep "warmed up"?
  • Any moment worth replaying in a coaching session: timestamp it now, not from memory later

After the session: feedback delivery

  • Feedback happens within 24 hours: never immediately after a bad call, in the middle of a live block
  • Lead with one specific thing that worked, named precisely (not "good job")
  • Pick ONE moment to work on and carry it into the 1:1 Cold Call Coaching Session Template: don't dump the whole list
  • Ask the rep what they noticed before giving your own read
  • End with a forward commitment, not just a critique

Do / Don't

Do

  • Take timestamped notes so you can return to the exact moment later.
  • Shadow a full block, not a highlight call, average calls tell you more than best calls.

Don't

  • React audibly, laugh, or sigh: even muted, reps sense a shadow's energy through their own nerves.
  • Shadow the same rep the day right after a hard coaching conversation, let it land first.

Sales Content Calendar Template

A repeatable quarterly cadence for auditing, refreshing, and retiring battlecards and playbooks — before reps quietly stop trusting them and start building their own shadow versions.

What's inside

  • 12-week quarterly cadence table (audit, field input, scoring, refresh sprint, retire, launch, adoption check, retro)
  • Asset tracker table template with owner, last-updated, next-review, status, usage, and win-rate columns
  • Status definitions (Active, Needs Refresh, Retire, Promote)
  • Roles and responsibilities table (Enablement lead, content owner, sales manager, RevOps)
  • A concrete retirement rule of thumb

A quarterly cadence that keeps battlecards, playbooks, and scripts trustworthy — so reps stop quietly building their own shadow versions because "the official one is out of date."

The Quarterly Cadence (repeat every quarter)

WeekPhaseWhat happens
1Audit kickoffPull usage analytics (opens, shares, last-used date) for every asset in the library. Flag anything untouched in 90+ days.
2Field inputSurvey/interview 5-8 reps and 2-3 managers: "What are you actually using? What do you build yourself because the official version doesn't work?"
3Score & triageRun every asset through the Sales Content Audit Checklist. Sort into Keep / Refresh / Retire / Promote.
4Prioritize refresh listRank Refresh-list items by deal volume and win-rate association — refresh what's used most first, not what's easiest.
5-8Refresh sprintUpdate pricing, logos, competitive positioning, screenshots. Assign a named owner per asset — no asset ships without one.
9Retire passArchive (don't just hide) retired assets. Redirect any links/searches to the replacement. Announce retirements in the same place you announce new content.
10Launch/refresh announcementShip refreshed + new assets together, with a 2-minute "what changed and why" — not a silent file swap.
11Adoption checkSpot-check 3-5 reps: can they find the new version? Do they know what changed?
12Retro + plan next quarterWhat got used, what didn't, what's coming next quarter (new competitor, new pricing, new segment) that content needs to get ahead of.

Status definitions:

  • Active — current, owned, used in the last 90 days
  • Needs Refresh — factually stale (pricing, logos, competitive info) but structurally still useful
  • Retire — superseded, unused, or actively causing reps to say wrong things
  • Promote — under-used but high win-rate association; needs a visibility push, not a rewrite

Roles & Responsibilities

RoleResponsibility
Enablement leadOwns the calendar, runs the audit, chases refresh owners
Content owner (per asset)Named individual accountable for accuracy each cycle
Sales managerSurfaces field feedback in Week 2, verifies adoption in Week 11
RevOpsProvides usage analytics for Week 1

Retirement Rule of Thumb

If an asset has under 5% usage across the sales team in a 90-day window and no manager will vouch for it in the Week 2 field input, retire it — don't refresh content nobody was using in the first place.

How to use it

Copy the 12-week cadence onto your actual quarterly calendar with real dates and owners, and keep the Asset Tracker table as a living document that gets updated at every Week-1 audit, not rebuilt from scratch each quarter.

Sales-to-CS Handoff Checklist

The information that must transfer from AE to AM/CSM at close, organized into eight categories, so the first 90 days don't start with a scramble.

How to use it

AE completes every section at or immediately after signature and walks the CSM through it live in a 20-minute handoff call before kickoff: treat any blank field as a risk to flag, not a box to skip.

What's inside

  • Deal and account economics transfer fields (ARR, terms, discounting rationale)
  • Full stakeholder map with buyer/champion/blocker roles
  • Champion identity and internal political context
  • Technical and implementation requirements with integration priorities
  • Verbatim log of every promise made during the sales cycle
  • Risk-flag section covering urgency drivers, budget stability, and near-miss objections
  • Customer's own success criteria and renewal-judging metrics
  • Kickoff logistics and access-provisioning checklist
  • Dual sign-off section for AE and CSM

Use this at signature, before the ink is dry. Every unchecked box is a landmine that goes off in week 3 of onboarding.

1. Deal & Account Economics

  • Final ARR/MRR and contract term (start date, end date, auto-renew terms)
  • Billing frequency and payment terms (net 30/60, PO required, invoicing contact)
  • Any non-standard discounting and the business reason for it
  • Multi-year ramp schedule, if applicable
  • Upsell/expansion products included, excluded, or promised "later"
  • Contract redlines or non-standard legal terms Legal should flag to CS (custom SLAs, data residency, indemnity carve-outs)

2. Stakeholder Map

  • Full list of everyone in the buying process: name, title, role (economic buyer, champion, user, blocker, influencer)
  • Who signed vs. who will actually use the product day-to-day (often different people)
  • Org chart or reporting lines between the above, if known
  • Anyone who was NOT won over: a skeptic, a blocker, someone who preferred a competitor
  • Stakeholders likely to leave/move roles in the next 6 months (flight risk = knowledge/relationship risk)

3. Champion & Political Context

  • Who is the internal champion: name, title, why they championed this deal
  • What the champion personally needs this deal to deliver (their internal win, not just the company's)
  • Champion's relationship with the economic buyer
  • Any internal politics CS should know about (replaced a competitor, prior failed vendor, contested budget)

4. Technical & Implementation Requirements

  • Required integrations and priority order (must-have for go-live vs. nice-to-have)
  • Data migration scope: what's coming from where, who owns extracting it
  • Security/compliance requirements surfaced during the sale (SSO, SOC 2 ask, DPA)
  • Environment/instance requirements (sandbox, specific role/permission structure)
  • Any pilot/POC results: what was tested, what passed, what was a caveat

5. Promises & Commitments Made During the Sale

  • Every feature, timeline, or capability promised verbally or in the deck that isn't in the standard contract: verbatim, not paraphrased
  • Roadmap items the customer was told are "coming soon," and the real target date (or "no committed date")
  • Specific individuals promised specific service levels (dedicated CSM, quarterly business reviews)
  • Pricing or renewal commitments made for future terms

6. Risk Flags

  • Why did they buy NOW: the trigger event, and does that urgency evaporate post-signature?
  • What alternative were they seriously considering, and why did we win
  • Any hesitation or "soft no" overcome late in the cycle: what was it, how was it resolved
  • Budget source and stability across the full term
  • Anything unusual about the buying process suggesting organizational dysfunction (rushed, under-scoped, missing stakeholder buy-in)

7. Success Criteria & Definition of Value

  • The customer's own words for what "this worked" looks like
  • Specific metrics they will use to judge renewal
  • Timeline they expect to see value by
  • Who internally will be asked "was this worth it" at renewal, and what they care about

8. Kickoff Logistics

  • Kickoff call scheduled with correct stakeholders invited (not just the champion)
  • Access provisioning status: logins, SSO config, admin rights assigned
  • Onboarding/implementation timeline shared with the customer before or during kickoff
  • Internal AE-to-CSM handoff call completed live (minimum 20 minutes); AE attends kickoff call #1

Sign-off

  • AE confirms this document is complete and accurate
  • CSM/AM confirms receipt and has scheduled kickoff within [X] business days of close
  • Both parties agree: no field above is blank without an explicit "N/A, confirmed not applicable"

Why the firm exists

Enablement sold methodology. Nobody measured the layer underneath it.

In every seat I held, the same pattern. Vendors sold methodologies. Managers coached opinions. Platforms scored deals. And the layer that actually decided whether a rep ramped went unobserved. People were diagnosed in language nobody could verify, then coached on plays they could not yet execute.

So I wrote the rubric I wanted: 54 named sales competencies across 27 role and motion specific frameworks, each with observable behaviour written out at four levels. Not a philosophy. A marking scheme you can hand to two managers and check whether they land in the same place.

Then AI started marking sales calls at scale, and almost nobody was testing whether the scores agreed with anything. Ramp plans, coaching time and quota attainment all get planned off scores like these. The error in the scores is already inside your number, whether or not you have measured it.

That is the whole job now: make sales skill measurable, then keep the measurement honest. ThinkWork installs it. PeerLab measures it. I run both.

Why this standard and not the tool you already own.

Prices

Always know what you will pay.

Packages

From £30 per person

Basic Training Package

£30per person

Camo overalls, facemask, a paintball marker, unlimited gas and 300 paintballs. Half day or full day.

Premium: the £10 upgrade

+£10per person

Not a separate ticket. Book the £30 package and add the £10 upgrade on top, which takes you from 300 to 500 paintballs and £40 a head.

Extra paintballs

£9per 100

Per 100, any time during the day. A bag of 500 is £30, or £25 if you pre-order it when you book.

Booking the upgrade for a group

The £10 upgrade is per person, not per booking. If ten of you are playing and you all want 500 paintballs, that is ten £30 places and ten £10 upgrades. One upgrade on a group booking only upgrades one player.

Buying paint in bulk

  • £9 per 100 paintballs
  • £30 per bag of 500 bought on the day
  • £25 per bag of 500 pre-ordered at the point of booking
  • Groups of 20+: 10% off  ·  30+: 15% off

Your £30 buys the morning session. Play on into the afternoon at no extra charge, with paint charged separately. We close around 15:30.

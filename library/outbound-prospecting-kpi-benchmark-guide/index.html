Outbound Prospecting KPI Benchmark Guide

Industry-standard connect rates, meeting-set rates, and cost-per-meeting figures broken out by channel and company segment, with the formulas to calculate your own numbers and a diagnostic table for when you fall short.

How to use it

Compare your team's actual connect, meeting-set, and cost-per-meeting numbers against the segment-matched ranges, then use the diagnostic table to pinpoint the root cause behind any metric that's falling short.

What's inside

  • Connect-rate benchmarks by channel (call, email, LinkedIn, video)
  • Meeting-set-rate benchmarks by segment (SMB/Mid-market/Enterprise)
  • Show-rate and opportunity-conversion benchmarks
  • Cost-per-meeting calculation and benchmark ranges by segment
  • Formulas to calculate your own numbers from raw activity data
  • Guidance on using benchmarks to set realistic team targets
  • Diagnostic table: below-benchmark symptom to likely root cause
  • Recommended benchmark review cadence

Use these as starting reference ranges, not absolutes, recalibrate against your own first full quarter of data as soon as you have it.

Connect rate by channel

ChannelTypical rangeNotes
Cold call8–15%Lower for enterprise (gatekeepers), higher for SMB direct-dial
Cold email (reply rate)15–25% open, 1–3% replyReply rate is the meaningful number, not open rate
LinkedIn message (1st-degree/connection accepted)20–35% responseHighest engagement per touch, lowest volume ceiling
Video message25–40% view rate, 8–15% replyStrong for standing out, expensive in rep time

Meeting-set rate by segment (meetings booked ÷ conversations)

SegmentTypical range
SMB (<100 employees)20–30%
Mid-market (100–1,000 employees)12–20%
Enterprise (1,000+ employees)6–12%

Enterprise numbers are lower because multi-thread, multi-touch cycles are the norm: a single conversation converting to a meeting is the exception, not the rule (see the Account-Based Prospecting Playbook).

Show rate & opportunity conversion

MetricTypical range
Show rate (meetings held ÷ booked)70–85%
Opportunity conversion (opps created ÷ meetings held)30–50%

Cost per meeting

Formula: (Fully-loaded SDR cost per month ÷ meetings booked per month) = cost per meeting

SegmentTypical cost-per-meeting range
SMB$50–$150
Mid-market$150–$400
Enterprise$400–$1,200+

Enterprise cost-per-meeting is higher because of the multi-touch, multi-thread effort per account: evaluate against average deal size, not against SMB cost-per-meeting.

Calculate your own numbers

MetricFormula
Connect rateConnects ÷ Dials
Meeting-set rateMeetings Booked ÷ Conversations
Show rateMeetings Held ÷ Meetings Booked
Opportunity conversionOpportunities Created ÷ Meetings Held
Cost per meetingFully-loaded rep cost (monthly) ÷ Meetings Booked (monthly)

Setting realistic targets

  1. Segment your targets by SMB/Mid-market/Enterprise, a single blended target across segments will always look wrong for someone.
  2. Set targets at the midpoint of the range in year one; move to the top of the range only after a full quarter of clean baseline data.
  3. Never set a target on Show Rate or Opportunity Conversion alone without also tracking Meetings Booked volume, a rep can inflate one metric by starving the other.

Diagnostic table: below benchmark, likely cause

SymptomMost likely root causeWhere to look
Connect rate below rangeBad list/data quality, or dialing wrong hoursData source audit; time-of-day analysis
Meeting-set rate below range, connect rate fineWeak discovery/ask: conversations happening, not convertingCall review against Cold Call Certification Scorecard categories 4–7
Show rate below rangePoor meeting confirmation process, meetings booked too far outAdd confirmation touch 24h before; tighten booking-to-meeting gap
Opportunity conversion below rangeMeetings are happening but with wrong persona / no real painBuying Committee Map: check role fit before booking
Cost per meeting rising quarter over quarterRep tenure/ramp issue, or account list quality degradingSegment cost-per-meeting by rep tenure and by account tier

Review cadence

Recalibrate these benchmarks against your own data once per quarter minimum. Ranges above assume a mature outbound motion with clean CRM data, a brand-new SDR function should expect the bottom of every range for its first two quarters.

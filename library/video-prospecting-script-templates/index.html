Video Prospecting Script Templates

Five word-for-word 30-second video scripts for LinkedIn and email outreach, built on a Hook-Relevance-Value-CTA structure, plus delivery tips for reps who dread being on camera.

How to use it

Pick the template that matches your trigger (event, referral, content, competitor, or none), fill the brackets, record in one take using the delivery tips, and send with the paired follow-up message the same day.

What's inside

  • The 30-second Hook-Relevance-Value-CTA framework with exact timing
  • Trigger-event script template
  • Mutual-connection/referral script template
  • Content-engagement script template
  • Competitor-switch script template
  • No-trigger / role-based relevance script template
  • Camera-shy delivery tips
  • Paired follow-up message templates (LinkedIn + email)

The structure: 30 seconds, every time:

SegmentTimeJob
Hook0–5sSay their name, say why this video exists: no "hi, hope you're well"
Relevance5–12sThe specific reason this is for them (trigger, mutual connection, content, competitor)
Value12–22sOne sentence, one outcome: never a feature list
CTA22–30sOne specific, low-friction ask

Record 2–3 takes max. The first take is usually the most natural: resist over-scripting your delivery, just hit the beats.

Template 1: Trigger event (funding, expansion, leadership change)

"Hi [Name], [Rep] here at [Company]. Saw [Company/their company] just [trigger event, e.g. 'closed your Series B' / 'opened the Austin office'], congrats. Usually when that happens, [specific operational pressure that follows the trigger] becomes the next problem. We help teams like [peer company] handle exactly that. If it's relevant, worth 15 minutes, I'll drop a link below."

Template 2, Mutual connection / referral

"Hi [Name], [Rep] from [Company]. [Mutual connection] mentioned you're leading [function] at [Company] and thought we should connect. We've helped a few people in [mutual connection]'s network with [specific outcome], wanted to see if that's on your radar too. If so, happy to grab 15 minutes."

Template 3, Content engagement (they posted/commented on something relevant)

"Hi [Name], [Rep] here. Saw your post about [specific topic they posted on] and it's exactly the problem we spend our day on. Wanted to share how [similar company] approached the same thing: not a pitch, just thought it might be useful given what you wrote. Open to a quick look?"

Template 4, Competitor switch (they use a competitor's tool)

"Hi [Name], [Rep] from [Company]. Noticed [Company] uses [competitor] for [function]: no complaints assumed, just curious how it's going. A few teams that made the switch to us saw [specific outcome, e.g. 'a third fewer manual steps']. If [competitor] is working perfectly, ignore this: if there's friction, worth a look."

Template 5, No trigger / pure role relevance

"Hi [Name], [Rep] at [Company]. No specific reason for reaching out other than: you run [function] at a company that looks like our best customers. Most [role]s we talk to are dealing with [named problem]. If that's true for you too, happy to show you how [peer company] solved it: 15 minutes, your call."

Delivery tips for camera-shy reps

  • Prop the script, don't memorize it. Print it in 24pt font, tape it just behind the camera lens so your eyeline stays close to natural.
  • Screen-share instead of face-to-camera for the "relevance" section, pull up their LinkedIn or company site and talk over it; your face isn't required for the whole 30 seconds.
  • Natural beats polished. A small stumble reads as human, not unprofessional: don't re-record more than 3 times, diminishing returns kick in fast.
  • Sit, don't stand, and keep the camera at eye level, phone propped against a book works fine.
  • Smile before you hit record, not during, it changes your baseline tone for the whole take.
  • First and last 3 seconds matter most, nail the hook and the CTA; the middle can be looser.

Paired follow-up message (send alongside or right after the video)

LinkedIn:

"Sent you a quick video above ↑, 30 seconds on [specific reason]. No worries if it's not relevant, just let me know either way."

Email:

Subject: quick video for [Company] [Name], recorded a 30-second video for you: [link]. Covers [one-line reason]. Take a look when you get a sec, happy to skip straight to a call if that's easier: [scheduling link].

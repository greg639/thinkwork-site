Top 50 Objection Rebuttals by Industry (SaaS, Insurance, Real Estate, Finance)

A 50-rebuttal swipe file organized into SaaS, Insurance, Real Estate, and Finance, because "too expensive" needs a completely different answer depending on what's actually being sold.

How to use it

Find your industry section, match the objection to the closest of the 12 categories, and use the rebuttal as a word-for-word starting point: swap in your actual product, policy, listing, or plan specifics.

What's inside

  • 12 SaaS-specific word-for-word rebuttals
  • 12 Insurance-specific word-for-word rebuttals
  • 12 Real Estate-specific word-for-word rebuttals
  • 12 Finance/Advisory-specific word-for-word rebuttals
  • 2 universal catch-all closers usable in any industry
  • A consistent 12-objection taxonomy used across all four industries for fast cross-reference

Same 12 objection types show up in every industry, the words a prospect uses to defuse them do not transfer. This swipe file gives you the industry-correct version of each.

SaaS (1–12)

  1. "Too expensive / not in budget": "Totally fair, most of our customers said the same before they ran the numbers. What's it costing you monthly to keep [manual process/current tool] running? Our average customer saves [X hours/$Y] in the first quarter, usually more than the subscription itself."
  2. "Need to think about it": "Makes sense: what specifically do you need to think through: the price, the fit with your stack, or getting internal buy-in? Let's tackle that one now."
  3. "Send me some info": "Happy to: quick question so I send the right thing: is it the ROI case, the security docs, or a comparison to [Competitor] you need?"
  4. "We already use [Competitor]": "Good, that tells me you're sold on the category. Teams that switch usually do it because of [specific gap, e.g. native integrations]. Want to see that side-by-side?"
  5. "Need to check with IT/security": "Smart move, let's get ahead of it. I'll send our SOC 2 report and security questionnaire now so IT has zero surprises."
  6. "Not a good time": "Understood: is that a resourcing thing, or just not a priority this quarter? If it's timing, let's pencil in the right month now."
  7. "We tried a tool like this before and it flopped": "What happened: adoption, or the tool just didn't do what you needed? That tells me exactly what to prove before you commit again."
  8. "Not sure this works for a team like ours": "Fair, here's a customer in your exact vertical who had the same doubt. Want a call with them directly, no rep involved?"
  9. "Your price is higher than [Competitor]": "It can be, the difference is [e.g. we include implementation and support, they charge extra for both]. Priced in, what's the real gap?"
  10. "We're fine with our current spreadsheet/process": "'Fine' is doing a lot of work there. What would it take for 'fine' to become 'actually good'?"
  11. "I'm not the one who decides this": "Got it, who else needs to be in the room? I'd rather loop them in now than have you relay this secondhand."
  12. "Circle back next quarter": "I'll hold you to that, what changes between now and then? If nothing does, let's start now."

Insurance (13–24)

  1. "Premium's too high": "I hear that a lot, what are you comparing it to? A cheaper quote usually means less coverage, and I'd rather show you the gap now than have you find it at claim time."
  2. "Need to think about it": "Of course: is it the premium, the coverage limits, or wanting a second quote? Let's go through whichever it is now."
  3. "Send me the quote/policy details": "Will do: is price, coverage, or the carrier's claims track record the main thing you're evaluating?"
  4. "We already have coverage with [Carrier]": "Good, so you already value being covered. When did you last actually compare rates or coverage? People are often surprised what's changed in a couple of years."
  5. "Need to check with my spouse/partner": "Makes sense, big decision. Want me to walk through it with both of you together?"
  6. "Not a good time: busy season": "Understood, but claims don't wait for your calendar. Can we lock in coverage now and revisit the finer details when it's calmer?"
  7. "Bad experience with a claim before": "That's exactly why I want to walk you through what's covered, line by line, so there's no surprise if you ever need to file."
  8. "Not sure I need this much coverage": "Let's look at what you'd actually be on the hook for without it. Underinsuring usually costs more than the premium difference."
  9. "Your premium is higher than [Competitor]": "Possibly, what's their deductible and coverage limit? Cheaper premiums often mean exclusions that show up exactly when you need the policy."
  10. "We're fine with our current policy": "When was it last reviewed against your current situation? Policies that fit two years ago often have gaps now."
  11. "I'm not the one who handles this": "No problem, who manages the policy decisions? I'd like to walk them through the gap directly."
  12. "Call me back at renewal time": "I'll note that: but if rates are moving, waiting until renewal can mean fewer options. Want a placeholder quote locked in now?"

Real Estate (25–36)

  1. "Too expensive/out of range": "Got it: is it the list price itself, or the monthly payment? Sometimes we can restructure the offer to hit the number that matters."
  2. "Need to think about it": "Fair on a decision this size: price, location, or timing of the move?"
  3. "Send me the listing details": "Sure: are you further along on financing, or still deciding on the area?"
  4. "Already working with another agent": "Sounds like you're serious about this. What's been missing so far? I don't want to just repeat what you've already heard."
  5. "Need to check with my spouse/family": "Of course, want to loop them into the next showing so we're not relaying details back and forth?"
  6. "Not the right time to buy/sell": "What would make it the right time: rates, a life event, or wanting to see more inventory first?"
  7. "Tried before and it fell through": "What happened: financing, inspection, or the wrong property? That tells me exactly what to watch for this time."
  8. "Not sure this is the right neighborhood/property": "What's the actual dealbreaker: schools, commute, size? Let's separate must-haves from nice-to-haves."
  9. "Your commission is higher than [discount broker]": "It can look that way upfront, the difference shows up in negotiation and days-on-market. What's that worth on a home this size?"
  10. "We're fine renting / staying put": "What would have to change for moving to be worth it? The rent-vs-own numbers sometimes surprise people once we run them."
  11. "I'm not the decision-maker": "Got it, who else is involved? I'd rather walk them through it directly than have it get lost in translation."
  12. "Revisit next season/year": "I'll hold that, but inventory and rates shift a lot in that window. Want me to flag you the moment something matches?"

Finance / Financial Advisory (37–48)

  1. "Your fees are too high": "Fair question, what are you comparing the fee to? A lot of people don't realize what they're already paying in hidden fund fees. Let's put both side by side."
  2. "Need to think about it": "Makes sense for something this important: the fee structure, the strategy, or wanting a second opinion?"
  3. "Send me a proposal/plan": "Happy to: retirement timeline, risk tolerance, or fee structure: which matters most to you?"
  4. "I already have an advisor/broker": "Good, you're already thinking about this seriously. When's the last time you got a second opinion on performance and fees?"
  5. "Need to check with my spouse/partner": "Absolutely, want to bring them into the next conversation so we build the plan together?"
  6. "Not the right time: market's uncertain": "That's actually when a plan matters most: what feels uncertain: the market, your job situation, or the strategy?"
  7. "Tried an advisor before, didn't go well": "What happened: performance, communication, or feeling like you weren't a priority? That tells me exactly what to do differently."
  8. "Not sure I have enough assets for this": "Let's find out, a lot of people underestimate what a plan does at their level. Worth 20 minutes before ruling it out?"
  9. "Your fees are higher than [robo-advisor]": "They can be: what are you getting for that lower fee: less personalization, no human review during a downturn? Let's weigh that."
  10. "We're fine managing this ourselves": "Fair: how much time is that costing you, and how confident are you it's optimized? A second set of eyes is the value, not a takeover."
  11. "I'm not the one who handles our finances": "Got it, who does? I'd like them in the conversation so the plan reflects both your goals."
  12. "Let's talk after [event/next year]": "I'll hold that date, but want a light-touch check-in before then so we're not starting from scratch?"

Universal Closers (49–50)

  1. "I just don't think this is for us" (final catch-all): "Understood: before I let this go, can I ask what specifically doesn't fit? I'd rather hear the real reason than guess."
  2. Silence after a strong pitch (non-verbal objection): "I want to make sure I'm solving the right problem, what's the one thing standing between you and moving forward?"

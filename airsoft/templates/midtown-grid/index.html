Straight answers

Questions people type into a search box

Where is Oakridge Airsoft?
Oak Ridge Wood, Forest Lane, Ashdown, East Sussex, AB1 2CD. Phone 01234 567890. Email hello@example.com.
When is Oakridge Airsoft open?
7 days, 10am to 10pm, dependent on bookings. Please call before visiting, as the arena only opens when events are booked.
How big is the Oakridge Airsoft arena?
The main airsoft arena is built over 40 acres, housing a woodland village with a fort, with blind corners, trenches, a village and a fort, props including vehicles, and sky bridges.
How much does a session at Oakridge Airsoft cost?
Session prices show inside the booking window once you press the booking button.
How much is membership at Oakridge Airsoft?
Memberships are £40 for 12 months, with a member rate on every game day, a club patch and member-only game nights and a free birthday game. Join on site or online. Joining online is proposed.
What are the FPS limits at Oakridge Airsoft?
0.20g at 350 fps, 0.25g at 313 fps and 0.28g at 296 fps. Only those three BB weights are allowed.
Do under-18s need full face protection at Oakridge Airsoft?
Yes. Anyone under 18 must wear full face protection. Players 18 and over must wear approved eye protection. Minors aged 12 to 17 may be dropped off by a parent or guardian and left to play, with a contact phone number.
Can I rent a gun at Oakridge Airsoft?
Yes. If you do not have your own gear and require an airsoft rental, choose a rental when you book. Pre-booking the rental online so it is on the rack when you arrive is proposed.
Do you do HPA refills at Oakridge Airsoft?
Yes. Refills cost £1 per fill, and are free with a site membership.
Does Oakridge Airsoft do birthday parties?
Yes. Oakridge Airsoft hosts parties and business events, and describes itself as players from 11, families welcome. A quote on screen with a deposit button is proposed.

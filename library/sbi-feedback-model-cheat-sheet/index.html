SBI Feedback Model Cheat Sheet

Situation-Behavior-Impact scripting cards that turn vague 'be more confident' feedback into specific, actionable coaching language.

What's inside

  • The S-B-I model with a sentence stem for each step
  • Rule for what counts as an observable Behavior vs. a judgment
  • 4 before/after examples turning vague feedback into SBI form
  • Scripting card: weak discovery questions
  • Scripting card: no next step secured
  • Scripting card: talking over the prospect
  • Scripting card: rushing past an objection
  • Word-for-word delivery opener to use live

Situation → Behavior → Impact. The fastest way to turn "be more confident" into feedback a rep can actually act on.

The model

StepWhat it isSentence stem
SituationThe specific moment — when and where this happened"In the call with [account] on [date/moment in the call]..."
BehaviorThe observable action — what they actually said or did, no interpretation"...when you [specific, observable action]..."
ImpactWhat resulted — on the prospect, the deal, or the room"...the result was [specific consequence]."

The rule: Behavior must be something a video camera could capture. "You seemed unprepared" is not a behavior — it's a judgment. "You paused for 8 seconds before answering their pricing question and then said 'I'd have to check'" is a behavior.

Before / after: turning vague feedback into SBI

Vague feedbackSBI version
"Be more confident on calls.""In yesterday's call with Acme, when they pushed back on price, you said 'I guess we could maybe look at a discount' — the impact was they went quiet and didn't push further, which usually means they lost confidence in the number."
"You talk too much.""In the discovery call with Beta Corp, you spoke for the first 6 minutes straight before asking a question — the impact was we didn't learn what their actual trigger was until minute 20, which cost us time to build a real business case."
"Good energy, needs more structure.""On the call with Delta Inc, you opened with rapport and energy, but by minute 10 there was no agenda stated — the impact was the prospect drove the conversation, and we ended without a clear next step."
"Great call!" (make positive feedback specific too)"When the prospect said 'we're not actively looking,' you didn't argue — you asked 'what would have to change for that to be worth revisiting,' and the impact was they opened up about a Q3 budget review we didn't know existed."

Scripting cards for common coaching moments

Weak discovery questions "In the call with [account], when they mentioned [pain], you moved straight to your pitch instead of asking a follow-up — the impact was we pitched a solution without knowing if it was even their top priority."

No next step secured "At the end of the call with [account], you said 'I'll follow up,' but didn't propose a specific day or time — the impact was the deal has no forward motion and no urgency for them to respond."

Talking over the prospect / cutting them off "Around minute 14, [prospect] started answering your question and you jumped in with a follow-up before they finished — the impact was we lost the second half of what might have been their real objection."

Rushing past an objection "When they said 'we already have a tool for this,' you moved on within 5 seconds without asking what wasn't working about it — the impact was we don't actually know if this is a real blocker or a soft deflection."

How to deliver it (word for word opener)

"Can I share something specific I noticed on that call? [wait for yes] In the moment when [Situation], you [Behavior] — and what I noticed happen next was [Impact]. What was going through your mind there?"

Always end with a question, not a verdict. SBI describes what happened; it doesn't diagnose why. Let the rep explain the "why" — that's where real coaching starts.

How to use it

Before your next call review or coaching conversation, write your feedback out in the S / B / I columns first — if you can't fill in an observable Behavior, you're not ready to give the feedback yet.

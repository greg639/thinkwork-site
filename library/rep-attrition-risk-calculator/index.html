Rep Attrition Risk Calculator

A weighted scoring model that combines tenure-curve risk, ramp trajectory versus plan, and manager-observed mindset signals into a single 0-100 flight-risk score per rep, plus the one action to take based on what's actually driving that score.

How to use it

Score every rep on your roster monthly using the three components, rank by total score, and for anyone in the High or Severe band, identify the largest-contributing component and run the matched one action this week.

What's inside

  • Tenure risk table modeling the sales attrition "bathtub curve" (0-20 pts)
  • Ramp Trajectory Index formula and scoring band (0-30 pts)
  • 5-signal Mindset Scorecard for managers to rate directly from 1:1 observation (0-50 pts)
  • Combined 0-100 Flight-Risk formula
  • 4-band risk interpretation: Low / Moderate / High / Severe
  • Per-rep scoring worksheet
  • Team roll-up table for stack-ranking risk across a full roster
  • Driver-identification logic matched to a one-action table
  • Monthly refresh rule plus off-cycle re-score triggers

Most attrition surprises managers because they're watching one signal (a bad month, a resignation email) instead of the three that actually compound: where a rep sits on the tenure curve, whether their ramp trajectory has quietly stalled, and what their day-to-day mindset signals have been saying for weeks before they ever update LinkedIn.

Run this per rep, monthly, or the moment any single input changes sharply.

Component A: Tenure Risk (0–20 points)

Sales attrition isn't linear: it follows a "bathtub" shape: risk is highest in the first "reality gap" window and again around the 18–24 month "restlessness" window, with calmer stretches in between.

Component B, Ramp Trajectory (0–30 points)

Ramp Index = (Actual attainment ÷ Expected attainment for their tenure month) × 100

Use your org's own ramp plan for "expected attainment." If you don't have one, use straight-line to-quota by month 6 as the default expectation.

Component C: Mindset Scorecard (0–50 points)

Rate each signal from direct observation in 1:1s, pipeline reviews, and team interactions over the last 30 days, not from a single data point.

Signal0 = No concern3 = Mild6 = Moderate10 = Severe/consistent

Team roll-up

Sort your full roster by Total, highest first. Anyone in High or Severe gets a named action and a named date this week, not a general note to "keep an eye on it."

Find the driver, then take one action

Identify which of the three components contributed the largest share of the rep's score. That's the driver: start there, not with all three.

DriverOne action
Tenure-driven (score mostly from Component A)Name the tenure window out loud in your next 1:1: "Reps at your stage often hit a reality-gap/restlessness point, how's that landing for you?" Normalizing it defuses it.
Ramp-driven (score mostly from Component B)Run a specific ramp diagnostic this week: pull their pipeline and call activity and find the one stage (prospecting volume, discovery-to-demo conversion, close rate) that's actually behind, don't coach generically on "activity."
Mindset-driven (score mostly from Component C, or any single signal at 10)Address the single highest-scoring signal directly and specifically in your next 1:1, e.g. if Peer Connection scored 10, don't open with "how's it going," open with "I've noticed you've been quieter in team huddles lately, what's going on?"

Refresh cadence

Re-score every rep monthly as a baseline. Re-score immediately, off-cycle, for any rep who: misses a forecasted deal by a wide margin, has a manager-relationship rupture, or gets flagged with the ramp-decline trend above.

How much does a game day with AberAirsoft cost?
Walk-on with your own kit is £25. The rental package is £35 and the walk-on is included. Proposed: pay the whole ticket online, no service fee, no cash at the gate.
How old do you have to be?
The minimum age to play airsoft with us is 10.
When is the next game day?
Sunday 27 September 2026 at Ammanford Airsoft and Sunday 4 October 2026 at The Garrison, Aberystwyth, both 09:00 to 16:00. Gates open 9am, games start at 10.30am, the day ends 4pm.
What is in the rental package?
One airsoft gun, one magazine full of BBs and one full face mask, with the walk-on price included. Extra ammo is sold on site in bags of 3,000 rounds for around £15.
How do I pay?
Today: a deposit online with a ticket service fee on top, £5 for a walk-on or £15 for a rental package, and the remaining £20 in cash on the day. Cash only for the remaining payment on game days. Proposed: pay by card online when you book, in full, and your place and your rental are sorted before the day.
Where is The Garrison?
Frongoch Farm, Aberystwyth, Ceredigion, SY23 3DG.
Which other grounds have game days?
Ammanford Airsoft at Hen Fynydd, Cwm Felin Rd, Ammanford, SA18 2SN, and Delta 4 at Llandewi Ystradenny, Powys, LD1 6SF.
What are the site limits?
AEG 350fps, 1.14J. DMR, locked semi, 400fps, 1.48J. Bolt action sniper 2.32J.
Do I need to fill in a waiver?
Yes. All players complete the yearly insurance waiver form once a year. Proposed: sign it once at the checkout and it is held on your account for twelve months.
Can I get a refund?
All game bookings are non-refundable and non-transferable to other players or to the next game day. If a game is cancelled you get a full refund, or the deposit is kept for a future event.
Is the shop open?
The physical shop is no longer open. Orders are still taken on game days and by message on Facebook, with delivery or collection on a game day. Card payments are taken for shop items.
Do you do kit for private parties?
Yes, rental kits can be supplied for private parties and events at our grounds; use the contact form to ask. A quote on screen with a deposit button is proposed.

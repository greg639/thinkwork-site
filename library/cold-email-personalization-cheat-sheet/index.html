The 90-second research routine (run before every cold send)

  1. 20 sec — LinkedIn profile: current role, tenure, recent activity.
  2. 20 sec — Company LinkedIn page: recent posts, headcount trend.
  3. 20 sec — Google News: "[Company name]" for the last 30 days.
  4. 20 sec — Company careers page: open roles signal priorities.
  5. 10 sec — Pick the single most specific detail. Not the most impressive one — the most specific one. Specificity, not impressiveness, is what proves you did the work.

The rule that keeps personalization from becoming filler

Only use a data point in the opening line if it changes what you say next. If the personalized detail and the rest of the email would read identically without it, it's decoration, not personalization — and prospects can tell the difference.

How to use it

Before writing any cold email, run the 90-second routine, pick whichever data point from the table is most specific for that prospect, and build the opening line around it before writing the rest of the email.

Sales Team Objection Handling Readiness Diagnostic

A 24-question, 5-dimension maturity assessment that scores your team's objection-handling readiness and places you on a 5-stage maturity model with stage-specific next actions.

How to use it

Score all 24 questions honestly as a team rather than just from leadership's view, sum the total, find your maturity stage, and use the matching action list as your next 90-day enablement priority.

What's inside

  • 5 diagnostic dimensions: Process, Skill/Certification, Content/Tools, Data/Measurement, Coaching
  • 24 scored questions on a 0–4 scale
  • Full scoring key and score bands
  • 5-stage maturity model (Ad Hoc through Optimized)
  • Directional benchmark ranges per stage
  • Stage-by-stage action recommendations

Score each question 0–4, sum across all 24, and find your maturity stage.

Scoring key: 0 = No/not at all · 1 = Informal/ad hoc · 2 = Documented but inconsistently used · 3 = Consistently used · 4 = Embedded, measured, and improved over time.

Maturity Stages

ScoreStageDescription
0–191: Ad HocReactive, entirely rep-dependent, no shared standard
20–382: DevelopingSome scripts/training exist but used inconsistently
39–573: DefinedDocumented taxonomy, framework, and onboarding process in place
58–774: ManagedMeasured with data, certification, and regular coaching
78–965: OptimizedSystematic, revenue-linked, continuously improved

Directional benchmarks: most B2B sales orgs land in Stage 2–3 (roughly 25–50). Orgs with dedicated enablement investment tend to reach Stage 4 (58+). Stage 5 is rare without a named enablement function driving it continuously: treat these as general practitioner observations, not a specific published study.

Action by Stage

  • Stage 1: Start with the 6-family objection taxonomy and the A-L-E-R-T framework (see the New-Hire Onboarding Toolkit).
  • Stage 2: Formalize certification (Bronze/Silver/Gold Rubric) so scoring isn't gut-feel.
  • Stage 3: Instrument ORR tracking and structured CRM loss-reasons (Data & Measurement dimension).
  • Stage 4: Run the ROI/Conversion calculators to quantify the value of further investment.
  • Stage 5: Focus on Gold-tier coaching capacity and win-back triggers from deal autopsies.

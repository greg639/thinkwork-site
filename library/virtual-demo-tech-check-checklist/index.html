Virtual Demo Tech-Check Checklist

The pre-call checklist that eliminates the #1 demo killer: a tech failure in the first 90 seconds: organized by T-24h, T-2h, T-15min, and T-2min.

How to use it

Work through it top to bottom starting 24 hours before every virtual demo, and keep the T-15-minute and T-2-minute sections visible on a second screen during the call itself.

What's inside

  • T-24 hour checklist (environment data, links, custom slides)
  • T-2 hour checklist (restart, notifications, connection, power, backups)
  • T-15 minute checklist (self-test video/audio/screen share, framing)
  • T-2 minute checklist (mute, water, recording)
  • First-90-seconds-of-call protocol
  • Mid-demo tech failure recovery steps and script

The #1 demo killer isn't a bad narrative, it's 90 seconds of dead air fixing audio while the prospect's attention evaporates. Run this every time, no exceptions, even on your 500th demo.

T-24 Hours

  • Confirm meeting link, dial-in, and calendar invite sent to all attendees (including any added late)
  • Confirm demo environment/account is loaded with realistic data relevant to this prospect's industry (not blank or generic seed data)
  • Confirm any custom slides, ROI numbers, or logos in the deck are prospect-specific and spelled correctly
  • Test screen-share of the exact browser tab/app you'll use: not a different device than the one you'll actually present from

T-2 Hours

  • Restart your laptop (clears memory leaks, notification backlogs, forces updates to finish now instead of mid-demo)
  • Close every application and browser tab except what you need: no email, Slack, or chat clients running
  • Turn off ALL notifications: OS-level, browser, Slack, email, calendar pop-ups, phone (put phone in another room or on airplane mode)
  • Confirm wired ethernet connection if available; if Wi-Fi only, run a speed test (need reliably 10+ Mbps upload)
  • Charge laptop to 100% and plug in: do not run on battery
  • Confirm backup device (second laptop or tablet) is charged and has the meeting link ready
  • Confirm mobile hotspot is set up and tested as an internet backup

T-15 Minutes

  • Join the meeting room alone to test video, audio in, audio out, and screen share end-to-end
  • Check camera framing, lighting (face lit from front, not backlit by a window), and background
  • Log into the demo environment and navigate to the exact starting screen: don't start cold from a login page
  • Open only the tabs/windows you'll actually show, in the order you'll show them
  • Silence/mute any desktop widgets, dock badges, or messaging previews that could pop up on shared screen
  • Have the Demo Storyboard printed or on a second monitor: never scroll through it on the shared screen

T-2 Minutes

  • Re-join or confirm you're still connected with stable audio/video
  • Mute yourself until the prospect joins to avoid open-mic noise
  • Have water within reach, phone silenced and out of view
  • Confirm recording tool (if used) is armed and consent language ready if required

First 90 Seconds of the Call (the danger zone)

  • Confirm everyone can hear and see you before you start sharing your screen, ask directly: "Can you all see my screen clearly?"
  • Do not start screen-share until audio/video is confirmed two-way
  • If anything fails in these 90 seconds: acknowledge it once, calmly, execute your backup plan (switch device, switch to phone hotspot, or send the backup recorded walkthrough link): do not apologize repeatedly or narrate the troubleshooting

If Something Still Breaks Mid-Demo

  • Have a backup device already logged in and standing by, not to be set up from scratch after failure
  • Have a pre-recorded 3-minute version of the core scene ready to share as a fallback if live environment goes down entirely
  • Know your one-line recovery script in advance: "That's actually a good moment to show you what this looks like from a recorded walkthrough, one second." (Never: "Sorry, this never happens, let me just…")

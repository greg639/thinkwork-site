Playbook Adoption Scorecard

A stage-by-stage scoring system that tells you whether reps are actually running the playbook in live deals, or whether it was published once and forgotten.

What's inside

  • Stage-mapping step to define your own pipeline stages
  • Four adoption metrics scored per stage: reference rate, artifact usage, manager-observed adherence, outcome correlation
  • Per-rep and per-team roll-up scoring (0-20 per stage)
  • Green / Yellow / Red banding with a required action for each
  • A rep-problem vs. playbook-problem diagnostic rule for reading team-wide Red scores
  • A fill-in team dashboard template across all stages

Purpose: Measure whether reps are actually executing the playbook stage-by-stage — not just whether it exists and was announced in a kickoff six months ago. Distinguishes individual non-adoption (coach the rep) from systemic playbook failure (fix the playbook).

Cadence: Monthly, using CRM stage-gate data + a sample of call recordings (minimum 5 calls per rep per stage per quarter) + manager 1:1 notes.

Step 1 — Map your playbook's stages

List your actual pipeline stages (example below — replace with yours):

  1. Discovery
  2. Qualification
  3. Demo / Proof
  4. Proposal
  5. Negotiation
  6. Close

Step 2 — Score each stage on 4 adoption metrics (0-5 each, 20 pts per stage)

Metric01-23-45
Reference rate — rep opened/consulted the stage guide during a deal at that stage (CRM/content-tool logs)NeverRarely (<20% of deals)Sometimes (20-60%)Consistently (>60%)
Artifact usage — the stage's actual template/talk-track/email was used, with evidence (attached doc, sent email, logged call)No evidenceEvidence in <25% of deals25-60%>60%
Manager-observed adherence — call recording or live-shadow scored against the stage's specific required behaviorsBehaviors absent1 of 3 present2 of 3 presentAll 3 present
Outcome correlation — stage-specific KPI is being hit (e.g., Discovery: qualification fields completed; Demo: multithread count ≥2; Proposal: mutual close plan attached)KPI not tracked / 0%<40%40-75%>75%

Step 3 — Roll up scores

Per rep, per stage: sum the 4 metrics (max 20). Per rep, overall: sum across all stages (max = 20 × number of stages). Per team: average the per-rep overall scores.

Scoring Bands (per stage, out of 20)

BandScoreMeaningAction
Green16-20Stage is genuinely adoptedNo action; use as internal case study
Yellow10-15Partial adoption1:1 coaching on the specific missing metric this month
Red0-9Adoption has failed at this stageInvestigate before blaming the rep — see Step 4

Step 4 — Diagnose: rep problem or playbook problem?

If a stage is Red for one or two reps → individual coaching gap. Pair with a Green-scoring peer; shadow a live call; re-certify on that stage.

If a stage is Red across the majority of the team → the playbook itself has failed at that stage. Common root causes:

  • The stage guide contradicts what actually works in your market (reps have quietly self-corrected)
  • The associated artifact is too long, poorly formatted, or lives somewhere reps don't naturally look
  • The stage's "required behavior" was never trained, only published
  • The KPI being measured isn't actually controllable by the rep at that stage

Rule: never run a re-training push on a stage that's Red team-wide without first checking whether the playbook content is the actual defect — retraining reps on broken content just produces a second round of non-adoption.

How to use it

Score every rep against each stage monthly using CRM data, a call-recording sample, and manager notes; apply the rep-problem vs. playbook-problem rule before deciding whether to coach the rep or rebuild the stage.

Individual Development Plan (IDP) Template for Sales Reps

A quarter-by-quarter development plan that ties your named skill gaps to specific practice reps, peer examples to study, and a measurable finish line — not a vague "improve your selling" goal.

What's inside

  • Skill Gap Analysis table for naming your top 3 skill gaps with evidence from real calls
  • A repeatable quarter-plan block (use it for all 4 quarters)
  • Career / Role / Results "why master this" fields tied to your named skill focus
  • A week-by-week practice-rep log
  • Peer example fields — who to study and specifically what to watch for
  • Measurable, checkable finish-line criteria instead of vague goals
  • Mid-quarter check-in and end-of-quarter review sections
  • A 12-month rollup table plus manager/rep sign-off

Section A — Profile & Direction (fill in once, revisit each quarter)

  • Name / Role / Current Level: ____________
  • Manager: ____________
  • Career direction (12-24 months): [ ] Deepen this role [ ] Move to ____________ [ ] Move into ____________ specialization
  • Why this direction (career lens — what does hitting this open up for you?): ____________

Cold Calling Tone & Pace Self-Assessment Quiz

A self-scoring quiz on vocal tonality, pacing, and energy — scored against a recording of your own real calls — that flags exactly which delivery habit is costing you connects.

What's inside

  • Recording setup instructions (score the tape, not your memory)
  • 12 self-rated statements across 6 delivery dimensions
  • Dimensions: Pace, Pitch & Inflection, Volume & Energy, Word Choice & Filler, Pausing, Authenticity
  • Scoring key per dimension (out of 10)
  • A habit-to-fix mapping table
  • A 2-week practice drill for your lowest-scoring dimension

Most delivery habits are invisible to the person doing them — you can't hear your own uptalk or monotone the way a prospect does. This quiz forces an outside-ear check by having you score a recording of yourself, not your memory of how the call felt.

Before you start

  1. Record yourself on your next 3 cold calls (a phone voice memo app is enough — you only need audio).
  2. Play back one full call, start to finish, before scoring anything below.
  3. Score based on what you actually hear, not how you intended to sound.

Rate each statement 1 (not at all) to 5 (strongly) based on the recording.

Part 1 — Pace

#StatementScore
1I speed up noticeably in the first 10 seconds compared to how I speak normally.
2I run sentences together with no breath or pause between them.

Part 2 — Pitch & Inflection

#StatementScore
3My voice rises at the end of statements that aren't questions ("uptalk").
4My tone stays flat/monotone through most of the call regardless of content.

Part 3 — Volume & Energy

#StatementScore
5My energy noticeably drops after the first 15 seconds.
6I sound quieter or more hesitant than my normal speaking volume.

Part 4 — Word Choice & Filler

#StatementScore
7I use filler words ("um," "like," "just," "sort of") more than twice in the opening 30 seconds.
8I over-apologize ("sorry to bother you," "I know this is random") before stating my reason for calling.

Part 5 — Pausing

#StatementScore
9I fill every silence instead of pausing after a question to let the prospect answer.
10I don't pause at all after my opening line before continuing.

Part 6 — Authenticity / "Read" Quality

#StatementScore
11It's obvious from the recording that I'm reading or reciting rather than speaking naturally.
12My recorded voice sounds noticeably different (more robotic, more rushed) than how I sound talking to a colleague.

Scoring

Add the two scores in each Part (range 2–10 per dimension). A dimension scoring 7 or higher is an active, connect-costing habit worth fixing first. Score all six dimensions, then rank them — fix the highest first; the others often improve as a side effect.

Habit-to-fix table

Pitch & InflectionUptalk reads as uncertainty; monotone reads as disinterestPractice ending statements on a level or falling pitch; add deliberate emphasis on one key word per sentence

Practice drill

  1. Pick your single highest-scoring (worst) dimension.
  2. Record 5 practice openers today, applying only that one fix.
  3. Play back #1 and #5 back to back — the difference should be audible even to you.
  4. Re-run this quiz in two weeks on a fresh recording.

How to use it

Record three real cold calls, score the recording (not your memory) against all six dimensions, then drill your single worst-scoring habit for two weeks before rescoring.

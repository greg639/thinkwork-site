Team Social Selling Index Benchmark Tracker

A quarterly tracking template for logging every rep's Social Selling Index across LinkedIn's four SSI pillars, so managers can see the team trend line move — or stall — at a glance instead of guessing who's actually building a social pipeline.

What SSI Actually Measures

LinkedIn's Social Selling Index (SSI) scores every profile 0–100, split across four pillars, 0–25 each:

  1. Establish your professional brand — profile completeness, content quality and consistency
  2. Find the right people — how effectively they use search/lead tools to identify the right accounts and contacts
  3. Engage with insights — sharing and reacting to relevant content, starting conversations from it
  4. Build relationships — the size and seniority-relevance of their network of decision-makers

Any rep can check their own score free at linkedin.com/sales/ssi — no Sales Navigator seat required to view it, though the underlying activity that drives pillars 2 and 4 is easier with one.

Team Roll-Up Formulas

Paste these into your tracker sheet:

  • Team Average Total: =AVERAGE(TotalColumn)
  • Team Median Total: =MEDIAN(TotalColumn)
  • % Strong or Elite: =(COUNTIF(BandColumn,"Strong")+COUNTIF(BandColumn,"Elite"))/COUNT(RepColumn)
  • Biggest mover: rep with MAX(QoQ Δ)
  • Biggest decliner: rep with MIN(QoQ Δ)

Flag Rules — When a Score Triggers a Conversation

  • QoQ Δ ≤ -10 points → automatic flag, regardless of band
  • Any single pillar ≤10/25 for 2 consecutive quarters → flag, with that pillar named as the specific coaching focus
  • Total below 40 for 2 consecutive quarters → flag as a development priority, not just a data point

Manager Cadence

  1. Pull every rep's score on the same day each quarter (e.g. first Monday) — consistency matters more than the exact date
  2. Complete the team roll-up within 3 business days
  3. Hold flagged-rep 1:1s within 10 business days of the pull — lead with the specific pillar that triggered the flag, not a generic "your score dropped"
  4. Share the team roll-up (not individual scores) in a team meeting to normalize tracking this as a real metric, not a vanity number

How to use it

Copy the table into a spreadsheet, pull each rep's SSI score (or proxy score) on the same day every quarter, and use the banding and flag rules to decide who needs a coaching conversation versus who's on track.

Demo Certification Program Design Guide

A blueprint for enablement leaders building a formal, tiered demo-certification program: competency tiers, evaluation method, governance, and an 8-week rollout plan.

How to use it

Assign a single named program owner, follow the 8-week rollout timeline to launch Tier 1 first, and use the standardized evaluation scenario plus dual grading on the MEDDPICC scorecard and self-assessment for every certification cycle going forward.

What's inside

  • The business case for certifying demos in one paragraph
  • 3-tier architecture: Certified, Advanced, Master, with explicit entry criteria
  • Evaluation method: standardized scenario, dual grading, calibration process
  • Recertification cadence plus early-trigger rules
  • Governance model: named owner, calibration panel, appeals process
  • Incentive and consequence design (access-gated, not comp-gated in year one)
  • 8-week rollout timeline
  • How the program pulls together the other resources in this library

Why Certify Demos

Without certification, "who's allowed to demo to enterprise prospects" defaults to tenure or manager gut-feel, not demonstrated competency. A certification program makes the bar explicit, makes coaching investment visible as a career path, and gives you a defensible answer when a rep asks "why can't I run that account's demo yet."

The 3-Tier Architecture

Tier 1: Certified (entry bar, required to demo unsupervised)

Requirements:

  • Score 10+ (Solid or above) on all 6 competencies on the Demo Skill Self-Assessment
  • Score 16+ (Developing-to-Solid floor) on the MEDDPICC Demo Scorecard on a recorded evaluation demo
  • Complete battlecard-integration training and pass a live objection role-play
  • Zero use of banned/fake-urgency language observed in the evaluation demo

Tier 2: Advanced (required for enterprise/strategic accounts)

Requirements:

  • Score 16+ (Advanced) on at least 4 of 6 competencies, none below 10
  • Score 24+ (Qualification-Confirmed) on the MEDDPICC Demo Scorecard on two consecutive evaluation demos
  • Demonstrated recovery from a live technical failure (evaluated via recording or shadowed live demo)
  • At least one Post-Demo Feedback Survey Promoter-band (9–10) result on a real deal in the last quarter

Tier 3: Master (coaching-eligible, trains others)

Requirements:

  • Advanced tier held for 2+ consecutive quarters
  • Has mentored at least one rep from Developing to Solid on a specific competency (tracked via a skill development tracker)
  • Contributes to the objection-response library or battlecard content, not just uses it
  • Approved by calibration panel, not self-nominated

Evaluation Method

  • Format: Recorded demo against a standardized scenario (same fictional account, same trigger, same objections): not a real live deal, so grading is fair and comparable across reps.
  • Graders: Minimum 2 graders per evaluation (manager + one peer Master-tier rep or enablement lead) scoring independently on the MEDDPICC Demo Scorecard and the relevant Demo Skill Self-Assessment competencies, then reconciling any score gap greater than 4 points.
  • Calibration: Before each certification cycle, graders jointly score one sample recording together to align on what a "4" versus a "2" actually looks like, skip this and scores drift between graders over time.

Recertification

  • Cadence: Tier 1 recertifies annually; Tier 2 and 3 recertify every 6 months given the higher bar and faster product/market change.
  • Triggers for early re-evaluation regardless of cadence: 3 consecutive Detractor-band (0–6) Post-Demo Feedback Survey results, or a MEDDPICC Demo Scorecard drop into "At-Risk" band on 2 consecutive real deals reviewed in weekly coaching.

Governance Model

  • Program owner: One named enablement lead: not a committee: accountable for the standard, the calendar, and the calibration sessions.
  • Calibration panel: Program owner + 2 rotating Master-tier reps + 1 sales manager, refreshed every 2 quarters to avoid the bar quietly drifting to fit whoever's on the panel longest.
  • Appeals process: Any rep disputing a score can request a third independent grader within 5 business days; the third score is averaged with the closer of the original two, not simply added as a tiebreaker vote.

Incentive & Consequence Design

  • Incentive: Tier gates access to account segments (e.g., only Tier 2+ demos strategic accounts), a visible title/badge in internal systems, and is a named input to promotion criteria for senior/principal rep tracks.
  • Consequence: Failure to hold Tier 1 blocks unsupervised demos on any deal above a defined size threshold: paired demos with a Tier 2+ rep instead, not a blanket performance flag.
  • Avoid tying certification directly to compensation in year one: tie it to account access and career track first, so the program is read as a development investment, not a punitive gate, while it's still being calibrated.

8-Week Rollout Timeline

WeekMilestone
1–2Program owner named; tier criteria finalized; calibration panel formed
3Calibration session, panel jointly scores sample recordings to align grading
4Standardized evaluation scenario built and piloted with 2–3 volunteer reps
5Full team completes the Demo Skill Self-Assessment as a baseline
6–7First certification cohort submits recorded evaluations; graded and reconciled
8Results announced; Tier 1 gate takes effect for new deals going forward (never retroactively pulled from reps already running live deals)

How This Program Pulls Everything Together

This guide is the assembly instructions for the rest of the library:

  • The "Why Change Now" Narrative Framework and Battlecard-to-Demo Cheat Sheet define the behaviors being certified.
  • The Demo Skill Self-Assessment and MEDDPICC Demo Scorecard are the two instruments used for every evaluation.
  • The Demo Environment & Sandbox Setup Checklist ensures the evaluation environment itself doesn't invalidate the result.
  • The Post-Demo Prospect Feedback Survey supplies the real-deal evidence used for recertification triggers.
  • The Demo Coaching Toolkit for Sales Managers is the weekly operating rhythm that keeps reps moving toward the next tier between formal certification cycles.

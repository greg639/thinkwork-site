Straight answers

Questions people type into a search box

How old do you have to be to play at Ace Combat Airsoft?
Eleven or older. Anyone under 16 must wear a full face mask, and a parent or guardian signs the consent form.
How much does a day at Ace Combat Airsoft cost?
Walk-on with your own kit is £32. The rental package is £57: ASG M4, full face mask, two high cap magazines, assault vest and 1000 rounds. Tea and coffee are included in both. Lunch is £6.50.
When is Ace Combat Airsoft open?
Sunday game days, 08:30 to 16:00. The published dates are Sundays 27 September, 11 October, 25 October (Halloween), 8 November, 22 November, 6 December and 20 December (Christmas) 2026. Book here and pay by card when you book.
Can I hire a gun at Ace Combat Airsoft?
Yes. Choose the rental package when you book. Today a £25 deposit is paid online as you book and the balance in cash on the day; let us know by the Friday before and the deposit is refunded. Proposed: the package paid in full when you book, so the rack is counted on Friday.
How much is membership at Ace Combat Airsoft?
Adult membership is £80 and junior membership is £80. Both give £5 off the walk-on fee, no more safety brief and 10% off the on-site shop. UKARA registration is £10.
What are the FPS limits at Ace Combat Airsoft?
With a 0.2g BB: pistols and full auto 360 FPS (1.2 joules); semi-locked DMR 450 FPS (1.87 joules), age 18 and over; bolt action sniper 500 FPS (2.31 joules), no gas, age 18 and over; under-18 bolt action 400 FPS (1.48 joules). Anything over 360 FPS has a minimum engagement distance of 30 metres.
Is Ace Combat Airsoft UKARA registered?
Yes, a UKARA registered game site since 12 November 2007. Registration needs three games at the same site over at least 56 days, age 18 or over and a form of identification; it costs £10 and stays valid while you keep attending.
Is there food and a shop?
Yes. The safezone is a fully enclosed shelter with lighting and a well-stocked shop selling snacks, drinks and essential airsoft gear; free tea and coffee all day. Lunch is £6.50, booked ahead. Toilets are by the car park.
Where is Ace Combat Airsoft?
Old Hay, Brenchley, Kent, TN12 7DG: a private road on the left called Old Hay, by the converted oast house, then follow the red signs to the car park. A short drive from the M20 and five minutes from The Hop Farm in Paddock Wood.
Can I book a birthday party, a stag or hen do, or a team-building day?
Yes: kids' parties from age 11, stag and hen events and corporate team building, arranged around the group. A quote on screen with a deposit is proposed to replace the enquiry form.
Does airsoft at Ace Combat Airsoft count for the Duke of Edinburgh Award?
Airsoft is officially listed as an approved physical activity for the Duke of Edinburgh Award scheme.

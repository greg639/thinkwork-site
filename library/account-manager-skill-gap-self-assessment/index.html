Account Manager Skill-Gap Self-Assessment

A 20-question self-audit across the five core account-management competencies, with a personalized development report and specific actions for closing your lowest-scoring gaps.

What's inside

  • 20 self-rating statements across 5 core account-management competencies
  • A 1–5 scoring scale with per-competency and total scoring
  • Four development bands per competency: Foundational, Developing, Proficient, Advanced
  • A personalized development action for every competency scoring below Proficient
  • A full development report table to track scores over time
  • Direct links to the specific tools (stakeholder map, ROI slide, save plan) that close each gap

How this works

Rate yourself honestly on all 20 statements using this scale:

1 = Strongly Disagree · 2 = Disagree · 3 = Neutral · 4 = Agree · 5 = Strongly Agree

There are 5 competencies, 4 statements each. Score each competency out of 20, then read your development report.

Competency 1: Relationship & Stakeholder Management

  1. I can name every stakeholder in my top accounts and their level of influence over the renewal decision.
  2. I have a direct relationship with at least one economic buyer (not just a day-to-day user) in each of my top accounts.
  3. I proactively build relationships with new stakeholders before they become critical to a decision.
  4. I know how each key stakeholder in my accounts is personally measured/evaluated in their role.

Subtotal: ____ / 20

Competency 2: Account Strategy & Planning

  1. Every one of my top accounts has a written account plan I update at least quarterly.
  2. I can articulate each account's business goals for this year without checking my notes.
  3. I proactively identify whitespace (unused seats, unadopted modules, adjacent needs) rather than waiting for the customer to ask.
  4. I prioritize my time across my book based on risk and opportunity, not just whoever emails me first.

Subtotal: ____ / 20

Competency 3: Value Communication & ROI Articulation

  1. I can quantify the value delivered to each top account in the customer's own business metrics (not just usage stats).
  2. I proactively share ROI/value summaries with customers rather than waiting to be asked to justify the spend.
  3. I can translate product usage data into a story a non-technical executive would find compelling in under 2 minutes.
  4. I tailor my value story to what each specific stakeholder cares about, rather than using one generic pitch.

Subtotal: ____ / 20

Competency 4: Renewal & Expansion Execution

  1. I start renewal conversations at least 60 days before contract end, not at the deadline.
  2. I have a specific plan for expansion (upsell/cross-sell) in each account, not just a plan to hold flat.
  3. I can handle a price objection without immediately offering a discount.
  4. I've successfully turned around at least one at-risk account in the last 12 months using a documented save plan.

Subtotal: ____ / 20

Competency 5: Data-Driven Account Health Management

  1. I check usage/health data on my top accounts at least monthly, not just before a QBR.
  2. I can identify a churn-risk signal (usage drop, sentiment dip, stakeholder change) before the customer raises it themselves.
  3. I use account health data to decide where to spend my time this week.
  4. I keep my CRM/account records current enough that a colleague could take over one of my accounts with minimal handoff loss.

Subtotal: ____ / 20

Bands per competency:

  • 4–9: Foundational — this is your highest-leverage development priority. Pick one habit below and build it into your weekly routine for the next 30 days.
  • 10–14: Developing — you have the basics; inconsistency is likely costing you in your toughest accounts.
  • 15–17: Proficient — solid and reliable; sharpen with the stretch habit below.
  • 18–20: Advanced — this is a strength; consider mentoring a peer or documenting your approach as a team playbook.

Development actions by competency (for any score under 15)

  • Relationship & Stakeholder Management → Build a stakeholder map (see the Stakeholder Mapping Cheat Sheet) for your top 5 accounts this week.
  • Account Strategy & Planning → Block 90 minutes every Friday to write or update one account plan — rotate through your book.
  • Value Communication & ROI Articulation → Before your next QBR, build a one-slide ROI summary using the customer's own KPIs, not your product's usage metrics.
  • Renewal & Expansion Execution → Set a recurring reminder at 60 days pre-renewal for every account, without exception.
  • Data-Driven Account Health Management → Run the Churn Signal Quiz on every account scoring below 15 here, once a month.

How to use it

Score yourself competency by competency, identify your lowest-scoring area, and commit to the single development action listed for it for the next 30 days before retaking the assessment.

LinkedIn Sales Navigator Alternatives & Add-Ons Directory

The standalone databases that can replace Sales Navigator entirely, and the extensions that extend it — organized so you know which category you actually need before you buy.

What's inside

  • Full alternatives — standalone prospecting databases that replace Sales Nav
  • CRM-sync add-ons that extend Sales Nav into your pipeline
  • List-export and cleanup add-ons
  • Automation add-ons for connections and messaging (with a risk note)
  • Personality/insight add-ons for messaging tone
  • A fit table by team size and budget
  • A caution note on LinkedIn's terms of service and account-restriction risk

Full Alternatives (Replace Sales Navigator)

  • Apollo.io — the largest combined database + engagement platform alternative; many teams replace Sales Nav and a separate sequencer with Apollo alone.
  • Lusha — contact/company data with a strong browser extension; lighter-weight and lower-cost than Sales Nav.
  • Cognism — phone-verified international data with a strong compliance posture, popular for EMEA-focused teams.
  • ZoomInfo — the highest-priced alternative but the deepest data and intent layer; typically replaces Sales Nav for larger enterprise teams.
  • RocketReach / Seamless.AI / UpLead — lower-cost contact-lookup alternatives suited to SMB budgets.

Add-Ons & Extensions (Extend Sales Navigator)

  • Surfe (formerly Leadjet) — syncs Sales Navigator profiles and conversations directly into your CRM with one click.
  • Evaboot — exports and cleans Sales Navigator search results/lead lists into a usable spreadsheet.
  • Dux-Soup — automates profile visits, connection requests, and follow-up messaging on top of your existing Sales Nav searches.
  • Expandi / We-Connect / Waalaxy — LinkedIn automation layers for running connection + message cadences at scale.
  • Crystal Knows — adds DISC-style personality insight to a profile to inform messaging tone before you reach out.
  • Octopus CRM — lightweight LinkedIn automation suited to smaller teams already living inside Sales Navigator day to day.

Caution: LinkedIn's Terms of Service

Automation tools (connection-request bots, auto-messaging) run against LinkedIn's terms of service and carry real account-restriction risk, especially at high volume. Keep daily action limits conservative, spread activity across a warm-up period for new accounts, and treat any automation tool as a productivity aid layered on top of genuine human review — not a replacement for it.

How to use it

If you're deciding whether to keep paying for Sales Navigator, compare it against the alternatives list first; if you already have it and just need it to do more, pick one add-on from the extension list that matches your specific gap (CRM sync, list export, or automation).

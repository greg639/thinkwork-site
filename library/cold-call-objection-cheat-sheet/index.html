Cold Call Objection Cheat Sheet

A single printable page covering the 10 objections reps hear most in the first 30 seconds of a cold call, each with a 10-second response and a follow-up question to keep the line open.

What's inside

  • One-page table of the 10 most common first-30-seconds objections
  • A 10-second scripted response for each objection
  • A follow-up question for each to keep the call alive
  • The 'Acknowledge → Reframe → Ask' golden rule framework
  • Covers 'not interested,' 'busy,' 'send an email,' 'already have someone,' and 6 more
  • A print/pin formatting note for use during live dialing

One-page quick reference for the first 30 seconds of a call.

The Golden Rule: Acknowledge → Reframe → Ask. Never argue with the objection. Acknowledge it in one breath, reframe in one sentence, then ask a question that keeps them talking.

#You HearSay This (10 seconds)Follow-Up Question
1"I'm not interested""Fair — most people say that before they know what this is.""Can I tell you the one thing we help with in 10 seconds?"
2"I'm busy / bad time""Totally get it — I'll be fast.""Can I ask one qualifying question before I let you go?"
3"Just send me an email""Can do — I want it to be useful, not generic.""What's the one thing you'd want it to address?"
4"We already have someone for that""Good to know — glad it's covered.""What's the one thing you wish worked better with them?"
5"How'd you get this number?""[Honest source] — I reached out for a specific reason.""Want the 15-second version of why I called?"
6"We don't have budget""Understood — that's common before people see the cost of the status quo.""Worth 10 minutes to see if that changes anything?"
7"Take me off your list""Done — you won't hear from me again on this.""Just curious — bad timing, or genuinely not relevant?"
8"What's this about?""[Company] helps [role] do [outcome] — calling because [trigger].""Worth two minutes?"
9"I'm not the decision maker""Appreciate you saying so.""Who owns this, and is it even on their radar right now?"
10"We're all set""Good to hear — glad things are running smoothly.""All set at a 9 or 10, or a 7 nobody's questioned lately?"

Print/pin note: Keep this face-up next to your dialer. Read the matching row, not the whole sheet — this is muscle memory, not a script to be read verbatim on the call.

How to use it

Print it and keep it face-up next to your dialer; glance at the matching row the instant you hear the objection rather than reading the whole sheet mid-call.

How a turnover works

A day in the life of your holiday let.

From "thanks, we loved it" to the next guest's key in the door, this is what happens in between.

  1. 1
    Check-out morning

    Guests leave. We let ourselves in.

    We use the spare key we hold, or the key safe, so you do not have to be there. No waiting about for a handover.

  2. 2
    First job

    Count it before we clean it.

    The inventory check comes first, on a tablet, against the list we built at your walk-through. Anything chipped, stained or missing gets a photo before it becomes an argument.

  3. 3
    The main event

    Clean, reset, restock.

    Beds made with fresh linen, towels folded, soap and loo roll topped up, the kitchen wiped down. The place should look like the listing photos again.

  4. 4
    While we are there

    A safety eye-over.

    Smoke and CO alarms tested, a look for drips, loose fittings and anything the last guests broke. Small fixes can be done on the spot once you have said yes.

    Alarms tested every visit
  5. 5
    Before check-in

    Ticked off and sent to you.

    The checklist, notes and photos go into your owner portal, so you can see it is done without having to ask. See the portal.

    Checklist in your portal
  6. 6
    Late that evening, maybe

    A guest is stuck outside.

    Flight delayed, key safe code lost, phone at two per cent. We hold the spare and we are on call, so it is one call to us instead of a bad review.

This is a typical turnover. Your own to-do list is built at the free walk-through. [owner photos to replace these stand-ins]

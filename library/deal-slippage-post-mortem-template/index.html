Deal Slippage Post-Mortem Template

A structured after-action review to run on every deal that slips a forecasted quarter, so you find the real root cause instead of a vague "pushed to Q3" and the same pattern repeats next quarter.

What's inside

  • Deal snapshot fields (name, amount, original vs. new close date, rep, manager, forecast category, stage)
  • Timeline reconstruction table comparing planned vs. actual milestone dates
  • 8-category root cause classification (single-threaded, no compelling event, champion left, legal/procurement bottleneck, budget not confirmed, competitive re-open, rep over-forecasted, internal reorg)
  • Contributing factor checklist across process, product, competitive and internal causes
  • Live 5-Whys root cause drill script
  • Forecast accuracy honesty check
  • Action items table with owner and due date
  • Cross-deal pattern log to catch repeat root causes across the quarter
  • Manager sign-off and calibration note

Run this within 5 business days of any deal slipping out of its forecasted quarter. One post-mortem per slipped deal. Rep completes Sections 1-4 before the meeting; manager facilitates Sections 5-7 live.

Section 3: Root Cause Classification

Pick the ONE primary category that best explains the slip (secondary factors go in Section 4).

  • Single-threaded — deal died/stalled with one champion, no multi-thread into economic buyer or procurement
  • No compelling event — buyer had no internal deadline forcing a decision by the forecasted date
  • Champion left / went dark — internal champion changed roles, lost influence, or stopped responding
  • Legal/procurement bottleneck — paperwork (security review, MSA redlines, procurement queue) outlasted the forecast window
  • Budget not confirmed — deal was forecasted before budget was actually approved/allocated
  • Competitive re-open — a competitor entered or re-entered the evaluation late
  • Rep over-forecasted — deal was never realistically a Commit; optimism bias, not a buyer-side event, caused the slip
  • Internal reorg/priority shift on buyer side — buyer's business priorities changed independent of our sales process

Section 4: Contributing Factors (check all that apply)

  • Economic buyer never met directly
  • No mutual close plan existed or it wasn't followed
  • Discovery was incomplete (unclear success criteria / metrics)
  • Pricing/packaging required unplanned approval escalation
  • Security/compliance review not started early enough
  • CRM stage did not reflect real deal progress (stage inflation)
  • Manager was not looped in until after the slip was already likely
  • Deal desk / legal was engaged too late

How to use it

Complete one copy per slipped deal within 5 business days of the slip; the rep fills Sections 1-4 solo, then the manager runs Sections 5-7 as a live 20-minute conversation, and Section 8 gets rolled up across the team each quarter to spot repeat root causes.

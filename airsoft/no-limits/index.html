Straight answers

Questions people type
into a search box

How much is paintball at No Limits Paintball and Laser?
£40 per person for a three-hour indoor session: professional marker, tactical vest and mask, unlimited reballs, multiple game missions, the indoor multi level arena. Ages 11 and over.
How much is laser tag?
£20 per person for one hour or £30 for two, with safe laser equipment, full head protection, a tactical vest and trained marshals. Ages 6 and over.
How much is airsoft?
The two-hour Airsoft Experience is £40 per person with a rental rifle, 1,000 BBs and full face protection. Sunday walk-on is £30 for seven hours (£25 for members), Wednesday walk-on is £23 for four hours (£20 for members). Equipment hire is £10.
Is there a minimum age?
Laser tag is ages 6 and over. Paintball and airsoft are 11 and over. Under 18s at airsoft must have a parent or guardian present and sign the parental disclaimer.
Is it suitable for beginners?
Yes. All sessions are marshalled and structured. We brief every group before play. You don’t need experience to start.
What should I wear?
Comfortable clothing you can move in. Trainers recommended. All essential equipment is provided on site.
Does paintball hurt?
Paintball is a high impact activity. Protective equipment is provided and games are supervised. Most players describe it as intense rather than painful.
Do we need to buy extra paint?
No. All sessions include unlimited reballs. There are no mid game purchases or add ons.
How many players per session?
Group sizes vary by session and game type. Proposed: the slot picker shows the places left on each slot, so you do not have to ring to ask.
How much is membership?
£25 a year: discounts on walk-ons and consumables, member events, and eligibility for UKARA registration after three games over at least two months, 18 and over.
How do I book?
Today: call 07464 482410 or email info@nolimitspaintballandlaser.co.uk to check availability, and we lock in your slot. Proposed: pick the game and the slot on the page and pay the deposit by card.
Where is No Limits Paintball and Laser?
Unit 4, King Street (rear), Gateshead, NE8 2YP, five minutes from Newcastle city centre, with free parking on site. Phone 07464 482410.

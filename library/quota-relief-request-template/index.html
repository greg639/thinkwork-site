Quota Relief Request Template

A pre-built business-case format for requesting a quota adjustment after territory loss, market shock, or extended leave — built to get a fast, fair answer instead of a stalled conversation.

What's inside

  • Full fill-in template: rep info, current quota, trigger event, and requested adjustment
  • 3 qualifying trigger categories (territory change, market shock, extended leave) with what evidence each needs
  • The impact-quantification formula that converts the trigger into a defensible dollar/percentage ask
  • A supporting-data checklist to attach before submitting
  • The approval chain and expected turnaround time to set with your manager up front
  • A worked example filled in end to end for a territory-loss scenario
  • Guidance on what NOT to include (subjective language that weakens an otherwise strong case)

Section 3 — Impact Quantification

Use the formula matching your trigger category:

Territory loss: `` Adjusted Quota = Original Quota × (Remaining Book Value / Original Book Value) ``

Market shock: `` Adjusted Quota = Original Quota × (1 − Team-Wide Attainment Shortfall %) `` (Use the average shortfall % across the whole affected team/segment, not just your own number — this is what makes the case defensible rather than self-serving.)

Extended leave: `` Adjusted Quota = Original Quota × (Selling Days Remaining / Total Period Selling Days) ``

Section 4 — Supporting Data Checklist

  • CRM export showing removed/affected accounts and their trailing-12-month revenue
  • Team or segment-wide attainment data (for market-shock requests — never submit solo data alone)
  • HR/leave documentation (for extended-leave requests)
  • Original quota-setting documentation or territory assignment record
  • Manager's written acknowledgment that the trigger event occurred (separate from their approval of the adjustment itself)

Section 5 — Approval Chain & Timeline

StepOwnerTarget turnaround
1. Submit to direct managerRep—
2. Manager review & sign-off on trigger validityManager5 business days
3. Comp/RevOps calculation reviewComp team5 business days
4. Final approvalManager's manager or comp committee5 business days
Total expected turnaround~15 business days

Set this timeline explicitly with your manager at submission — a request with no agreed timeline is the most common way these stall indefinitely.

Worked example (Territory loss)

Original quota: $800,000. Territory realignment removed 3 named accounts worth $180,000 in trailing-12-month revenue out of a $800,000 book. Adjusted Quota = $800,000 × (($800,000 − $180,000) / $800,000) = $800,000 × 0.775 = $620,000 Requested adjusted quota: $620,000, effective the date of territory change, prorated for the remainder of the period.

What NOT to include

  • Subjective framing ("this territory was always harder") — replace with the actual data comparison
  • Comparisons to specific named colleagues' quotas — keep the case about your own book, not relative fairness arguments
  • Requests without a formula-based number — "please lower my quota" without Section 3 completed will stall in review every time

How to use it

Fill in the section matching your trigger category, run the matching formula in Section 3 to arrive at a specific dollar number, attach every item on the Section 4 checklist, and agree the Section 5 timeline with your manager the same day you submit.

New Hire Onboarding Feedback Survey Template

The end-of-ramp survey that tells enablement what to fix in the next cohort's onboarding, straight from the people who just lived it.

What's inside

  • Section A — Pre-boarding & first impressions (4 questions)
  • Section B — Training content quality and pacing (5 questions)
  • Section C — Manager & buddy support (4 questions)
  • Section D — Confidence by skill area, week one vs. now (5 ratings)
  • Section E — Overall ramp timeline + recommend-to-a-friend question
  • Enablement-only scoring and cohort-trend summary table
  • Top-3-themes and committed-change fields for closing the loop

When to send: End of ramp — typically day 60 or day 90, once the new hire has run real calls independently. Format: 1–5 scale unless noted (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree). Anonymize by cohort, not by individual, if you want candor.

Section A — Pre-boarding & First Impressions

  1. I felt informed and prepared before my first day. (1–5)
  2. The paperwork/logistics process was smooth. (1–5)
  3. I knew who my manager and onboarding buddy were before I started. (1–5)
  4. Open text: What's one thing that would have made your first week smoother?

Section B — Training Content

  1. The training content was relevant to what I actually do day-to-day. (1–5)
  2. The pace of training matched my ability to absorb it (not too fast, not too slow). (1–5)
  3. I had enough hands-on practice (roleplay, shadowing, live reps) before being expected to perform. (1–5)
  4. The tools/systems training (CRM, dialer, etc.) prepared me to use them confidently. (1–5)
  5. Open text: Which part of training was most valuable? Which part would you cut or shorten?

Section C — Manager & Buddy Support

  1. My manager checked in with me regularly during ramp. (1–5)
  2. My onboarding buddy was genuinely useful. (1–5)
  3. I felt comfortable asking "dumb questions" during my first month. (1–5)
  4. I received specific, actionable feedback on my calls/work — not just "good job." (1–5)

Section D — Confidence by Skill Area

Rate your current confidence (1–5) in each, and compare to how you felt in week one:

  1. Discovery / qualifying conversations
  2. Handling objections
  3. Product/domain knowledge
  4. Navigating internal tools and process
  5. Understanding how my role ladders up to company goals

Section E — Overall

  1. Looking back, I felt "ramped" and productive by day: [30 / 60 / 90 / still ramping] (select one)
  2. I would recommend this onboarding experience to a friend joining in my role. (1–5, or 0–10 NPS-style)
  3. Open text: If you were redesigning onboarding for the next cohort, what's the one change you'd make?
  4. Open text: Anything else you want enablement or leadership to know?

How to use it

Send as a form (Google Forms/Typeform) at day 60 or day 90 of every cohort, then fill in the enablement scoring table to compare trends and commit one concrete onboarding change before the next cohort starts.

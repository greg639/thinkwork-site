Sales Kickoff Pre-Read Packet Template

Prep materials sent before kickoff so reps arrive primed instead of cold — a welcome letter, state-of-business memo, self-assessment, and reflection prompts, all ready to fill in and send.

What's inside

  • Welcome letter template with fill-in theme and rationale
  • Agenda-at-a-glance grid
  • State-of-business memo template (targets, wins, market shifts)
  • Competitive landscape one-pager outline
  • Pre-arrival self-assessment (linked to the Skill Mastery Quiz)
  • 3 reflection questions reps answer before they arrive
  • What-to-bring / logistics checklist
  • Manager pre-brief conversation guide (1:1 before kickoff)

Send this to every attendee 1–2 weeks before kickoff. The goal: reps arrive primed with context and a personal goal, so sessions can start at depth instead of re-explaining the basics.

1. Welcome letter (fill in and send as the cover email)

Subject: Your [Company] Kickoff Pre-Read — please complete before [date] Hi team, This year's kickoff theme is "[theme]." We chose it because [one sentence: the business reason this theme matters right now]. Attached is everything you need to arrive ready — not cold. It should take about [X minutes] to work through. Please complete the self-assessment and reflection questions before [date] — your manager will use them in your pre-brief 1:1. See you in [city]. [Sender name / role]

5. Pre-arrival self-assessment

Before you arrive, complete the Skill Mastery Self-Check Quiz (Discovery / Negotiation / Objection Handling). Bring your scores — several breakout sessions will ask you to work from your own lowest-scoring item.

6. Reflection questions (answer before you arrive)

  1. What's one deal in the last quarter you wish had gone differently — and which specific skill, if it had been stronger, would have changed the outcome?
  2. What's the one thing you want to be able to do differently by the end of this kickoff?
  3. What's one piece of feedback you've been given repeatedly but haven't yet fixed?

(Bring written answers — your manager will reference these in your pre-brief.)

7. Logistics checklist

  • Travel booked and confirmed
  • Hotel confirmed, arrival/departure dates match agenda
  • Dress code confirmed: _________
  • Laptop + charger + company badge/ID
  • Breakout pre-reads printed or downloaded offline
  • Expense process for the trip reviewed

8. Manager pre-brief guide (1:1, the week before kickoff)

Use these prompts in a 15-minute call with each rep before they travel:

  1. "Walk me through your answers to the 3 reflection questions — what stood out to you?"
  2. "Looking at your Skill Mastery scores, which of the three is your priority this quarter?"
  3. "What's one specific thing you want to leave kickoff able to do that you can't do today?"
  4. "I'll check in with you on this 30 days after we're back — what should I ask you?"

How to use it

Send the completed packet 1-2 weeks before kickoff, and have managers run the pre-brief 1:1 the week before so every rep arrives with a personal goal for the event, not just a boarding pass.

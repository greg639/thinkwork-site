Questions

FAQ

The things we are asked most.

What happens if it rains?

You will get wet.

Does it hurt?

Close shots can sting a bit. We recommend an extra layer of clothing.

What should I wear?

Hardwearing clothes you can move around in freely, and sensible footwear with good ankle support. The terrain can be slippery. Not your best new trainers.

What time should we get there?

Gates open 08:00 and close 09:00. Go straight to reception to sign in before unloading. Chrono is open from 08:30, and the safety briefing is between 09:30 and 10:00. Rental players should arrive at 08:30.

What time does it finish?

That is up to you. Your £30 buys the morning session, and you can play on into the afternoon at no extra charge. We close around 15:30.

What is the minimum age?

10 years attained. Under 16s need written permission from a parent or guardian.

What are your FPS limits?

We chrono on joules and convert to your preferred BB weight. On 0.20g that is roughly 350fps for an AEG, 450fps for a DMR and 500fps bolt action.

Can I bring my own BBs?

Yes, if you can prove they are biodegradable. If you cannot, you will not be able to use them. We stock all weights of bio in the shop.

How much is a day?

From £30 per person. The full list is on prices.

How do I book?

Online through Eventbrite, or call 07722 22 1066. Minimum group size to hold an event is 10 players.

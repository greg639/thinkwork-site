Whitespace Mapping Worksheet

A one-page visual worksheet for mapping every business unit, product line, and use case inside an existing account, so you can see at a glance what you own, what's contested, and what's completely unowned.

What's inside

  • Account snapshot header: ARR, seats, renewal date, economic buyer, champion
  • Org/business-unit grid with a 4-state footprint legend (Owned / Piloting / Competitor / Unowned)
  • Use-case by business-unit matrix to surface job-to-be-done whitespace, not just seat whitespace
  • Stakeholder overlay tying a named budget owner to every filled cell
  • Impact x Access scoring formula for every whitespace cell
  • 2x2 prioritization quadrant: Attack Now / Build Access / Quick Win / Park
  • 90-day whitespace action plan with owner and target date per cell

Purpose: get one page that shows every team, product line, and use case inside an account - what you own, what's contested, and what's sitting completely unowned. Fill this out per strategic account, once a quarter, in a 30-45 minute working session with your CSM/AE pair.

Status legend (use for every row):

  • Owned (O) - live, paying, adopted
  • Piloting (P) - trial or early rollout in progress
  • Competitor (C) - using a named competitor's tool today
  • Unowned (U) - no tool in this category that you know of, or unknown

Section 5 - Whitespace Scoring

Score every whitespace cell (U or P) on two axes, 1-5 each:

  • Impact - seats x average deal size potential if this cell converts to Owned
  • Access - how warm is your path in: do you have a champion, a referral, or an existing relationship reaching this BU?

Section 6 - Prioritization Quadrant

Plot each whitespace cell from Section 5:

  • High Impact, High Access -> ATTACK NOW (this quarter)
  • High Impact, Low Access -> BUILD ACCESS (find the internal referral path first)
  • Low Impact, High Access -> QUICK WIN (land small, use it as an internal reference)
  • Low Impact, Low Access -> PARK (revisit next quarter)

How this gets used: the whitespace count in Section 3 becomes the account's expansion ceiling; the quadrant in Section 6 becomes the next-two-quarters pipeline plan. Re-run this worksheet every quarter - whitespace shrinks as you land and shifts as the account reorganizes.

How to use it

Run this in a 30-45 minute working session per strategic account each quarter with your CSM/AE pair; fill Sections 1-4 from what you already know plus a quick LinkedIn/org-chart pull, then use the scoring and quadrant in Sections 5-6 to build the 90-day plan in Section 7.

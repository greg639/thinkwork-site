New Sales Leader 30-60-90 Day Plan Template

A fill-in operating plan for a new sales leader's first 90 days: a 30-day diagnostic before you touch anything, 2-3 credible quick wins in days 31-60, and full ownership of the number and the rebuild by day 90.

How to use it

Fill in every bracketed field before day one, agree the three checkpoint dates with your own manager up front, and update the document weekly rather than treating it as a one-time write-up.

What's inside

  • Day 1-30 diagnostic checklist (stakeholder interviews, pipeline audit, win/loss pull, ride-alongs, systems/tools audit)
  • Stakeholder interview table with the exact question to ask each role
  • Day 30 red-flag log for anything needing your manager's sign-off before you act
  • Days 31-60 quick-win selection criteria plus a rebuild-in-progress tracker
  • Weekly coaching-rhythm checklist to stand up immediately (1:1s, call reviews, pipeline reviews)
  • Days 61-90 forecast-ownership and structural-decision tracker (territory, comp, people)
  • Day 90 scorecard comparing baseline vs. actual vs. target across 6 core metrics
  • One-page summary template to hand your own manager at each checkpoint

How to use this document

Fill in every bracketed field before you start. This is a working document: update it weekly, not once. Bring it to your first 1:1 with your own manager and agree the Day 30, 60, and 90 checkpoints before you begin.

Phase 1, Days 1-30: Diagnose. Do not fix anything yet.

The only job in the first 30 days is to build an accurate picture. Sales leaders who fail in year one almost always fix something in week two that didn't need fixing, or break something that was quietly working.

1:1 Stakeholder Interviews (target: complete by Day 20)

Talk to every person on this list before you change a single process, comp plan, or territory.

Pipeline & Number Audit

  • Pull last 4 quarters of pipeline creation, conversion, and win-rate by stage
  • Identify which stage deals actually die at (not which stage they're logged as dying at)
  • Check forecast accuracy: compare last 2 quarters' committed forecast to actual close
  • Identify sandbagging or happy-ears patterns (deals stuck at 90% for 60+ days)
  • Segment win rate by rep, by deal source, by competitor present

Win/Loss Review

  • Read (don't just skim) the last 10 closed-won deal notes
  • Read the last 10 closed-lost deal notes
  • Listen to or read 5 call recordings/transcripts from each quartile of performer (top, middle, bottom)
  • Note the single biggest recurring reason deals are lost: _______________________

Ride-Alongs / Live Observation

  • Shadow or listen to at least 2 calls per rep (minimum)
  • Note skill gaps repeated across 3+ reps: these are team-level gaps, not individual ones: _______________________

Systems & Tools Audit

  • CRM hygiene: is pipeline data trustworthy or theater?
  • Comp plan: does it reward the behavior you actually want?
  • Territory/account assignment: any obvious imbalance?
  • Enablement/onboarding materials: current or abandoned?

Phase 2, Days 31-60: Quick wins + start the rebuild

Quick wins buy credibility. Pick wins that are visible, fast, and don't require permission you don't have yet.

Quick-Win Selection Criteria

A candidate quick win must be:

  • Fixable without new budget or headcount
  • Visible to the team within 2 weeks
  • Tied to a metric someone already tracks

  • Call-review rhythm set (minimum: 1 recorded call per rep per week reviewed together)
  • Pipeline review cadence set (weekly, deal-by-deal, not just stage totals)

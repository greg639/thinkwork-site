Self-reflection prompts

Day 30: What's the one thing about this role that's harder than I expected? What do I need more support on?

Day 60: Where am I ahead of this plan, and where am I behind? What's the root cause of the gap, if any?

Day 90: What would I tell a new hire starting this role today that I wish someone had told me?

How to use it

New hire fills this out in draft form during week one, reviews it with their manager to set realistic targets, then both sign it and use it as the agenda for every 1:1 through day 90.

Sales Kickoff Methodology Training Outline

A ready-to-run, time-blocked session plan for (re)launching your sales methodology at SKO: pre-work, a full facilitator agenda with four live exercises, and the 30-60-90 reinforcement plan that actually makes it stick after everyone goes home.

How to use it

Send the pre-work packet a week before session day, run the agenda as a facilitator script on the day, then treat the 30-60-90 reinforcement plan as mandatory, the session alone will not create adoption.

A full-day plan for relaunching or reinforcing your sales qualification methodology at SKO. Built to be run, not read, every block below is time-boxed and has a facilitator action attached.

Pre-work (send 1 week before session day, ~45 minutes, individual)

  • Read: 2-page methodology one-pager (framework stages + why-now rationale)
  • Watch: 15-minute recorded "why this, why now" video from sales leadership
  • Self-audit: pick 2 recent deals (1 won, 1 lost) and tag which qualification questions you did and didn't ask, using the framework's checklist
  • Bring: those 2 deals to the session (printed or pulled up in CRM)

Session-day agenda (6 hours)

09:00–09:15: Open: Why This, Why Now (15 min)

Leader shares one stat that shows the cost of not having rigor, e.g., win rate on deals with no confirmed economic buyer vs. deals with one identified. Frame: "This isn't new hoops, it's how we protect the deals you're already working."

09:15–10:00: Methodology Deep Dive, Part 1 (45 min)

Walk each stage/letter of the framework against a real closed-won deal, annotated live. Open Q&A for the last 10 minutes.

10:00–10:45, Exercise 1: Deal Triage Workshop (45 min)

  • Breakout groups of 3–4.
  • Each rep brings their 2 pre-work deals.
  • Group scores each deal against the framework's checklist (see the New Rep Certification Scorecard for a compatible rubric).
  • Output: each rep names the ONE missing qualification element most likely to have cost them the lost deal.

10:45–11:00: Break

11:00–11:45: Methodology Deep Dive, Part 2 (45 min)

Focus stage: objection-proofing the champion / economic-buyer conversation.

11:45–12:30, Exercise 2: Qualification Sprint role-play (45 min)

  • Pairs. One plays rep, one plays a skeptical economic buyer using a "hostile persona" card (pull objections from the Cold Call Opener Swipe File companion resource or your own objection bank).
  • 8-minute rounds, then swap roles.
  • A third person or facilitator scores using the same rubric reps will be certified against.

12:30–13:15: Lunch

13:15–14:00: Parallel tracks

Managers: Calibration session: score 3 recorded calls independently, then compare. Goal: managers land within 1 point of each other on the same call. Reps: Exercise 3: Objection Relay (45 min): round-robin, each rep gets 90 seconds to respond to a randomly drawn hard objection card, group scores 1–3, rotate.

14:00–14:45: Live Deal Review Using the Framework (45 min)

Pick 2 real, currently-open pipeline deals (anonymized if needed). Whole room applies the framework live; facilitator narrates the thinking out loud.

14:45–15:00: Break

15:00–15:30: Certification Exam Walkthrough (30 min)

Explain format, pass threshold, and retake policy. Book certification exam slots for the following week.

15:30–15:50, 30-60-90 Reinforcement Plan Reveal (20 min)

Show reps exactly what reinforcement looks like so today doesn't feel like a one-off event.

15:50–16:00, Close: Commitments (10 min)

Each rep writes one sentence: "The one thing I'll do differently in my very next call is ___." Collect digitally, manager follows up on this specific commitment in the next 1:1.

Materials checklist

  • Methodology one-pager (pre-work)
  • Recorded leadership "why now" video
  • Deal Triage Workshop scoring sheet
  • Hostile-persona role-play cards (8–10 personas)
  • Objection cards (20+)
  • 3 recorded calls for manager calibration
  • 2 anonymized live pipeline deals for the group review
  • Certification exam logistics slide + booking sheet
  • Commitment-capture form (digital or paper)

Post-SKO 30-60-90 reinforcement plan

This is what makes it stick. Most SKO content dies within two weeks without it.

Days 1–30, Embed

  • Week 1: manager reviews each rep's "one thing" commitment in 1:1.
  • Weekly 15-minute team huddle: one rep scores a live deal against the framework in front of the group.
  • CRM fields required for the methodology's stages go live, not optional.
  • Certification exams sat by day 21.

Days 31–60: Calibrate

  • Manager-led deal reviews require framework language, no exceptions.
  • Skip-level spot check: second-line manager sits in on one deal review per manager.
  • Mid-point pulse survey (1 question, anonymous): "Are you using this without being asked?"

Days 61–90, Prove it

  • Compare cohort win rate and cycle time (deals worked entirely post-SKO) against the trailing-12-month baseline.
  • Recognize the rep who applied the framework most rigorously, not just the one who won the most dollars. Reinforce the behavior, not only the outcome.
  • Retire the pre-methodology CRM fields entirely. No option to fall back to the old way.

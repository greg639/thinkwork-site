02 / How a turnover works

Check-out to guest-ready.

Six steps, every time. Same order, same checklist, so you are not wondering what happened between guests.

  1. 01

    Check-out

    The guest leaves. We let ourselves in with the spare key and walk the property.

    Status: arrived
  2. 02

    Condition check

    Every room photographed. Damage, wear and anything missing gets logged against your baseline.

    Status: logged
  3. 03

    Clean + reset

    Beds made, bathrooms and kitchen cleaned, linen changed, the place set up the way your guests expect.

    Status: reset
  4. 04

    Inventory + restock

    Towels, crockery and consumables counted and topped up. If something is short or broken, you hear.

    Status: counted
  5. 05

    Fix what's broken

    Dripping tap, loose handle, dead bulb. Small repairs done while we are there, bigger ones quoted.

    Status: fixed
  6. 06

    Guest-ready

    Alarms tested, key back where it lives, report and photos in your portal. Stamped.

    Status: guest-ready

Your own sequence is set at the free walk-through: we build the inventory and a custom to-do list for your property, then quote.

10 / Straight answers

Asked and answered.

Q01What is a turnover?

A turnover (also called a changeover or turnaround) is everything that has to happen between one guest checking out and the next checking in: checking the property, cleaning it, resetting the beds and restocking, so it is guest-ready. We do turnovers for holiday lets in Eastbourne and Bexhill.

Q02What do you do in a changeover?

We check the property against your inventory, photograph its condition, clean, change and make the beds, restock consumables, test the smoke and CO alarms, deal with small repairs and put the key back where it lives. You see the checklist ticked off in your owner portal. Your own checklist is built at the free walk-through.

Q03Can you hold keys?

Yes. Key holding is included with Priority Club membership: we hold a spare set so we can get in for turnovers and be there if a guest is locked out. Prefer a key safe or a smart lock code? We work with those too. How keys are tagged, stored and signed out is agreed with you at the walk-through.

Q04What areas do you cover?

For now, Eastbourne and Bexhill: postcode districts BN20 to BN24, TN39 and TN40. Use the postcode checker on this page. If you are outside those districts, join the list and we will tell you when we reach you.

Q05How are you charged?

Everything is on the rates list on this page: holiday-let changeovers are priced by bedroom count, add-ons such as linen and oven cleans are priced separately, end of tenancy and void work is priced by bedroom count, and handyman work by the hour, half-day or full day. Priority Club members, who pay a monthly fee per property with a six-month minimum, get lower rates. Short-let care is firmed up after the free walk-through. draft rate, owner to confirm

Q06What if a guest is locked out?

For Priority Club members, they call us and, because we hold a spare set, we come and let them in or talk them through the key safe or smart lock. Guests of hosts without membership should call a locksmith. Call-out hours are agreed when you sign up. [owner to confirm call-out hours]

Q07Do you work on weekends?

Holiday lets change over every day of the week, so weekend turnovers are part of the job. Exact days and any out-of-hours cover are agreed at your walk-through. [owner to confirm weekend availability]

Q08Do you do compliance paperwork for me?

We keep a record of when your gas safety record, electrical checks, PAT testing, smoke and CO alarms and fire safety checks fall due, and remind you before they lapse. We test alarms at each visit. The certificates themselves come from the qualified engineers who issue them, and what applies depends on how you let the property, so confirm requirements with your council, the fire service and your insurer.

Q09Do you work for people who are not hosts?

Yes. The handyman and decorating side is open to anyone in Eastbourne and Bexhill: leaks and tap repairs, doors and locks, fencing and gates, flat-pack and fixings, painting and decorating, plastering touch-ups, sealant and odd jobs. Send photos and get a quote.

Q10Do you do end of tenancy and void work?

Yes. End of tenancy and void work is a third service line: end of tenancy cleans, void repairs, reinstatement and a final check, priced by bedroom count. Full refurbs and major works are quoted separately.

Sales Mindset Podcast Directory

20 real podcasts, sorted by the specific mental-game problem they solve — rejection, imposter syndrome, burnout, money mindset, comparison, and more — so a rep's commute actually rebuilds their head instead of just filling the silence.

What's inside

  • 20 podcasts organized into 9 mental-game problem categories
  • Rejection & call reluctance shows
  • Confidence & imposter syndrome shows
  • Burnout & resilience shows
  • Money mindset shows
  • Comparison, ego, and long-game shows
  • A manager-specific category for leadership mindset
  • A "how to actually use this" listening protocol, not just a list

Podcast feeds and episode numbering change constantly — search each show's own archive for the themes below rather than relying on a specific episode number, which can shift or get renumbered after this was written. Every show listed here is an established, ongoing series a rep can subscribe to directly.

How to Use This

Don't hand a rep the whole list. Diagnose the specific mental-game problem first (use the Limiting-Belief Reframe Worksheet or the Mindset 1:1 Coaching Script to find it), then assign one category. Debrief afterward with one question: "What's one line from that episode you'd actually say out loud on a call?" — that turns passive listening into something usable.

Rejection & Call Reluctance

  1. Sales Gravy Podcast (Jeb Blount) — deep, recurring archive on rejection, cold call anxiety, and objection handling; search the feed for episodes tagged "rejection" or "cold calling mindset."
  2. The Salesman Podcast (Will Barron) — one of the largest sales interview archives; search for episodes on call reluctance and fear of the phone.
  3. The Sales Evangelist (Donald Kelly) — strong on first-call anxiety for newer reps specifically; look for episodes framed around "overcoming fear."

Confidence & Imposter Syndrome

  1. 30 Minutes to President's Club (Nick Cegelski & Armand Farrokh) — tactical but frequently addresses the confidence gap between what a rep knows and what they say out loud; search for episodes on "confidence" or "tonality."
  2. Modern Selling (Mario Martinez Jr.) — episodes on personal branding and showing up with authority even early in tenure.
  3. The Mindset Mentor (Rob Dial) — not sales-specific, but its imposter-syndrome and self-doubt episodes translate directly to a rep's first year.

Burnout & Resilience

  1. The Brutal Truth About Sales & Selling (Brian Burns / Ray Makela) — unusually candid about the psychological toll of the job; search for episodes on burnout and long-term sustainability.
  2. The Ziglar Show (Tom Ziglar) — legacy show built on resilience and "attitude" as a practiced skill, not a personality trait.
  3. The Tim Ferriss Show — not sales-specific, but its episodes with high performers on recovery, rest, and sustainable output are directly useful for reps burning the candle too fast.

Money Mindset

  1. Sell or Die (Jeffrey Gitomer & Jennifer Gitomer) — direct, repeated focus on the psychology of asking for money and charging what you're worth.
  2. The Six-Figure Sales Coach (Nikki Rausch) — strong on the psychology underneath pricing conversations and self-worth tied to income.

Comparison, Ego & the Long Game

  1. Sales Success Stories (Scott Ingram) — interviews with individual top performers; useful specifically for showing reps that "overnight success" always has a multi-year story underneath it, killing the comparison trap.
  2. Same Side Selling Podcast (Ian Altman) — reframes competition and ego away from beating the prospect toward genuinely serving them, useful for reps stuck in a scarcity/rival mindset.
  3. Sales Influence (Victor Antonio) — psychology-of-influence angle that helps reps separate their ego from the outcome of a single call.

Discipline & Consistency

  1. Make It Happen Mondays (John Barrows) — built around weekly reset and consistency, good as a literal Monday-morning listen.
  2. The Advanced Selling Podcast (Bill Caskey & Bryan Neale) — long-running focus on self-talk and the internal narrative that drives (or kills) daily discipline.

Business & Mental Toughness (broader than sales, still directly useful)

  1. The Game with Alex Hormozi — blunt, high-volume content on mental toughness and reframing setbacks as data.
  2. The GaryVee Audio Experience (Gary Vaynerchuk) — useful for reps who respond to high-energy, direct-confrontation-of-excuses framing.

For Managers — Leadership Mindset

  1. Predictable Revenue Podcast — manager- and leader-facing episodes on building resilient teams, not just individual reps.
  2. EntreLeadership (Ramsey) — leadership-specific mindset content on building culture and coaching through adversity, useful for the manager listening on their own commute, not just assigning to reps.

Listening Protocol (attach this to every assignment)

  • Assign one episode topic, matched to a diagnosed problem — not the whole feed.
  • Ask the rep to text one line or idea from the episode that landed, same day.
  • In the next 1:1, ask: "Did you use that idea on a call yet? What happened?"
  • Rotate categories monthly so the same problem doesn't get over-indexed while others go unaddressed.

How to use it

Match the rep's current mental-game problem to a category (not just their role), assign one show for the week, and debrief with one question: "what's one line from that episode you'd actually say out loud on a call."

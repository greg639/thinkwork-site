New-Hire Sales Ramp Plan (30/60/90-Day Template)

Week-by-week onboarding milestones anchored to named competency levels, not just activity quotas, so ramp actually means skill growth.

How to use it

Give the new hire and their manager the checklist on day one, score the scorecard together at each 30-day checkpoint, and use the Week 12 competency levels (not activity numbers) as the actual ramp-graduation gate.

What's inside

  • A 4-level competency scale definition: Foundational, Developing, Proficient, Advanced
  • 6 tracked competencies: Product & Value Narrative, Discovery & Qualification, Objection Handling, Negotiation, Pipeline/Forecast Management, Territory/Account Planning
  • Week-by-week checklist for Days 1-30 with Checkpoint 1

New-Hire Sales Ramp Plan: 30/60/90 Day Template

Anchored to named competency levels, not just activity quotas, so "ramped" means the rep can actually do the job, not just that they made enough calls.

Competency levels (use across every skill below)

  • L1, Foundational: Can explain the concept; hasn't executed it live yet.
  • L2, Developing: Can execute with a script/support; inconsistent results.
  • L3, Proficient: Executes independently with consistent results.
  • L4, Advanced: Adapts to novel situations; could coach a peer.

Competencies tracked

Product & Value Narrative · Discovery & Qualification · Objection Handling · Negotiation · Pipeline/Forecast Management · Territory/Account Planning

Days 1–30 (Weeks 1–4): Target: L1 across the board, L2 on Product & Value Narrative

  • Week 1: Systems/tools setup complete; shadow 3 live calls; complete Product Certification Module 1
  • Week 2: Complete full product certification; shadow 5 more calls including discovery calls; first mock discovery call with manager
  • Week 3: First live discovery call (manager silent-listens); draft initial territory/account plan
  • Week 4: Checkpoint 1: Manager scores each competency (L1/L2); account plan finalized; first outbound sequence live

Days 31–60 (Weeks 5–8): Target: L2 on Discovery & Objection Handling, first live deals building

  • Week 5: First full discovery call run solo; objection-handling roleplay lab
  • Week 6: First proposal/demo delivered solo (manager shadows); negotiation fundamentals training
  • Week 7: First real negotiation conversation, manager-coached beforehand
  • Week 8: Checkpoint 2: Manager re-scores each competency; pipeline coverage check against ramped quota; mid-point 1:1 with skip-level manager

Days 61–90 (Weeks 9–13): Target: L3 on Discovery & Objection Handling, L2 on Negotiation, first closed-won

  • Week 9: Full quota-carrying activity cadence in place; shadow a peer's negotiation call
  • Week 10: First deal reaches late-stage/negotiation solo
  • Week 11: Push toward first closed-won deal
  • Week 12: Checkpoint 3 (Ramp Review): Must reach L3 on Discovery & Qualification and Objection Handling to be declared "ramped": activity volume alone does not graduate a rep
  • Week 13: Ramp graduation review with manager + enablement; set next-quarter development goals toward L4

Manager's note

Track call volume, meetings booked, and pipeline generated throughout, but they are not the ramp gate. A rep can hit every activity number and still not be ramped if they can't run a discovery call independently at a consistent standard. The competency scorecard above is what determines graduation from ramp, not the activity dashboard.

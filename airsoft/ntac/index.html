Straight answers

Questions people type into a search box

How old do you have to be to play at NTAC Airsoft?
Eight and over. Players aged 8 to 11 play alongside a family member; the minimum age to be dropped off is 12. Anyone under 18 needs a guardian over 18 to sign the paperwork.
How much does a day at NTAC Airsoft cost?
Walk-on with your own kit is £20 for a full day or £10 for a half day. Hire is £40 for a full day or £30 for a half day, game fee included. Members pay £10 and £5 to walk on, or £30 for a full day of hire.
Do I need to be a member?
No. Walk-ons are £20 a day. Membership takes £10 off every full day and renews at £30 for a year; joining is done on site with a photo for the ID card. Joining online, photo included, is proposed.
When is NTAC Airsoft open?
Every Sunday. Gates open at 08:00, FPS testing at 09:30, the safety brief at 09:45, games from 10:00 till 16:00 with lunch at 12:30.
What does hire kit include?
An M4 and sling, two magazines, a face mask, a camo jacket, a tac-vest and 3,300 rounds of ammo, with the game fee included. Players must book hire kit online or may miss out.
What are the chrono limits at NTAC Airsoft?
350fps for full auto on 0.20g to 0.28g, DMR locked at 420fps, and up to 500fps for single-shot snipers on 0.20g to 0.46g with a 25 metre engagement rule. Three chronos are used on the day.
Do you do HPA refills?
Yes, £1.50 per 1kg bottle for game day players. Battery charging is free.
Is there food?
The tuck shop on site has hot food and snacks, or bring your own. Tea, coffee and bottled water are free. There are toilets on site.
How do I pay?
Today: book online and pay on the day, cash or card. Proposed: pay by card online when you book, and your place, your hire kit and your membership are all sorted before Sunday.
Where is NTAC Airsoft?
Quarry House Woods, Elemore Lane, Pittington, Durham, DH6 1QQ. Phone 07944 312532.
Can I hire the site for a party or team building?
Yes. Private hire is £40 a player for 15 to 50 players, with a £50 non-refundable deposit and at least 14 days' notice. Stag and hen, birthdays, film shoots and team building. A quote on screen with the deposit button is proposed to replace "just email for all details".
Are there night games?
Yes, 17:00 to 23:55. Halloween and Bonfire night run on the Friday evening with five hours of play, normal game day charges and a free BBQ.

AIEach question is phrased the way people type it, answered in the first sentence, and published as FAQPage structured data. Only the verified answers go into the structured data; the proposed parts are kept out of it so nothing an assistant quotes is invented.

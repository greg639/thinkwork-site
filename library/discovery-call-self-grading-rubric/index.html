Discovery Call Self-Grading Rubric

A rubric reps score themselves against immediately after hanging up: while the call is still fresh: to catch what worked, what to fix next time, and whether this deal is actually as qualified as it felt in the room.

How to use it

Fill this out within 15 minutes of ending any discovery call, score honestly against the behavioral anchors, and check the red-flag section before trusting your total score.

What's inside

  • 8 scored categories, 1-5 scale each
  • Behavioral anchors for score 1, 3, and 5 in every category
  • Total score interpretation bands
  • Red-flag override section
  • Self-coaching prompt for the lowest-scoring category

Fill this out within 15 minutes of ending the call, before you talk yourself into a better story than what actually happened.

1. Pre-Call Preparation

  • 1: Opened cold, no research, generic questions
  • 3: Reviewed company/contact basics, had a rough agenda
  • 5: Had specific hypotheses about their pain going in, tailored questions to their industry/role, knew who else was likely in the buying process

2. Opening & Agenda-Setting

  • 1: Jumped straight into pitching or asked "so tell me about your business"
  • 3: Set a basic agenda, got light buy-in
  • 5: Set a clear mutual agenda, confirmed time available, got explicit agreement on what a good outcome from this call looks like for them

3. Situation Questions

  • 1: Asked surface-level questions already answerable from their website
  • 3: Asked reasonable situation questions but mostly closed-ended
  • 5: Asked open situation questions that surfaced context I didn't already know, without feeling like an interrogation

4. Pain & Impact Questions

  • 1: Accepted the first pain mentioned at face value, didn't quantify
  • 3: Dug one layer deeper, got a rough sense of impact
  • 5: Quantified the pain in their own numbers/language, connected it to a business consequence they hadn't stated first

5. Qualification (Budget / Authority / Timeline / Process)

  • 1: Didn't ask, or asked so bluntly it created friction
  • 3: Got partial answers, some "I'll have to check"
  • 5: Got clear, specific answers on process and timeline, and a credible read on budget/authority without it feeling transactional

6. Listening & Talk Ratio

  • 1: I did most of the talking, pitched features unprompted
  • 3: Roughly balanced, some over-explaining
  • 5: They talked more than me, I used silence after questions, I referenced something they said earlier in a later question

7. Multi-Threading

  • 1: Didn't ask who else is involved
  • 3: Asked, got a vague answer, didn't push
  • 5: Got specific names/roles of at least 2 other stakeholders and a plan to reach them

8. Next Steps & Commitment

  • 1: Ended with "I'll follow up": no date, no mutual commitment
  • 3: Got a next call booked but no specific mutual actions
  • 5: Got a dated next step with named owners on both sides, and confirmed what needs to be true for this to keep moving

Total Score: ___ / 40

Interpretation Bands

  • 34-40: Strong discovery call: this deal's qualification data is trustworthy
  • 24-33: Solid but incomplete: identify the lowest 1-2 categories and address specifically before the next call
  • 14-23: Discovery gaps significant enough that "qualified" is optimistic: plan a deliberate recovery call
  • Below 14: Treat this as an information-gathering call only: do not advance the stage in CRM yet

Red-Flag Override (regardless of total score)

Circle any that apply: a single red flag caps your interpretation at "solid but incomplete" even with a high total:

  • I never got a name/role for anyone besides the person on the call
  • I could not restate their business pain in a single sentence without notes
  • There is no dated next step in the calendar
  • I talked more than they did

Self-Coaching Prompt

For your single lowest-scoring category, write one sentence:

"Next call, I will specifically ___."

Reread it before your next discovery call of the day.

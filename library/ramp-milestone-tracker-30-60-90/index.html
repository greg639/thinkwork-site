Ramp Milestone Tracker (30/60/90 Days)

One fill-in tracker per new hire mapping the specific milestones they need to hit at 30, 60, and 90 days across product knowledge, activity, skill, and pipeline — with clear escalation guidance when they slip.

What's inside

  • Fill-in header block per new hire
  • 30-day milestones across 4 categories
  • 60-day milestones across 4 categories
  • 90-day milestones across 4 categories
  • Status checkbox + notes line at each checkpoint
  • Escalation guidance for At Risk / Behind / Performance Concern outcomes

One copy of this tracker per new hire. Fill in the specific milestone for your org/segment where a range is given, then check off as they're hit. Review at each milestone date with the rep directly, not just internally.

30-Day Milestones

Product & Process Knowledge

  • Can explain the product/value proposition in their own words, unscripted
  • Has completed all required onboarding/certification modules
  • Knows the CRM well enough to log a call without help

Activity

  • Has shadowed at least 10 calls (see Call Shadowing Checklist)
  • Has taken at least ___ live discovery calls solo (fill in your target, e.g. 15)
  • Meeting weekly with mentor per the Buddy Program cadence

Skill Demonstration

  • Manager has listened to/reviewed at least 2 live or recorded calls
  • Can run the Universal Discovery Structure without a script in front of them
  • Has correctly logged CRM notes per the CRM Hygiene Cheat Sheet on every call this week

Pipeline

  • Has generated first ___ qualified opportunities (fill in target)
  • Pipeline stages in CRM accurately reflect deal reality (manager-verified)

60-Day Milestones

Product & Process Knowledge

  • Can handle at least 8 of the 12 objections from the Objection Handling Cheat Sheet without hesitating
  • Can name 2–3 relevant case studies/proof points for their most common deal type unprompted

Activity

  • Running discovery calls fully solo, no shadow needed
  • Weekly 1:1 cadence shifted to skill + pipeline mix (per Manager's 1:1 guide)
  • Mentor sessions moved to weeks 3–6 cadence (2x/week)

Skill Demonstration

  • Has run at least 1 full-cycle deal conversation from discovery through a next-stage advance without mentor/manager intervention
  • Sales Readiness Self-Assessment retaken — total score improved vs. Day 1 baseline
  • No section of the Self-Assessment scoring below 3.0 average

Pipeline

  • Pipeline value at or above cohort median for day 60 (see Time-to-First-Deal Tracker for benchmarks)
  • At least 1 deal in late-stage (proposal/negotiation) or closed

60-Day Status: ☐ On Track ☐ At Risk ☐ Behind — Notes: _______________

90-Day Milestones

Product & Process Knowledge

  • Fully independent on product positioning and competitive differentiation
  • Can mentor/help a newer hire on at least one skill area confidently

Activity

  • Full independent activity cadence matching tenured rep expectations for the segment
  • Buddy/Mentor pairing formally closed out (see Sales Buddy/Mentor Program Playbook end criteria)

Skill Demonstration

  • First deal closed-won (flag if not — this is the single biggest ramp risk signal; cross-check against Time-to-First-Deal benchmark)
  • CRM hygiene fully self-sufficient, zero manager reminders needed in the last 2 weeks
  • Sales Readiness Self-Assessment retaken again — total score ≥ 80/100 OR clear upward trend with a named remaining focus area

Pipeline

  • Pipeline coverage at or above segment target (e.g., 3x quota in pipeline)
  • Forecast accuracy for their own deals within acceptable variance (manager-defined)

90-Day Status: ☐ Ramped — Full Autonomy ☐ Ramped with Support ☐ Extend Ramp Plan ☐ Performance Concern — Escalate

What to Do at Each Review

  • If "On Track" at all three checkpoints: move to standard rep cadence, no special tracking needed after 90 days
  • If "At Risk" at 30 or 60 days: name the specific blocker together with the rep (skill, volume, territory, personal) and adjust — don't just note it and hope 60 fixes itself
  • If "Behind" at 60 days: escalate to a formal ramp-extension conversation before day 90, with a clear, written plan and a new checkpoint date
  • If "Performance Concern" at 90 days: this feeds directly into a documented performance conversation — pull the Cost of a Bad Sales Hire Calculator to inform how much runway makes sense to extend vs. not

How to use it

Create one copy per new hire at kickoff, fill in your org's specific numeric targets where marked, and check off milestones live during the 30/60/90-day review conversations.

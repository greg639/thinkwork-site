B. Opening Script Skeleton

  1. State your name and company clearly, no rushing.
  2. Acknowledge the interruption and ask for a specific, short amount of time ("Do you have 27 seconds?").
  3. Deliver the pattern-interrupt framing line - built from the specific trigger on the Planning Card, not a generic opener.
  4. Ask permission to ask one question before pitching anything.
  5. Ask the lead discovery question.

C. Top-8 Objection-Response Matrix (condensed)

ObjectionResponse Approach
"Send me some information"Acknowledge, ask one clarifying question to make the info genuinely relevant, offer a specific short call as the alternative
"I'm not interested"Acknowledge as a reflex, not a final answer; ask one low-commitment question tied to a likely pain point
"We already have a vendor"Acknowledge, ask what's working/not working with the current vendor rather than arguing against them
"I'm busy right now"Acknowledge, offer a specific callback window (not "whenever"), confirm before hanging up
"How did you get my number?"Answer honestly and briefly, pivot back to the reason for the call
"We don't have budget"Acknowledge, ask about timing/planning cycles rather than pushing on budget directly
"Just email me"Acknowledge, offer to email and propose a specific short call to make sure the email is relevant
"Not the right person"Thank them, ask for the correct name/contact, ask if you can mention who referred you

D. Discovery Question Bank (12 questions)

  1. Walk me through how your team handles [process] today.
  2. What's working well about that, and what isn't?
  3. How is that being measured today?
  4. Who else is affected when that process breaks down?
  5. What's the plan if nothing changes here in the next 6 months?
  6. What have you already tried to fix this?
  7. Who else would need to be involved in a decision here?
  8. What would "solved" actually look like for your team?
  9. How is this handled at [comparable team/department]?
  10. What's the cost of this staying the same for another quarter?
  11. Is this a priority right now, or later this year?
  12. What would make this worth revisiting in more depth?

E. Next-Step / Close Scripts (3 variants)

Variant 1 - Direct ask: "Based on what you've shared, I think a 20-minute call with [role] would be worth it. Does Tuesday at 10am or Thursday at 2pm work better?"

Variant 2 - Low-commitment bridge: "I don't want to take more of your time right now - would it be OK if I sent over [specific relevant resource] and followed up in a week?"

Variant 3 - Referral bridge: "It sounds like [colleague/department] might be the better fit for this conversation - would you be open to a quick intro?"

F. Compliance Quick-Reference (6 items - full checklist is a standalone resource)

  • List scrubbed against National DNC Registry within 31 days
  • Internal company DNC list checked before dialing
  • Calling within 8am-9pm recipient local time
  • Recording disclosure delivered before recording begins (if applicable)
  • Opt-out requests logged and honored same day
  • Rep has completed compliance training and is certified to dial

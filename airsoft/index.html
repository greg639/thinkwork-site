The AI part, in plain English

Players ask AI now. Make sure it knows you.

  • Found by AI

    More players ask ChatGPT or Google's AI where to play. Your site is built so those assistants can read it and point players to you.

  • Answered by AI

    A chat assistant on your site answers the questions you get every week. What to bring, the age limit, can I hire kit. It can take the booking too, at midnight if that's when they ask.

  • Built into it

    Where it saves you time, AI goes inside the software as well. Like drafting the game-day email, or spotting the regulars who haven't been back.

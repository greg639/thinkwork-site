Straight answers

Questions people type into a search box

Where is District 23?
Leeholme Road, Billingham, Stockton-on-Tees, TS23 3TA. Phone 07848 162614, email info@district23.co.uk.
When is District 23 open?
7 days, 10am to 10pm, dependent on bookings. Please call before visiting, as the arena only opens when events are booked.
How big is the District 23 arena?
The main airsoft arena is built over 17,000 sq. feet, housing buildings which recreate an American Midtown District, with plenty of blind corners and alleys, props including vehicles, and sky bridges.
How much does a session at District 23 cost?
Session prices show inside the FareHarbor window when you click Book Now.
How much is membership at District 23?
Memberships are £60 for 12 months, with discounts on field fees, retailer discounts, a club patch, member-only social events, free game perk offers and a free birthday session. Join by seeing staff on site or by email. Joining online is proposed.
What are the FPS limits at District 23?
0.20g at 350 fps, 0.25g at 313 fps and 0.28g at 296 fps. Only those three BB weights are allowed.
Do under-18s need full face protection at District 23?
Yes. Anyone under 18 must wear full face protection. Players 18 and over must wear approved eye protection. Minors aged 12 to 17 may be dropped off by a parent or guardian and left to play, with a contact phone number.
Can I rent a gun at District 23?
Yes. If you do not have your own gear and require an airsoft rental, choose a rental when you book. Pre-booking the rental online so it is on the rack when you arrive is proposed.
Do you do HPA refills at District 23?
Yes. Refills cost £1 per fill, and are free with a site membership.
Does District 23 do birthday parties?
Yes. District 23 hosts parties and business events, and describes itself as fun for all ages. A quote on screen with a deposit button is proposed.

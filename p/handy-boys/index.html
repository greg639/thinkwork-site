Concept for Handy Boys, Eastbourne and Bexhill

Your website and your back end.

Three directions for the website, and a working prototype of the platform behind it: your side, and a login for each property owner. Open each one and click around.

The website, three directions
A. Guest-ReadyPremium and calm. Reads like a boutique hotel's housekeeping team.
B. SeafrontWarm and local. Two doors: the holiday let, and the home.
C. WorkshopBold and graphic. Job tickets, hi-vis yellow, no mistaking the trade.
The platform, clickable prototype
Operator console and owner portalSwitch between your view and a property owner's view at the top. Calendar, inventories, checklists, quotes, compliance reminders, keys, retainers and invoices, plus a phone view for running a turnover. Tick something in the phone view, then switch to the owner portal to watch it appear.
Concept only. Photos are illustrations, all names and amounts in the platform are sample data, and prices are drafts for you to confirm. Nothing here takes payments or stores anything.

Sales Kickoff 12-Week Planning Checklist

A week-by-week runbook for planning a sales kickoff end to end, covering theme, logistics, content, and post-event reinforcement, so nothing falls through the cracks.

What's inside

  • 12 pre-event weekly checklists from T-minus 12 to T-minus 1
  • An event-week checklist covering execution, recording, and live feedback
  • 5 post-event checklists at Week 1, 2, 4, 8, and 12 covering reinforcement and ROI review
  • Explicit checkbox items for theme/big-rocks definition, budget, and objectives
  • Logistics items: venue, travel, AV, swag, run-of-show, contingency planning
  • Content items: agenda drafts, rehearsals, speaker confirmation, breakout materials
  • Reinforcement items: manager debriefs, CRM goal translation, skill-check checkpoints, ROI review

A week-by-week runbook from kickoff-of-planning to twelve weeks post-event, covering theme, logistics, content, and reinforcement, so nothing falls through the cracks.

T-Minus 12 Weeks (Planning Start)

  • Confirm SKO dates and venue
  • Set total budget (see Budget & ROI Calculator in this library)
  • Assign SKO owner + cross-functional planning committee (Sales, Enablement, Marketing, Events)
  • Define the theme and the 1–2 "big rocks" this SKO must land — not a laundry list

T-Minus 11 Weeks

  • Draft measurable objectives (e.g., target ramp-time reduction, named skill certifications to complete)
  • Align theme/narrative with CEO/CRO

T-Minus 10 Weeks

  • Finalize venue contract, travel logistics, room blocks
  • Negotiate AV and catering contracts
  • Confirm attendee list and roles (reps, CS, marketing, leadership, guests)

T-Minus 9 Weeks

  • Build the content skeleton — map sessions to Vision / Skill-Building / Celebration days
  • Identify internal vs. external speakers needed per session

T-Minus 8 Weeks

  • Send save-the-date + any pre-work (pre-read, self-assessment, pre-SKO survey)
  • Begin external speaker confirmations

T-Minus 7 Weeks

  • Lock agenda draft v1
  • Assign session owners
  • Begin deck/material development

T-Minus 6 Weeks

  • Finalize room blocks, ground transport, AV run-of-show draft
  • Place swag/merch orders (lead times run long — don't leave this late)

T-Minus 5 Weeks

  • Set content rehearsal schedule
  • Draft breakout/workshop materials
  • Design gamification/competition mechanics if used

T-Minus 4 Weeks

  • Full run-of-show v2 complete
  • Confirm all speakers and vendors in writing
  • Finalize award/recognition list
  • Begin attendee comms cadence

T-Minus 3 Weeks

  • Dry-run rehearsal with all internal speakers
  • Complete AV/tech checks
  • Order printed materials and name badges

T-Minus 2 Weeks

  • Final logistics confirmation — headcounts, dietary needs, transport manifests
  • Build contingency plan (no-shows, AV failure, weather)
  • Draft the post-event reinforcement plan (30/60/90-day cadence)

T-Minus 1 Week

  • On-site setup begins
  • Final rehearsal
  • Send attendees final logistics details
  • Brief all facilitators/session owners

Event Week

  • Execute Day 1 (Vision), Day 2 (Skill-Building), Day 3 (Celebration) — see the 3-Day Agenda Builder in this library
  • Record all sessions for post-event reference
  • Run a live feedback pulse survey (end of Day 2 or Day 3)

Post-Event Week 1

  • Send recap email with recordings and materials
  • Distribute playbooks/session assets
  • Compile and share feedback/NPS results with leadership

Post-Event Week 2

  • Managers run team debriefs
  • Translate SKO commitments into individual/team goals in CRM

Post-Event Week 4

  • First skill-check/certification checkpoint on key SKO training
  • Identify and address gaps

Post-Event Week 8

  • Mid-point reinforcement pulse — is the new play/skill actually being used?
  • Tie findings into manager 1:1 coaching

Post-Event Week 12

  • Full ROI review — attainment/ramp movement vs. pre-SKO baseline
  • Feed lessons learned into next SKO's planning cycle

How to use it

Assign each weekly block to a named owner on the planning committee at T-minus 12, and check items off in a shared tracker so the committee can see at a glance which week is behind.

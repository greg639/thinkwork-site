Deal Risk Red-Flag Checklist

12 warning signs that predict a deal will slip or die, with a scoring threshold and an immediate next action for each — so a rep catches the problem in week two instead of week twelve.

What's inside

  • Single-threaded contact flag
  • Champion gone quiet 5+ business days flag
  • No economic buyer meeting flag
  • Rep-proposed (not buyer-stated) timeline flag
  • No compelling event flag
  • 'Just waiting to hear back' with no date flag
  • Legal/security/procurement not yet engaged flag
  • Champion can't name the approval chain flag
  • Unconfirmed 'budget not an issue' flag
  • Meetings being rescheduled or shortened flag
  • Scoring bands (0-1 healthy, 2-3 at risk, 4+ likely to slip) with immediate actions per flag

12 warning signs that predict a deal will slip or die

Check any that apply to a deal you're working right now. Two or more checked = high risk of slip. Catch it in week two, not week twelve.

  1. ☐ Single-threaded contact. You have a real relationship with exactly one person at the account, and no other contact would take your call if that person left tomorrow.
  2. ☐ Champion has gone quiet for 5+ business days after previously being responsive, with no calendar item scheduled.
  3. ☐ No economic buyer meeting has happened. You've never been in a room (or on a call) with the person who actually signs, only heard about them secondhand.
  4. ☐ The timeline is yours, not theirs. The close date on the deal was proposed by you, not stated independently by the buyer.
  5. ☐ No compelling event. Nothing costs the buyer money, time, or risk if they simply do nothing this quarter.
  6. ☐ "Just waiting to hear back" for more than one week with no specific date given for when you'll hear back.
  7. ☐ Legal/security/procurement has not engaged, despite a close date inside the current stated close window.
  8. ☐ The champion can't name who else has to approve it, or gives a vague answer like "it should just need a signature."
  9. ☐ Budget was described as "not an issue" but no one can point to a line item, PO, or approved spend category.
  10. ☐ Meetings are getting rescheduled or shortened, especially by the buyer side, especially more than once.
  11. ☐ The buyer's internal champion is junior relative to the deal size and has never successfully sold anything similar upward before.
  12. ☐ No mutual action plan exists — there is no document, however informal, that both sides agreed to with dates and owners.

Scoring

  • 0–1 checked: Healthy. Proceed on current timeline.
  • 2–3 checked: At risk. Address the specific flags this week — don't wait for next review.
  • 4+ checked: Likely to slip or die this period. Requalify from the top: is there really a deal here, or is this an opportunity that should be re-worked as prospecting?

What to do the moment you check a box (don't wait for the weekly review)

  • Single-threaded / no economic buyer access → ask your champion directly: "Who else should be in the next call, and can you set it up?" If they can't or won't, that itself is diagnostic.
  • Gone quiet → use the companion Stalled Deal Revival Playbook rather than sending another "just checking in."
  • No compelling event → go back to discovery. A deal without urgency isn't a Q-this-quarter deal — recategorize it honestly.
  • No mutual action plan → propose one in writing today, even a simple three-line email: "Here's what I understand still needs to happen and by when — did I get that right?"

How to use it

Run this against any deal in your pipeline, especially anything that's been in the same stage for more than two weeks — two or more checked boxes means intervene now, not at the next forecast call.

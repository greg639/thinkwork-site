Weekly Prospecting Activity Scorecard

The leading-indicator tracker — touches, connects, and meetings booked, broken out by channel — built to catch a slump 2–3 weeks before it shows up in pipeline.

What's inside

  • Fill-in weekly table: Email, LinkedIn, Calls, Video, Combined outcomes
  • Starting-point benchmark ranges per channel (calibrate to your own data)
  • Red-flag threshold for each metric
  • 4 diagnostic red-flag patterns (volume-fine-but-zero-replies, etc.)
  • 5 weekly 1:1 review questions tied directly to the scorecard
  • Guidance on how many weeks of data before a trend is real

Activity volume tells you effort. This scorecard tells you where in the funnel that effort is breaking down — which is the thing that actually predicts next month's pipeline, not this week's dial count alone.

Red-flag diagnostic patterns

Use these to turn a bad number into a specific fix, not a vague "try harder":

  1. Volume on track, replies flat for 2+ consecutive weeks → messaging problem, not a volume problem. Run drafts through the Cold Email Copy Review Scorecard.
  2. High reply rate, zero meetings booked → CTA problem. The reply ask isn't converting to a booked slot.
  3. High connect rate on calls, low conversation-to-advance rate → opener/script problem, not a dialing problem.
  4. Meetings booked but no-show rate above 20% → qualification or confirmation problem, not a scheduling one.

Weekly 1:1 review questions

  1. Which row is furthest below target this week, and is that the first week or the third?
  2. Where in the funnel did the drop happen — sent, opened, replied, or booked?
  3. What's one thing you changed this week, and what did it do to the numbers?
  4. Is this a volume problem, a messaging problem, or a targeting problem — pick one.
  5. What's the one row you're going to move next week, and how?

Trend rule: don't act on a single bad week — one week of data is noise. Two consecutive weeks below target on the same row is a real signal worth a coaching conversation.

How to use it

Fill in the table every Friday from the sequencing tool and dialer logs, compare each row against its benchmark and red-flag line, then walk into the weekly 1:1 with the specific row that's off, not a general "how's prospecting going."

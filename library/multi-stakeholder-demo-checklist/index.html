Multi-Stakeholder Demo Checklist

Ensures a demo with five attendees speaks to all five roles instead of just the loudest voice in the room — stakeholder mapping worksheet, role-specific checklist, mid-demo check-ins, and post-demo follow-up.

What's inside

  • Pre-demo stakeholder mapping worksheet (name, role type, what they care about, risk if ignored)
  • Role-specific checklist items for Economic Buyer, Champion, Technical Evaluator, End User, and Skeptic/Blocker
  • Procurement/Legal checklist items when they're present
  • Word-for-word mid-demo "temperature check" script, one line per role
  • Post-demo stakeholder-specific follow-up checklist

A demo with 5 attendees and one narrative aimed at the loudest person in the room loses 4 of them. This checklist ensures every role gets addressed by name, not just implied.

Pre-demo stakeholder mapping worksheet

Fill this in before the meeting, using discovery notes and the invite list.

NameRole typeWhat they specifically care aboutRisk if ignored in the demo
Economic BuyerFeels it's "just a tool demo," no business case → stalls at approval
Champion/UserFeels unheard, disengages, stops internally selling for you
Technical EvaluatorUnanswered technical risk becomes a veto later
End UserSees no personal upside, becomes quiet resistance during rollout
Skeptic/BlockerObjection goes unaddressed and surfaces after the call, out of your sight

Role-specific checklist

Economic Buyer

  • Addressed them by name at least once with a business-outcome statement (cost, revenue, risk)
  • Showed or referenced a quantified ROI/business case, not just features
  • Confirmed their specific approval process and timeline out loud
  • Did not let the demo run past their stated available time

Champion/User

  • Opened with their specific pain point, in their own words
  • Gave them a moment in the demo to react or ask a question, not just observe
  • Confirmed they have what they need to sell this internally after the call
  • Asked directly what would help them advocate for this with the rest of the room

Technical Evaluator

  • Covered architecture, security, or integration specifically, even briefly
  • Did not guess on a technical question — offered a precise follow-up instead
  • Offered a dedicated technical deep-dive session as a next step
  • Addressed any stated technical constraint from discovery by name

End User

  • Showed the actual day-to-day workflow they'd use, not just admin/reporting views
  • Framed at least one outcome in terms of their daily experience getting easier, not just organizational metrics
  • Gave them room to ask a "dumb question" without it being brushed past

Skeptic/Blocker

  • Named their likely objection proactively rather than waiting for them to raise it
  • Did not talk over or dismiss any pushback from them
  • Followed the Listen-Acknowledge-Explore-Respond sequence if they objected
  • Left the call having addressed their specific concern, even if not fully resolved

Procurement/Legal (if present)

  • Confirmed contracting/security review process and typical timeline
  • Did not treat their presence as purely administrative — asked what they need to see
  • Offered relevant documentation (security, compliance, DPA) proactively

Mid-demo "temperature check" script

Use these once each, by name, at natural breakpoints — not all in a row.

  • To the Economic Buyer: "[Name], does the math we just walked through match how you'd want to build the business case internally?"
  • To the Champion: "[Name], is this matching what you were hoping I'd show today?"
  • To the Technical Evaluator: "[Name], anything here raising a flag from an architecture or security standpoint?"
  • To the End User: "[Name], if you were using this day to day, does that feel intuitive, or is there something that'd trip you up?"
  • To the Skeptic: "[Name], I know you had a concern about [X] — does what we just covered address that, or is there more to unpack?"

Post-demo stakeholder-specific follow-up checklist

  • Sent the Economic Buyer the ROI/business case in writing within 24 hours
  • Sent the Champion a summary they can forward internally, in their language
  • Scheduled the Technical Evaluator's deep-dive session with a specific date
  • Sent the End User any hands-on trial/sandbox access if available
  • Followed up individually with the Skeptic on their specific unresolved concern
  • Sent Procurement/Legal any requested documentation without being chased for it

How to use it

Fill in the stakeholder worksheet before the meeting, run the role-specific checklist items during the demo itself, use the temperature-check script once per named attendee at natural pauses, and complete the tailored follow-up checklist within 24 hours after the call.

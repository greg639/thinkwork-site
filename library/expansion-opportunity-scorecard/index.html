Expansion Opportunity Scorecard

Rank every account's expansion potential on one scorecard so managers know exactly where to point limited AM time and quota.

What's inside

  • 6 scoring dimensions: usage trend, whitespace size, champion strength, budget cycle timing, executive sponsorship, referenceability
  • 1-5 scoring scale with anchor descriptions per dimension
  • Weighted scoring formula producing a single expansion score
  • Rank-ordered account list template
  • Tiering guidance: Now / Next Quarter / Nurture
  • Time-allocation guidance by tier

Score every account in your book on the 6 dimensions below, 1-5 each. Multiply by weight, sum for a total expansion score out of 100. Re-rank the whole book quarterly.

Tiering

  • 50-70: Now - active expansion motion this quarter, top priority for AM time
  • 30-49: Next Quarter - build access/champion strength this quarter, execute next
  • Below 30: Nurture - light-touch relationship building only, revisit score next quarter

Time Allocation Guidance

Allocate AM expansion-focused time roughly in proportion to tier: 60% of expansion hours to "Now" accounts, 30% to "Next Quarter" accounts building toward readiness, 10% to "Nurture" accounts to keep the relationship warm without over-investing.

How to use it

Score every account in the book quarterly on the 6 weighted dimensions, rank by total score into the template, and allocate AM expansion time by tier - heaviest on Now, lightest on Nurture.

Objection Handling Certification Rubric (Bronze / Silver / Gold)

An S-SEA-style, competency-tiered mastery rubric with observable behavioral anchors across seven dimensions, used to certify reps into Bronze, Silver, or Gold objection-handling tiers.

What's inside

  • 7 scored competency dimensions
  • Bronze tier behavioral anchors across all 7 dimensions
  • Silver tier behavioral anchors across all 7 dimensions
  • Gold tier behavioral anchors across all 7 dimensions (including coaching capability)
  • 1–5 scoring scale with generic anchors
  • Assessment method and required evidence per tier
  • Certification thresholds (minimum averages plus floor rules)
  • Recertification cadence
  • Printable scorecard grid

Seven scored dimensions, three tiers. A rep's tier is determined by the LOWEST dimension score that clears the floor for that tier — one weak dimension caps the whole certification.

The 7 Competency Dimensions

  1. Acknowledgment & Rapport
  2. Diagnostic Questioning (isolating the real objection)
  3. Reframe & Value Articulation
  4. Proof & Evidence Use
  5. Close / Commitment
  6. Objection Prevention
  7. Recovery (when an objection resurfaces)

Scoring Scale (applied to every dimension)

ScoreAnchor
1Not demonstrated
2Emerging / inconsistent
3Meets bar reliably
4Strong / adapts to context
5Expert / teaches others

Bronze — Foundational (safe for unsupervised live calls)

DimensionBehavioral anchor
AcknowledgmentConsistently acknowledges without arguing or getting defensive; may sound scripted
Diagnostic QuestioningAsks at least one clarifying question before responding; doesn't always isolate the true root objection
ReframeUses a memorized reframe correctly matched to the objection family
ProofReferences at least one proof point from memory when prompted
CloseAttempts a trial close after responding, though timing may be off
PreventionNot yet demonstrated — reactive only
RecoveryRepeats the same rebuttal if the objection resurfaces

Assessment: 5 recorded/live calls reviewed + certification roleplay (5 unscripted objections). Threshold: average ≥3.0, no dimension below 2.

Silver — Proficient (fluent across all 6 objection families, adapts in real time)

DimensionBehavioral anchor
AcknowledgmentFeels natural and conversational, varies phrasing to context
Diagnostic QuestioningConsistently distinguishes the stated objection from the real one before responding
ReframeAdapts the reframe to the specific prospect rather than reciting a script
ProofSelects the MOST relevant proof point unprompted
CloseTrial-closes at the right moment and correctly reads whether it's resolved
PreventionProactively defuses at least one likely objection before it's raised (voicemail/email/early call)
RecoveryAdapts approach with a new angle or proof when the objection resurfaces

Assessment: 8 recorded calls (rep-selected worst-case examples) + peer panel review + certification roleplay (8 objections, 2 atypical "curveballs"). Threshold: average ≥4.0, no dimension below 3, at least 1 documented real-call prevention instance.

Gold — Expert (sets the standard, coaches others, handles enterprise/high-stakes deals)

DimensionBehavioral anchor
AcknowledgmentHandles hostile/adversarial objections (legal, procurement) without losing rapport or control
Diagnostic QuestioningUncovers objections the prospect never verbalized ("silent objections") through pattern recognition
ReframeBuilds bespoke reframes on the fly for objections outside the standard 6 families
ProofCreates new proof assets from own deals for team-wide reuse
CloseConverts unresolved objections into a committed next step even in multi-stakeholder deals
PreventionBuilds prevention material used by others; measurably lowers objection frequency in own pipeline
RecoveryRevives previously "lost" deals at a rate above team average (see Deal Autopsy Checklist)
Coaching (Gold only)Can grade a Bronze/Silver roleplay with ≥90% inter-rater agreement with a certified assessor

Assessment: full deal review (3 closed-won + 3 closed-lost with objections annotated) + live shadow of 2 real high-stakes calls + calibration session with 2 existing Gold assessors. Threshold: average ≥4.5, no dimension below 4, passes calibration check.

Recertification Cadence

  • Bronze → reassessed at 90 days
  • Silver → reassessed at 6 months
  • Gold → annual, plus spot recalibration

How to use it

Score live or recorded calls against each of the 7 dimensions using the scorecard grid, apply the tier thresholds to determine Bronze/Silver/Gold eligibility, and schedule recertification on the stated cadence rather than certifying once and forgetting.

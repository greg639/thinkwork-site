Where to Find Sales Talent: 40 Sourcing Communities & Boards

A vetted, categorized list of 40 places to find sales talent beyond LinkedIn — niche job boards, practitioner communities, and diversity-focused networks — organized by who you're hiring and what each one is actually good for.

What's inside

  • 40 named sources across 8 categories: job boards, SDR/BDR pipelines, leadership communities, trainer alumni networks, diversity-focused networks, veteran/military transition, ecosystem-specific, and regional/local
  • A 'best for' use-case note on every single entry
  • Dedicated diversity-focused and veteran-hiring sections (12 sources total)
  • Guidance on picking 3–4 sources per role instead of running all 40 at once

A note before you start: communities and boards change fast — membership models, activity levels, and focus shift. Treat this as a starting map to explore this quarter, not a permanent source of truth; verify current activity before committing real recruiting budget or time to any one channel.

A. General & Startup/Tech Job Boards

  1. RepVue — sales-specific job board built on org transparency/comp reviews; best for AE and management roles where candidates want to vet the comp plan and culture before applying.
  2. Bravado / Canary — sales professional network with an integrated talent marketplace; best for warm, community-sourced candidates rather than cold job-board traffic.
  3. SalesHeads.com — long-running, sales-only job board; best as a low-cost, high-volume posting channel for AE/SDR/sales-management roles.
  4. Built In (city-specific: NYC, Chicago, Boston, LA, Austin, Seattle) — best for startup/tech sales roles where local ecosystem visibility matters.
  5. Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) — best for early-stage startup AE/SDR hires comfortable with startup risk and equity comp.
  6. Y Combinator's Work at a Startup — best for YC-network startups hiring first or second sales hires.

B. Early-Career / SDR-BDR Pipelines

  1. Vendition — SDR apprenticeship and placement program that trains and places entry-level sales talent; best when you need trained SDRs without building the ramp program yourself.
  2. SDR Nation — practitioner community focused specifically on SDR/BDR career development; best for sourcing ambitious, coachable early-career candidates.
  3. Handshake — campus recruiting platform; best for new-grad BDR/SDR pipelines, especially outside traditional target schools.
  4. CollegeRecruiter.com — entry-level-focused job board; best as a supplementary channel for early-career sales postings.
  5. RevGenius (SDR/BDR channels) — large practitioner Slack community with dedicated early-career channels; useful for sourcing and for gauging a candidate's engagement in the field.

C. Sales Leadership & Practitioner Communities

  1. Pavilion (formerly Revenue Collective) — membership community for GTM leaders; best for sourcing experienced sales managers/directors and warm intros through mutual members.
  2. RevGenius (general community) — large, free-to-join community across all GTM roles; best for broad visibility on a management-level opening.
  3. Modern Sales Pros — invite-oriented community for sales leaders at tech companies; best for VP/director-level sourcing conversations, not job-board-style posting.
  4. Sales Assembly — training and community platform for B2B sales teams at scale-ups; best for finding candidates actively investing in their own sales craft.
  5. Sales Hacker community — long-standing practitioner community/content hub; best for surfacing candidates through content engagement rather than direct postings.
  6. Surf and Sales — sales conference and community founded by practitioners for practitioners; best for building a pipeline of engaged, senior ICs and first-time managers over time, not urgent same-month hires.

D. Trainer & Methodology Alumni Networks

  1. Sales Gravy (Jeb Blount's community) — best for experienced reps and managers steeped in fundamentals-first sales training.
  2. JB Sales (JBarrows) alumni/community — best for candidates trained in a specific, well-known cold-outreach and qualification methodology.
  3. Winning by Design community — best for candidates with a strong grounding in customer-success-adjacent, process-driven B2B selling.
  4. Force Management alumni network — best for enterprise/complex-deal AE and sales-leadership candidates trained in structured qualification methodology.

E. Diversity-Focused Networks

  1. National Association of Women Sales Professionals (NAWSP) — best for sourcing and developing women in sales roles at every level.
  2. Women in Sales Everywhere (WISE) — community and events focused on advancing women in B2B sales.
  3. Sistas in Sales — community built for Black women in sales; best for building a genuinely diverse senior-AE and leadership pipeline.
  4. Women in Revenue — cross-functional GTM community (sales, marketing, CS) for women in revenue-generating roles.
  5. Prospanica — national association for Hispanic professionals; maintains career resources and a job board relevant to sales and business roles.
  6. National Black MBA Association — job board and network; best for sourcing sales and sales-leadership candidates from a strong professional pipeline.
  7. HBCUConnect.com — job board and network connecting employers with HBCU students and alumni; best for early-career and campus-style sales pipelines.
  8. Out & Equal Workplace Advocates — LGBTQ+ workplace equality organization running career fairs and employer partnerships; best for sourcing through an inclusive-employer lens.

F. Veteran & Military Transition Talent

  1. RecruitMilitary — large veteran-focused job board and career fair network; field/territory sales is consistently one of the top-hiring categories for transitioning veterans.
  2. Hire Heroes USA — nonprofit offering free career coaching and employer connections for veterans and military spouses; best for structured, higher-touch veteran hiring pipelines.
  3. DoD SkillBridge — Department of Defense program placing active-duty service members into civilian roles (including sales) during their last months of service, often as a trial period before formal hire.
  4. Military.com Career Center — job board and resources specifically for military-connected job seekers, including military spouses.

G. Ecosystem / Platform-Specific Communities

  1. Salesforce Trailblazer Community (Ohana) — best for sourcing AE, CSM, and sales-ops talent fluent in the Salesforce ecosystem.
  2. HubSpot Community — best for sourcing SMB/mid-market sales talent trained on the HubSpot stack and inbound-led motions.
  3. Otta — tech-focused job board with transparent culture/salary data; best for reaching candidates who filter specifically for growth-stage tech companies.
  4. The Muse — job board built around employer culture profiles; best when your employer brand/story is a genuine differentiator for the role.

H. Regional & Local

  1. Local SaaS/RevOps Meetup groups (via Meetup.com) — best for building a warm local pipeline of sales talent over time, especially in secondary tech hubs.
  2. Chief — membership network for senior women executives (cross-functional, includes CROs and VPs of Sales); best for senior sales-leadership sourcing.
  3. Local Chamber of Commerce / economic development sales-talent pipelines — best for regional field-sales and territory roles where local market knowledge matters more than tech-hub credentials.

How to use it

Pick 3–4 sources matched to the level and specialization of the role you're filling this quarter — don't run all 40 at once — and track which ones actually produce interviews-per-hire so your list narrows to what works for your specific roles.

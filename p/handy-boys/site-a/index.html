02How a turnover works

From check-out to guest-ready, in four steps.

Every changeover follows the same order, so nothing is skipped and you can see each step as it is done.

01

Reset & clean

Beds are stripped and dressed with fresh linen, the bathroom and kitchen cleaned, towels and throw set, and the consumables topped up, so the room looks like the listing photos.

  • Linen changed, bed dressed
  • Bathroom, kitchen and floors
  • Towels, soap and consumables restocked
02

Inventory & condition check

We walk the property with a tablet against your inventory. Anything missing, broken or marked is photographed and noted, before the next guest can find it.

  • Checked against your inventory list
  • Damage and wear photographed
  • Notes and photos sent to your portal
03

Maintenance & safety

Smoke and CO alarms are tested, and the dripping tap, loose handle or blown bulb is fixed in the same visit. Bigger jobs are quoted, not guessed at.

  • Alarms tested each visit
  • Small fixes done on the spot
  • Larger jobs quoted before work starts
04

Guest-ready, keys back

A final pass against the guest-ready list, the key returned to the safe or held by us, and the checklist signed off in your portal. The next guests arrive to a property that matches the listing.

  • Final pass on the guest-ready list
  • Key safe locked, spare key accounted for
  • Checklist signed off in your portal

10Questions

Straight answers.

What is a turnover?

A turnover, also called a changeover, is the work between one guest checking out and the next checking in. It covers cleaning, resetting the beds and linen, checking the inventory and condition, and fixing anything that needs it, so the property is guest-ready for the next arrival.

What do you do in a changeover?

We reset the beds and linen, clean, restock consumables and check everything against your inventory. We photograph any damage, test the smoke and CO alarms, handle small repairs and record every step in your owner portal, so you can watch the checklist being ticked off.

Can you hold keys for my property?

Yes, as part of Priority Club membership. We hold spare keys for your property, log each one, and attend if a guest is locked out or needs a key. Key holding is not offered on General rates.

What areas do you cover?

Eastbourne and Bexhill for now, including the postcode districts BN20 to BN24 and TN39 and TN40. Use the postcode checker on this page to confirm your address. If you are outside the area, join the list and we will tell you when we reach you.

How are you charged?

We start with a free walk-through, then quote your property. Holiday-let changeovers are priced by bedroom count, with add-ons such as linen and oven cleans, and handyman work is charged by the hour, call-out, half-day or full day. Regular hosts can join the Priority Club for a monthly fee per property, with lower rates and a 6-month minimum term. The rate card is a draft: draft rate, owner to confirm.

What if a guest is locked out?

Priority Club members call us and we attend with the spare key, so the guest gets a person rather than a voicemail, and the call-out is logged in your portal. On General rates lock-outs are not covered and the guest needs a locksmith. Out-of-hours cover and response times: [owner to confirm].

Do you work at weekends?

Changeovers often fall on weekends because that is when many guests come and go, and our work is built around your check-out and check-in times. Exactly which weekend slots are available is agreed at your walk-through: [owner to confirm weekend availability].

Do you do compliance paperwork for me?

We keep track of the dates and remind you before gas safety, electrical, PAT testing, smoke and CO alarm and fire safety checks fall due, and we keep the records in your portal. Certificates come from the qualified engineer, and what applies depends on your property and how you let it, so confirm requirements with your insurer and local council.

Do you do end of tenancy and void work?

Yes. We do end of tenancy cleans, void repairs, reinstatement and a final check for landlords and agents, priced by bedroom count. Full refurbishment and major works are quoted separately.

Do you do handyman and decorating for people who are not hosts?

Yes. Handyman, painting and decorating are open to anyone in Eastbourne and Bexhill: flat-pack and fixings, leaks and tap repairs, doors and locks, fencing and gates, plastering touch-ups, sealant and odd jobs. The same team services our short-let customers.

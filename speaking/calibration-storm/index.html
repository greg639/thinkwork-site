The session

Calibration Storm, round by round.

A game with a real score. The room is the player. Sales craft is what it learns.

110 MIN

Everyone marks the same call, cold

A real sales call plays, from our anonymised library. Not one of yours. Every phone in the room scores it. No rubric, no discussion, no conferring.

215 MIN

The spread goes on the wall

Every mark appears as a dot. The room is usually spread across all four levels on the same call. That spread is exactly why written standards exist.

320 MIN

One skill, opened all the way up

The full rubric on screen. What Foundation, Proficient, Advanced and Expert look like, with real examples of each performed on real calls. This is the teaching.

420 MIN

Mark again. Watch the room pull together.

The same call, plus five fresh ones. The spread pulls onto two adjacent levels, and the room’s agreement score is recalculated live. The jump is the proof.

515 MIN

Human markers and a machine drop in

Our Second Marking panel marked the same calls blind, without seeing any name. So did a commercial AI agent. Both land on your scoreboard, beside your room.

610 MIN

What that one skill unlocks

Where the skill you just learned sits in the wider standard, and which skills it has to come before. Then we book the 90-day re-mark.

One call runs in Portuguese with the subtitles off, to show the standard reads behaviour, not language. Nothing is ever attributed to a person, a manager or a team.

Cold Outreach Response Rate Benchmark Calculator

Compare your call, email, and LinkedIn response rates against channel and industry-adjusted benchmarks in minutes, with a 4-tier rating scale instead of a gut feeling about whether your numbers are good.

What's inside

  • Response rate formula and per-channel input table
  • Channel benchmark table (cold vs. warm) for calls, email, and LinkedIn
  • Industry/segment adjustment multipliers (SMB, mid-market, enterprise, regulated)
  • Performance Index formula and 4-tier rating scale (Needs Improvement / Developing / On Benchmark / Elite)
  • Fully worked example calculation
  • Monthly tracking table across all three channels

Compare your call, email, and LinkedIn response rates against channel and industry benchmarks in minutes — no software required, just the formulas and tables below.

Step 1 — Calculate Your Raw Response Rate Per Channel

Response Rate (%) = (Responses Received ÷ Touches Sent) × 100

ChannelTouches sentResponses receivedResponse rate
Cold calls=Responses/Touches×100
Cold email=Responses/Touches×100
LinkedIn outreach=Responses/Touches×100

"Response" = any reply that isn't an auto-reject (out-of-office, unsubscribe, "wrong number"). A one-word "not interested" still counts as a response for this calculation — it's a different metric (conversion rate) that measures whether the response was positive.

Step 2 — Compare Against Channel Benchmarks

ChannelCold / no prior relationshipWarm (referral, inbound, existing relationship)
Cold calls (connect rate)5–15%25–40%
Cold email reply rate1–3%8–15%
LinkedIn connection + message reply rate10–20%25–40%

Step 3 — Adjust for Industry/Segment

Apply a multiplier to the base benchmark depending on your market:

SegmentMultiplier
SMB (fast decision cycles, higher volume tolerance)×1.2
Mid-market×1.0 (baseline)
Enterprise (longer cycles, gatekeeper-heavy)×0.7
Highly regulated industry (finance, healthcare, government)×0.6

Adjusted Benchmark = Base Benchmark × Segment Multiplier

Step 4 — Score Yourself Against the Adjusted Benchmark

Performance Index = (Your Response Rate ÷ Adjusted Benchmark) × 100

Performance IndexRating
Below 50Needs Improvement — likely a targeting, list-quality, or messaging problem
50–99Developing — on the right track, room to optimize opener/subject line/list quality
100–149On Benchmark — performing in line with expectations for your segment
150+Elite — document and codify what you're doing differently; candidate for a team playbook

Worked Example

You're a mid-market rep. This month: 400 cold emails sent, 14 replies.

  • Response Rate = (14 ÷ 400) × 100 = 3.5%
  • Base benchmark (cold email, cold list) = 1–3% → use midpoint 2%
  • Segment multiplier (mid-market) = ×1.0
  • Adjusted Benchmark = 2%
  • Performance Index = (3.5 ÷ 2) × 100 = 175 → Elite

Your email response rate isn't just "fine" — it's meaningfully outperforming benchmark for your segment. Worth isolating what's different about your subject lines or list quality and turning it into a repeatable practice for the team.

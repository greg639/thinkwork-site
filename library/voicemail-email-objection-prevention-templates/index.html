Voicemail & Email Objection-Prevention Templates

Ten word-for-word async outreach templates — five voicemail scripts paired with five matching emails — that name and defuse the five most common objections before a live call even starts.

What's inside

  • 5 word-for-word voicemail scripts (under 30 seconds each)
  • 5 matching email templates with subject lines
  • Coverage across price, trust, competitor, timing, and proof objections
  • Placeholder / fill-in-field legend
  • Sequencing guidance — which to send first and when

The idea: don't wait for the objection on a live call — name it first, in an async channel, where the prospect can absorb the answer without feeling cornered. This is inoculation, not persuasion.

Placeholder legend: [First Name] [Your Name] [Company] [Category] [Competitor] [Calendar link] [Specific proof point/stat] — replace all before sending.

Sequencing: send the email first (or same day), then leave the matching voicemail within 24 hours referencing that you sent something. Reversing the order works too — just be consistent so the prospect isn't hearing/reading two unrelated messages.

1. Price / Budget Preemption

Voicemail (≈25 sec):

"Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company]. I know cost is usually the first question, so let me save you the guesswork — [Company] typically runs [$X–$Y] for a team your size, and most clients see that pay for itself inside [X months] through [specific outcome]. I'll follow up with an email that breaks that down. If it's useful, grab 15 minutes here: [Calendar link]. Thanks, [First Name]."

Email — Subject: "The budget number, upfront"

Hi [First Name], Most people I talk to want the price before anything else, so here it is: [Company] runs [$X–$Y] for a team of [size], and [X]% of clients recover that in the first [timeframe] through [specific result — e.g. a 12% lift in close rate]. If the range works, here's 15 minutes to see if the fit is right: [Calendar link] If it doesn't, no hard feelings — just let me know and I'll close the loop. [Your Name]

2. Trust / Legitimacy Preemption

Voicemail:

"Hi [First Name], [Your Name] from [Company] — you may not have heard of us, which is fair. Quick anchor: we work with teams like [2–3 recognizable names or 'teams in your industry'], and [specific proof point — e.g. 200+ sales teams]. I'll send a case study your way — if it's relevant, let's grab 15 minutes: [Calendar link]."

Email — Subject: "In case you haven't heard of us"

Hi [First Name], Fair chance you haven't come across [Company] yet. Quick anchor: [2–3 proof points / logos / stat]. Attached is a short case study from a team similar to yours. If it's relevant, here's 15 minutes: [Calendar link] [Your Name]

3. Competitor / "We Already Have Something" Preemption

Voicemail:

"Hi [First Name], [Your Name] here from [Company]. I'm guessing you've already got something in place for [Category] — most teams we work with did too. What tends to bring them to us is [specific gap the incumbent doesn't solve]. I'll send over a quick comparison — if it's relevant, here's 15 minutes: [Calendar link]."

Email — Subject: "Assuming you already have [Category] covered"

Hi [First Name], You're probably already using something for [Category] — most of our customers were too. The reason they switched (or added us) usually comes down to [specific gap]. I've attached a quick side-by-side. Worth 15 minutes? [Calendar link] [Your Name]

4. Timing Preemption

Voicemail:

"Hi [First Name], [Your Name] from [Company]. I know timing's everything, so I'll keep this to 10 seconds: if [specific trigger, e.g. Q3 planning] isn't on your radar yet, no worries — just tell me the better window and I'll come back then. If it is on your radar, here's 15 minutes: [Calendar link]."

Email — Subject: "Bad time? Tell me when's better"

Hi [First Name], If now isn't the right window, just reply with when is — I'll come back then, no follow-up spam in between. If it IS relevant now, here's 15 minutes: [Calendar link] [Your Name]

5. Proof / "Does This Actually Work" Preemption

Voicemail:

"Hi [First Name], [Your Name] from [Company]. I know 'does this actually work' is the real question, so instead of a pitch — I'll send you the exact before/after numbers from a team like yours: [specific stat]. If that's compelling, let's talk: [Calendar link]."

Email — Subject: "The numbers, not the pitch"

Hi [First Name], Skipping the pitch. Here's what changed for a team like yours: [specific before/after metric]. If that's compelling, 15 minutes: [Calendar link]. If not, tell me what number WOULD be compelling and I'll see if we can show it. [Your Name]

How to use it

Pick the objection category most likely to come up with this specific prospect, fill in the bracketed fields, and send the email and voicemail within 24 hours of each other so the objection is already defused before you get them live.

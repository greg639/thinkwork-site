SDR Onboarding Playbook

A complete 30-60-90 curriculum for standing up a new SDR: day-by-day training plan, the core cold-call script, an 8-touch cadence, a shadowing log, an objection bank, and the certification gate that clears reps for full territory.

How to use it

Follow the day-by-day 30/60/90 curriculum in order, use the embedded script, cadence, shadowing log and objection bank as working documents from week one, and gate territory assignment on the Day-90 graduation checklist.

What's inside

  • Days 1-30 foundation curriculum, week-by-week
  • Days 31-60 supervised execution plan with ramp quota
  • Days 61-90 certification and full-ramp plan
  • Core cold-call script skeleton (word-for-word opener through close)
  • 8-touch / 15-day outbound cadence table by channel
  • Call Shadowing Log template
  • Objection response bank (top 5 objections)
  • Day-90 graduation checklist gating full territory assignment

Philosophy: an SDR earns unsupervised territory. They don't get it on day one. Every phase below ends in a checkpoint that gates the next phase.

Days 1–30: Foundation

Week 1: Systems & Story

  • Tool logins: CRM, dialer, sequencer, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, call-intelligence tool
  • Company narrative, product walkthrough, pricing/packaging
  • ICP document: who we sell to, who we don't, disqualifiers
  • Competitive battlecards: top 3 competitors, how we win
  • Read the last 20 closed-won deal summaries

Week 2: Absorb the motion

  • Shadow 10 calls total: 5 AE discovery calls, 5 SDR prospecting calls, logged in the Call Shadowing Log below
  • Memorize the Core Cold Call Script (below) word-for-word
  • Memorize responses to the top 5 objections (Objection Bank below)
  • Build personal target account list (first 20 accounts)

Week 3: Paired dialing

  • Rep dials, manager/mentor listens live on every call and scores using a mini-rubric: Opening / Discovery / Ask for next step (3 criteria, 1–4 each)
  • Build 2 personal cadence variants (industry-specific opener)
  • Practice writing prospecting emails against real accounts (no sends yet: review only)

Week 4: First solo reps

  • Solo dial block at 25% of full daily volume target
  • Same-day call review with manager after every session
  • Day-30 checkpoint: readiness review (not certification): go/no-go on entering Days 31–60

Days 31–60: Supervised execution

  • Full 8-touch/15-day cadence (below) live on B-tier accounts only
  • Ramp quota: 50% of full target
  • Weekly 1:1 call review scored informally against the Cold Call Certification Scorecard categories
  • Objection-handling drill session 2×/week (manager role-plays the prospect)
  • Day-60 checkpoint: formal scorecard dry run: score but don't gate; identify weak categories for the final 30 days

Days 61–90: Certification & full ramp

  • Full quota volume, full territory (A/B/C-tier accounts)
  • Sit the Cold Call Certification Scorecard for real (3 calls, must pass to keep unsupervised territory)
  • Day-90 checkpoint: Graduation Checklist:
  • Passed Cold Call Certification Scorecard (≥80%, no auto-fails)
  • Hit ≥ 80% of ramp quota in Days 61–90
  • CRM hygiene: 100% of calls logged same-day for 2 consecutive weeks
  • Built and executed at least 1 full account plan independently
  • Manager sign-off
  • Graduate: transition to standard 1:1 cadence, assign permanent pod/team, full commission plan if applicable

Core cold-call script skeleton

Opening (0–10s): "Hi [Name], this is [Rep] from [Company]: I know this is a cold call, do you have 20 seconds?" Permission/reason (10–20s): "The reason I'm calling, I saw [trigger event / role-specific reason] and wanted to see if [problem hypothesis] is something you're dealing with." Discovery (20–60s): "How are you handling [specific process] today?" → follow-up: "What happens when [likely failure point]?" Value tie-in: "That's exactly what we help [similar company/persona] with: [one-sentence outcome, no feature dump]." Ask: "Worth 20 minutes next week to see if it's relevant? I've got Tuesday at 10 or Thursday at 2, which works better?"

Objection bank (top 5)

ObjectionResponse pattern
"Send me an email""Happy to: so I send the right thing, what's the one problem you'd want it to speak to?"
"We already use [competitor]""Makes sense, most of our customers switched from [competitor] too. Curious what prompted you to look at us today vs. staying put, is there something not working?"
"Not interested""Totally fair, most people say that on a cold call. Can I ask, is it 'not interested in this problem' or 'not interested in talking to me right now'?"
"No budget""Understood: this isn't a budget conversation yet, it's a 'does this problem matter' conversation. If it turns out it's worth solving, budget conversations follow."
"I'm busy, call me later""No problem, better to actually get you at a good time. Does [specific day/time] work, or is mornings generally better for you?"

Manager checklist for standing this up

  • Assign an onboarding mentor (not the hiring manager) per new SDR
  • Block manager calendar for Week 3 paired-dialing sessions before hire's start date
  • Pre-load target account lists so Week 2 isn't spent building lists from scratch
  • Schedule Day-30/60/90 checkpoints on the calendar at time of hire

Directory maintenance notes

  • Free tiers are the first thing vendors cut when they raise prices — re-verify every tool's current limit quarterly, not just at first use.
  • Never build a critical daily workflow around a single free-tier tool with a hard monthly cap; rotate 2–3 tools across the team to stay under limits.
  • Treat any free enrichment data as a starting hypothesis, not a verified fact — confirm role/title changes with a LinkedIn glance before outreach.

How to use it

Match your immediate need (email, phone, firmographic, tech-stack, or trigger-event data) to the right category, start with the most generous free tier for that category, and re-verify limits before scaling volume through any single tool.

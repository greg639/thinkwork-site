The bread club

A loaf a week, in your name.

Regulars already come every week. A club turns that into money in the bank on the first of the month, and tells the bakery how many loaves to make before the mixer starts.

Deliveroo takes a commission on every order it sends. A club billed directly does not.

Our story

To nourish our community with love and good health through the art of baking bread.

The mission the bakery sets out on its own site.

Founded in 2020 by three sisters. European style breads and pastry on the south coast, 100% organic flour and ingredients, sourdough fermented over 48 hours. Google lists the bakery as women owned.

Multi-Threading Stakeholder Map Template

A single-page map of every buyer, influencer, blocker and champion in the account, so you can see single-threaded exposure before the deal reaches legal, not after it stalls there.

How to use it

Fill in the stakeholder inventory after every meeting or org-chart discovery, plot everyone onto the Influence vs. Support grid, and re-run the risk flags and coalition score before each forecast call to catch single-threading before it stalls the deal in legal.

What's inside

  • Stakeholder inventory table (name, title, department, role, champion status, last/next contact)
  • 5-role classification system: Economic Buyer, Champion, Influencer, User, Blocker/Detractor, Procurement/Legal
  • Influence vs. Support 2x2 grid with reading guide
  • Single-thread risk flag list (escalate at 2+ checked)
  • Coalition strength score out of 10
  • Blocker/detractor neutralization plan table
  • Multi-thread action plan for the next 2 weeks
  • Org chart sketch area
  • Re-scoring cadence tied to stage changes and forecast calls

Fill this out for every deal above your average deal size threshold, and refresh it every time you have a new meeting or a stakeholder change. One map per opportunity.

2. Role Classification (assign one to each stakeholder above)

  • EB: Economic Buyer: controls/releases budget, can say yes when everyone else says maybe
  • CH: Champion: actively sells internally on your behalf when you're not in the room
  • IN: Influencer: technical or functional voice whose opinion moves the EB
  • US: User: will use the product day-to-day; low power, but their complaints can kill a deal
  • BL: Blocker/Detractor: actively prefers status quo, a competitor, or has no reason to change
  • PR: Procurement/Legal: process gatekeeper, not a buying-motivation stakeholder

3. Influence vs. Support Grid

Plot every stakeholder from Section 1 onto this grid (draw or use initials):

`` High Influence | COACH THEM | CHAMPION: PROTECT | (convert to | (arm them with | champion) | ammo, don't over-email) |--------------------|-------------------- Low Influence | IGNORE FOR NOW | KEEP WARM | (low priority) | (nice-to-have ally) +--------------------+-------------------- Low Support High Support ``

Read the grid: anything in the top-left (high influence, low/unknown support) is your single-threading risk, you have power in the room that isn't yet on your side.

4. Single-Thread Risk Flag

Check any that are true. 2 or more checked = escalate to manager this week.

  • Only 1 stakeholder has ever taken a call with us
  • No stakeholder above Director/VP level has been directly engaged
  • Champion has never introduced us to their manager or a peer
  • We cannot name who signs off in procurement/legal
  • Our only contact has gone dark (no response) for 7+ days
  • We have never spoken to anyone who will actually use the product day-to-day
  • Deal amount is above [$______ threshold] and fewer than 3 stakeholders are mapped

5. Coalition Strength Score

Score 0-2 for each, sum out of 10:

QuestionScore (0=no, 1=partial, 2=yes)
Do we have a named Economic Buyer contact?
Do we have at least one Champion actively selling for us?
Have we neutralized or converted the primary Blocker?
Have we engaged procurement/legal proactively (not reactively)?
Have we talked to an actual end user?
Total (out of 10)

0-4 = high risk, single-threaded. 5-7 = building. 8-10 = well-threaded, low risk.

Cadence

Re-score Sections 4 and 5 every time the deal stage changes, and before every internal forecast call. A coalition score that hasn't moved in 3 weeks is itself a warning sign.

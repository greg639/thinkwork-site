Strategic Account Plan Template

A one-page account plan covering goals, org chart, whitespace, and risk that's built to actually get updated quarterly — not filed away after the kickoff call.

What's inside

  • Account snapshot fields (ARR, tier, renewal date, tenure)
  • Customer's stated business goals vs. our account goals
  • Stakeholder/org chart map with role tags (economic buyer, champion, influencer, blocker, user)
  • Current-state usage and adoption summary
  • Whitespace and expansion opportunity log
  • Risk register with mitigation owner and date
  • Competitive landscape note
  • 90-day action plan with owners
  • Engagement cadence and next three touchpoints

One page. If a section hasn't changed in two consecutive quarterly reviews, that's a signal you don't actually know the account well enough yet — dig in before you copy it forward again.

Update this plan at every QBR at minimum. A plan that only exists in someone's head isn't a plan — it's a memory that leaves the company when the AM does.

How to use it

Fill in every section during your account planning session (or QBR prep), keep it to one page, and revisit it at least once a quarter — treat any section that hasn't changed in two reviews as a signal you don't know the account well enough.

Competency-Based Onboarding Curriculum Framework

A week-by-week onboarding map that names the exact competency, target mastery level, and evidence of readiness for every week, replacing a generic list of training modules with a graduation standard reps actually have to earn.

How to use it

Hand this to onboarding managers before a cohort starts; use the weekly map as the literal curriculum plan and the sign-off gate as the pass/fail checkpoint each Friday.

What's inside

  • Six-competency taxonomy with plain-language definitions
  • Five-level mastery scale (Novice → Expert) used throughout
  • Full 12-week map: competency focus, target level, delivery method and evidence required each week
  • Manager weekly sign-off gate (3 required questions before advancing a rep)
  • Escalation and remediation path for reps who miss the same target twice
  • Graduation competency profile, the minimum level required in each competency to exit onboarding
  • Post-graduation re-check cadence (30/60/90 days) per competency

Use this instead of: a week-by-week list of training modules ("Week 1: Product 101, Week 2: CRM Training, Week 3: Role Plays"). This framework names the competency, the mastery level, and the proof required, week by week.

The Six Core Competencies

Every onboarding activity should map to exactly one of these. If it doesn't, it isn't onboarding: it's orientation, and belongs in a different bucket (benefits, IT setup, etc.).

LevelNameBehavioral bar
1NoviceExecutes with a script/checklist and close supervision; inconsistent unsupported
2DevelopingExecutes independently in straightforward situations; needs coaching in complex ones

5ExpertSets the standard others train on; originates new approaches

Manager Weekly Sign-Off Gate

Before a rep moves to the next week, the manager answers three questions in writing:

  1. What specific behavior did I observe this week that proves the target level (not "seems ready")?
  2. What is the single weakest moment I observed, and what would one level higher have looked like?
  3. Am I advancing this rep on evidence, or on the calendar? (If calendar, stop and remediate instead.)

Escalation & Remediation Path

If a rep misses the target level on the same competency in two consecutive weekly checks:

  1. Manager pulls in an Advanced/Expert-level peer for a paired shadow session within 3 business days.
  2. Onboarding pauses forward progress on new competencies (does not restart from Week 1) until the flagged competency clears its bar.
  3. If still short after the paired session, escalate to enablement lead for a root-cause review (skill gap vs. confidence gap vs. role-fit question).

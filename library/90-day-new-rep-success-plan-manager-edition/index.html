90-Day New Rep Success Plan (Manager Edition)

The manager-side commitment plan that mirrors a new rep's 30-60-90: exactly what you'll teach, review and unblock in each phase, with a pass/fail checkpoint before advancing.

How to use it

Fill in and review this alongside the rep's own 30-60-90 plan at each phase boundary, and don't advance to the next phase until the listed success checkpoint is actually met.

90-Day New Rep Success Plan, Manager Edition

The manager-side commitments that make the rep's 30-60-90 plan actually achievable. Fill in and review with the rep at each phase boundary.

Phase 1: Days 1-30: Foundation

I will teach:

  • Our sales process stage-by-stage, using 2 real deals as examples
  • How to use the CRM the way our team actually uses it (not just the generic training)
  • Our discovery/qualification framework, with a live call demonstration
  • How and when to escalate to me vs. peers vs. other departments

I will review:

  • Daily: activity metrics (calls/emails logged): 5 minutes, async is fine
  • Weekly: 1 recorded call together using the New Rep Call Review Rubric
  • Weekly 1:1: 30 minutes, half progress / half open questions

I will unblock:

  • All tech stack access confirmed working by day 1 (see New Hire Tech Stack Checklist)
  • Introduce them to 3 people they'll need cross-functionally (CS, Solutions, Legal/contracts)
  • Remove them from any deal/quota pressure conversations until day 30

Success checkpoint before advancing to Phase 2:

  • Day-30 Skill Gap Diagnostic completed: no more than 2 domains in the "Critical" band
  • Rep has shadowed at least 5 live calls and reverse-shadowed at least 2

Phase 2: Days 31-60: Applied Practice

I will teach:

  • Objection handling specific to the objections THEY are actually hearing (not generic ones)
  • Negotiation basics relevant to our deal sizes/structures
  • How to multi-thread and identify additional stakeholders in a live deal

I will review:

  • Weekly: 2 recorded calls using the rubric (increase from 1)
  • Weekly: pipeline review: are they qualifying correctly, not just generating activity
  • Bi-weekly 1:1: add a career/skill-development conversation, not just deal review

I will unblock:

  • Pair them with a top-performing peer for 2 live call shadows this phase
  • Give explicit permission to bring me into a live deal if they're stuck, remove the fear of "looking bad"
  • Flag and address any tech stack or process friction they've hit by now

Success checkpoint before advancing to Phase 3:

  • Average rubric score of 3.5+/5 across the last 4 reviewed calls
  • At least 1 self-sourced qualified opportunity in active pipeline
  • CRM hygiene consistently accurate (spot-checked, not just self-reported)

Phase 3: Days 61-90: Independent Ramp

I will teach:

  • How to forecast their own pipeline accurately (not just optimistic)
  • Territory/account planning for sustainable pipeline generation beyond ramp
  • Where they sit relative to team benchmarks, and what "good" looks like at 6 and 12 months

I will review:

  • Weekly 1:1 returns to standard cadence (30 min, pipeline + coaching)
  • 1 recorded call per week continues through month 6, not just to day 90
  • Full re-run of the Skill Gap Diagnostic at day 90

I will unblock:

  • Set an explicit, realistic quota ramp (not full quota on day 91 if the plan assumed a slower ramp)
  • Confirm they know who to go to for each type of deal blocker going forward
  • Have the "what's next for your development" conversation: this is also a retention moment

Success checkpoint: full ramp complete:

  • Day-90 Skill Gap Diagnostic: no domains in "Critical" band, at least 4 of 6 "On Track"
  • Meeting or exceeding the agreed ramp-quota target for month 3
  • Rep can run a full call cycle without manager/peer support

End-of-Quarter Pipeline Scrub Toolkit

Everything a manager needs to run one clean, honest pipeline scrub in the final week of the quarter: a pre-scrub checklist, a word-for-word scrub script, and a tracker to run every deal through.

How to use it

Run the pre-scrub checklist 3-5 days before quarter close, work the scrub script one rep at a time (not as a group), force every deal into one of the 4 verdicts with no exceptions, and send the exec summary the same day so leadership sees the real number before quarter-end surprises do.

What's inside

  • The pre-scrub prep checklist (run 3-5 days before quarter close)
  • The scrub-meeting opening script
  • The per-deal scrub script with hard-stop disqualifying questions
  • The 4-verdict decision framework every deal must land in
  • The full scrub tracker table, ready to copy into a sheet
  • The post-scrub wrap-up script and exec-summary format

Part 1: Pre-Scrub Checklist (run 3-5 business days before quarter close)

  • Pull every open opportunity with a close date in the current quarter
  • Add every opportunity with no close date but stage ≥ Proposal (these get scrubbed too: they're often quietly slipping)
  • Flag any deal with zero logged activity in the last 14 days
  • Flag any deal that has already slipped its close date once or more this quarter
  • Pre-populate the tracker (Part 3) with deal name, rep, amount, current stage, current close date
  • Block 20-30 minutes per rep on the calendar: do not try to scrub the whole team in one group call
  • Send reps the tracker 24 hours ahead so they show up with answers, not surprises

Part 2, The Scrub Script

Opening (say this to each rep before starting deal-by-deal)

"This isn't a status update, it's a gut check. For every deal we go through, I need one of four verdicts: In, Slip, Kill, or Split. If a deal doesn't clearly earn 'In,' it doesn't stay in this quarter's number just because it's uncomfortable to move it."

Per-deal script

  1. "What's the single piece of evidence that this closes by quarter end: something the prospect said or did, not something we're hoping for?"
  2. "Who's the signer, and have they personally confirmed the date?"
  3. "Is there anything outstanding on their side: legal, procurement, budget approval, that isn't done?"
  4. "If this doesn't close in the next [X] business days, does it become a Slip or a Kill?"
  5. "Is there any part of this deal that could close as a smaller scope this quarter, with the rest pushed?" (this surfaces Splits)

The 4-verdict framework

VerdictDefinitionWhat happens next
InConfirmed signer, confirmed date, nothing outstandingStays in the quarter number as-is
SlipReal deal, real interest, but a specific blocker means it won't close this quarterMove close date, note the blocker and new evidence needed
KillNo activity, no confirmed next step, or prospect has gone darkClose-lost or moved to nurture: stop counting it
SplitPart of the deal can close now at reduced scope; the rest is real but laterCreate the smaller in-quarter deal, push the remainder as its own opportunity

Hard-stop disqualifying questions (if the rep can't answer these, the deal cannot be "In")

  • "What's the exact next step, and does it have a date attached from their side?"
  • "When did you last speak to someone other than your champion?"
  • "Has anyone on their side used the word 'yes' about anything specific this week?"

Part 4: Post-Scrub Wrap-Up

Manager closing script (say to the full team or in the recap email)

"After the scrub, our real in-quarter number is $[X] across [N] deals, which is [coverage ratio] against target. [N] deals moved to Slip, [N] were killed, and [N] split into a smaller in-quarter piece. Here's what each of us owns before [date]."

Exec-summary format (send up the chain same day)

```markdown

Quarter-End Pipeline Scrub Summary, [Date]

  • Starting forecast: $[X]
  • Post-scrub in-quarter number: $[Y]
  • Deals moved to Slip: [N] ($[amount])
  • Deals Killed: [N] ($[amount])
  • Deals Split: [N] (in-quarter piece: $[amount])
  • Coverage ratio post-scrub: [ratio]
  • Top 3 risks to the number this week: [list]

```

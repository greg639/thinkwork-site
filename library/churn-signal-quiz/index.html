Is My Account at Risk? Churn Signal Quiz

Answer 12 quick questions about usage, sentiment, and stakeholder engagement to get an instant churn-risk read on any account — plus what to do next based on your score.

What's inside

  • 12 diagnostic questions across usage, sentiment, and stakeholder engagement
  • A simple 0/1/2 scoring key you can run in under 5 minutes per account
  • Four risk bands — Healthy, Watch, At-Risk, Critical — each with a specific next action
  • Pattern-reading guidance for when one section scores high and others don't
  • Section-specific fixes: adoption problem vs. trust problem vs. relationship risk
  • A built-in escalation trigger for when to loop in a manager or exec sponsor

How this works

Answer all 12 questions about one specific account (not your whole book). Score each honestly — this is a diagnostic, not a performance review. Add up your points and read your risk band at the end.

Scoring key

  • Yes / Clearly true = 2 points
  • Somewhat / Unsure = 1 point
  • No / Clearly false = 0 points

Section A — Usage (the account's actual behavior)

  1. Has core product usage (logins, active seats, key workflows) declined over the last 90 days?
  2. Are fewer than half of the licensed/provisioned seats active in a typical week?
  3. Has usage of the single feature that drove the original purchase decision dropped or flatlined?
  4. Have support tickets shifted in tone from "how do I do X" to "this isn't working for our team" or "we're not getting value"?

Section B — Sentiment (how they talk about you)

  1. Has your last NPS, CSAT, or relationship-health score dropped versus the prior period, or gone unanswered?
  2. Was your last scheduled QBR or check-in rescheduled, shortened to under 15 minutes, or skipped outright?
  3. Has the tone in emails/calls shifted from partnership language ("we," "our roadmap") to transactional/vendor language ("your product," "the invoice")?
  4. Have you heard "let me check with the team" or "we'll circle back" more than once recently without an actual follow-up?

Section C — Stakeholder engagement (who's actually in the room)

  1. Has your economic buyer or main champion changed roles, been promoted out, or left the company in the last 6 months?
  2. Is there currently only one engaged stakeholder in the account (single-threaded relationship)?
  3. Has a new stakeholder joined the account with no prior relationship to your team or context on the original purchase?
  4. Has the account gone quiet — no replies, no meeting acceptances — for 3 or more consecutive weeks?

Reading the pattern, not just the number

  • High Section A, low B/C → Adoption problem. Fix with training/rollout, not a relationship save.
  • High Section B, low A/C → Trust/experience problem. A support or delivery issue is bleeding into sentiment — investigate recent tickets/incidents.
  • High Section C, low A/B → Relationship risk despite healthy product fit. Multi-thread immediately before the single contact leaves or goes quiet permanently.
  • High across all three → This is a genuine churn risk. Treat it with the same urgency as a stalled must-win deal.

How to use it

Run this on any account where something feels "off" — it takes under 5 minutes — and use the risk band to decide whether you handle it solo, loop in your manager, or escalate to an executive sponsor call.

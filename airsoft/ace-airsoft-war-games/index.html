Straight answers

Questions people type into a search box

How old do you have to be to play at Ace Airsoft War Games?
The minimum age to play is 12 years old. Under 16s need a parent or guardian over 18 on site throughout the day, though they do not have to play. Under 18s need a parent or guardian to sign the consent form at signing in, and must wear full face protection. Pyrotechnics are over 18s only.
How much does a day at Ace Airsoft War Games cost?
Own kit is £29 pre-booked online, or £32 if you walk on without booking. Rental kit is £51 including entry into all games. Free tea and coffee is in all three.
When are the game days?
We tend to hold open day games on the first and third Sunday of each month, confirmed with the site owner a couple of weeks beforehand. Gates open at 08:30, signing in closes at 09:30, the safety briefing is at 09:30, games start at 10:00, lunch is an hour at 13:00 and games end at 16:30.
What does the rental kit include?
Entry into games, one assault rifle, one magazine, 1,000 rounds of bottled ammo, a face mask and an assault vest, for £51. Rental kit must be pre booked and prepaid.
Do I need to be a member?
No. Standard club membership is £30 and issues a discount code worth £5 off the walk on fee at regular games, plus UKARA registration. It requires your own kit and the benefits do not apply to rental. UKARA only registration is £10. Applying the member price to your account instead of a typed code is proposed.
What are the joule limits at Ace Airsoft War Games?
Chrono with the heaviest weight BB you intend to use. Any gun capable of full auto is 1.2 joules maximum, pistols of all types 1.2 joules, DMRs 1.9 joules and single shot sniper rifles 2.4 joules, both with a 30 metre minimum engagement distance. HPA guns must be anti tamper tournament locked. Chronograph testing is carried out at the start of the day and may take place at random during the day.
Can I bring my own BBs?
Yes, as long as they are biodegradable: this is a bio BB only site. We stock 0.20g, 0.25g, 0.28g, 0.4g and 0.45g bio ammo on site. Anything other than those weights, please bring your own.
Do I have to book in advance?
Rental kit has to be pre booked and paid for online. With your own kit, booking online is £29 and walking on without booking is £32, and pre booking speeds up registration on the morning of games. Booking any game day six weeks out, rather than one date at a time, is proposed.
How do I pay?
Own kit places and rental kit are bought online. On site the payment is card only: there is no cash. Ammo and pyro are available to buy on the day. Paying for everything by card when you book is proposed.
Is there food?
Tea and coffee are free all day long from the cafe on site, which is a separate business and also sells burgers, hot dogs and chips at very reasonable prices. There are toilets in the safe zone area.
Where is Ace Airsoft War Games?
C/O Warped Sports, Tong Wood, Newport Road, Wolverhampton, Shropshire, TF11 8PN. what3words ///rationing.canyons.shadow. Leave the M54 at junction 3 onto the A41 towards Wolverhampton and Cosford: the site entrance is 20 metres from the roundabout. Phone 07786192832.
Can I book a private game for a birthday or a stag party?
Yes. Private games take 10 to 30 players aged 11 upwards, from 9am arrival to 4.30pm with an hour for lunch. A quote on screen and a deposit are proposed in place of emailing for a pricing structure.

Pyro, the rules

Retail cold burn only, over 18s only

  • Trusted manufacturers only, such as Enola Gaye or TLSFX
  • Thrown under arm, or better still rolled into position
  • Never launched or aimed at an individual
  • Never primed while moving, and never thrown blind
  • Banned: electric maroons, blank firing grenades and rounds, TRMRs, metal cased grenades, TAG rounds

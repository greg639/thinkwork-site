It's better to ask a question than to sit in silence.

When is the next Frontline event?
WW2 Event: Salm River, 4, 5 and 6 December 2026 at Swerford, Chipping Norton, OX7 4AT. British Airborne and US Airborne factions against all German factions. 23 bookings are needed for the event to go ahead.
How much does a Frontline weekend cost?
The full weekend is £90, or £45 for Saturday or Sunday. A £30 deposit secures the place and full payment is made on signing in at the event. Site hire, the camp site, insurance, in-game lunch and the evening social are included.
Can I rent a weapon?
Yes. Standard weapons are £25 for the weekend; the price may vary depending on the weapon. Booking the rental online with your ticket is proposed, so it is waiting for you at booking in.
What are the ration cards?
Ration cards let you eat scoff. A quarter card, Saturday breakfast or evening meal, is £7; a half card, Saturday breakfast and dinner, is £12; a full card, Saturday and Sunday breakfast and dinner, is £17. Baz's burger on Saturday evening is £3.50 online or £5 at the event. Bring your mess tins for lunch in the field.
What are the timings?
Friday: gates open 14:00, social and meal 18:00. Saturday: gates and breakfast 07:00, booking in and chrono 08:00, photograph 09:00, safety brief 09:30, game start 10:00, lunch in the field 12:30, game commence 13:30, endex 17:00, social and meal 18:00. Sunday: the same from 07:00, endex 17:00.
Can I camp?
Yes, at no extra cost. Period camping in a position out on the ground, or a modern tent in the safe zone with one raised fire pit for light. Players can arrive from 17:00 on the Friday of a weekend event to set up.
How old do you have to be?
Ages 13 to 16 must be accompanied by a participating adult.
What can I play as?
At Salm River, British Airborne or US Airborne against the German factions. Sniper roles are limited to four slots per side and medic roles to two per side. Sub machine guns should be limited to squad leaders, 2ics and officers; a rifleman with a sub machine gun is limited to semi-auto. Bringing a WW2 vehicle? Come for free.
Do I need a period loadout?
Correct loadouts are crucial to the immersion of the event. Each country represented in our events has its own basic loadout, curated to reflect the gear and weaponry used by soldiers from that nation at the time. Our loadouts page has the WWII and Vietnam guides.
Is alcohol sold?
No. We do not and have no intention of selling or distributing alcohol at our events.
What is the refund policy?
Event tickets are non refundable if you do not wish to attend. If Frontline Events must cancel an event, refunds will be issued.
What is The Zone?
The Zone is our STALKER inspired first-person role playing adventure combining airsoft, survival and horror, played with the FLETZ-D app. There are no Zone events for 2026.
How do I contact Frontline?
Telephone 07903 417160, email support@frontlineevent.co.uk, or ask in our Facebook group or Discord. Registered office 25 Hillcroft, Dunstable, LU6 1AQ.

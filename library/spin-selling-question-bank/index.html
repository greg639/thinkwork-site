SPIN Selling Question Bank

200+ ready-to-use Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-Payoff questions organized by category and deal stage, so you never freeze mid-discovery.

What's inside

  • 45 Situation questions covering process, team, tools, budget, and stakeholders
  • 53 Problem questions across quality, speed, cost, visibility, risk, scale, and morale
  • 40 Implication questions covering financial, downstream, career, and board-level stakes
  • 36 Need-Payoff questions covering value realization, renewal, and internal-selling framing
  • 10 universal follow-up probes to deepen any answer
  • Call-sequencing guide mapping question types to discovery-call phases
  • Bracket-adaptable [process]/[metric] placeholders usable across any industry

How to sequence these across a call

  • Opening (0-5 min): 2-3 Situation questions max — enough to orient, not interrogate
  • Discovery body: rotate Problem → Implication → Situation (as needed) → Problem
  • Before you propose anything: minimum 2 Need-Payoff questions per problem you plan to solve
  • Closing: reconfirm Need-Payoff answers back to the customer as your value proposition, in their words

Situation Questions (use early, sparingly — max 15% of talk time)

Current Process & Workflow

  1. Walk me through how [process] works today, start to finish.
  2. Who is involved in [process] at each step?
  3. How long does [process] typically take start to finish?
  4. What tools or systems do you use to manage [process] today?
  5. How is [process] different across teams/regions/products?
  6. What does a typical week look like for you and your team on this?
  7. Who owns this process end-to-end today?
  8. How did you handle [process] before your current system/approach?
  9. What's changed about this process in the last 12 months?
  10. How is success on [process] currently measured?

Team & Structure

  1. How many people are on your team, and how is work divided?
  2. Who reports to whom in this part of the org?
  3. How long has your current team been in place?
  4. What's the split between senior and junior people doing this work?
  5. Who else, outside your immediate team, touches this process?
  6. How is this function structured compared to a year ago?
  7. Are there plans to grow, shrink, or restructure this team?

Tools, Systems & Data

  1. What systems currently hold the data related to [problem area]?
  2. How well do your current tools talk to each other?
  3. Where does this data live once it's captured?
  4. How do you currently report [metric] to leadership?
  5. What's your tech stack for [function] today?
  6. How long have you used your current [tool/vendor]?
  7. What made you choose your current solution originally?
  8. When does your current contract/renewal come up?

Volume, Scale & Metrics

  1. What volume are we talking about — how many [units/deals/tickets/customers] per month?
  2. How has that volume changed over the last year?
  3. What's your current [metric] — conversion rate, cycle time, cost per unit?
  4. How do you track that number today?
  5. What's the target or benchmark you're measured against?
  6. How does your [metric] compare to this time last year?

Budget, Priorities & Change History

  1. Where does budget for this currently sit, and who controls it?
  2. What's driving the timing of this conversation now versus six months ago?
  3. What else is competing for budget/attention in this area this year?
  4. What's the last major change you made here, and how did it go?
  5. Who initiated the last change to this process?
  6. What's on your roadmap for this function over the next 12 months?

Stakeholders & Decision Landscape

  1. Who else besides you would be involved in evaluating a change here?
  2. Who uses the output of this process day-to-day?
  3. Who ultimately signs off on changes to this system?
  4. Has anyone tried to solve this before? What happened?
  5. Who internally is most affected if this doesn't get solved?
  6. Are other departments dealing with a version of this same issue?
  7. What's the reporting structure between your team and the exec sponsor?
  8. How were decisions like this made the last time your org went through one?

Problem Questions (the engine room of discovery)

Quality & Accuracy

  1. Where do errors or inconsistencies creep into [process]?
  2. How often does [process] produce a result that needs to be redone?
  3. What's the most common complaint about [process] internally?
  4. How confident are you in the accuracy of the data from [system]?
  5. Where do you see the most variation in how people execute [process]?

Speed & Efficiency

  1. Where does [process] tend to slow down or get stuck?
  2. What's the biggest bottleneck between [step A] and [step B]?
  3. How much manual work feels like it shouldn't be manual?
  4. What takes longer than it should in your current workflow?
  5. Where do handoffs between people or teams cause delay?
  6. What do people on your team complain about needing to do manually?

Cost & Resource Drain

  1. How much time does your team spend on [task] in a typical week?
  2. Where do you feel like you're paying for capability you don't fully use?
  3. What's the cost of the workaround you're using today?
  4. How many people would you need to add to keep scaling this as-is?
  5. What does it cost you when [process] goes wrong?

Visibility & Control

  1. How confident are you in your visibility into [metric] at any given moment?
  2. Where do you feel like you're flying blind?
  3. What questions from leadership do you struggle to answer quickly?
  4. How much of this process happens outside any system you can see into?
  5. What surprises you most about how [process] actually runs day-to-day?

Risk & Compliance

  1. What keeps you up at night about how [process] is handled today?
  2. Where are you most exposed if [risk event] happened tomorrow?
  3. How would you know if [compliance issue] were happening right now?
  4. What's your biggest single point of failure in this process?
  5. Who is the one person that, if they left, would put this at risk?

Growth & Scale Limits

  1. What happens to [process] if volume doubled next year?
  2. Where does your current approach start to break down at scale?
  3. What's stopped you from expanding [initiative] the way you'd like?
  4. What would need to be true for you to comfortably 3x this?

Team Experience & Morale

  1. What's the most frustrating part of this job, in your team's own words?
  2. Where do you see the most turnover or burnout risk?
  3. What do new hires find hardest to learn about this process?
  4. What do your best people spend time on that feels like a waste of their skill?

Competitive/Market Pressure

  1. Where do you feel competitors moving faster than you on this?
  2. What's changed in the market that makes the old way harder to defend?
  3. What do customers say when this process doesn't go well?
  4. Where have you lost business or renewal because of an issue here?

Prior Attempts

  1. What have you already tried to fix this, and why didn't it stick?
  2. What would need to be different this time for a new approach to work?
  3. What internal resistance did the last attempt run into?

Recap

  1. If you had to name the single biggest problem in this whole process, what would it be?
  2. Which of these would leadership be surprised is still happening?
  3. Which would you fix first if you could only fix one?
  4. Is there a problem we haven't talked about yet that's actually the real one?

Implication Questions (the highest-skill category)

Financial Impact

  1. What does that delay cost you in real dollars?
  2. How does that error rate translate into lost revenue or rework cost?
  3. If that bottleneck adds two weeks to every deal, what does that do to forecasting?
  4. What's the fully loaded cost of headcount used to manually patch this?
  5. If this doesn't improve, what does next year's budget conversation look like?

Downstream/Cross-Functional Effects

  1. How does that delay affect [downstream team]'s ability to hit their numbers?
  2. Who else feels the pain when this goes wrong, even if they never mention it?
  3. What does this bottleneck do to your ability to deliver on commitments to [customer/exec]?
  4. If [team] is waiting on this, what's that doing to their own targets?
  5. How does this issue show up by the time it reaches the customer?

Time & Opportunity Cost

  1. What could your best people be doing instead, if they weren't stuck fixing this manually?
  2. What's the opportunity cost of time spent fixing this instead of growing the business?
  3. If you got that time back, what would you actually do with it?
  4. How much faster could you move on [strategic priority] without this friction?

Risk Escalation

  1. What happens if this small issue becomes a big one during your busiest season?
  2. If this failed publicly, what would that cost you with customers?
  3. What's the worst-case version of this problem, and how close have you come?
  4. If a regulator/auditor asked about this tomorrow, how comfortable would you be?
  5. What happens to your renewal/retention numbers if this pattern continues?

Personal/Career Stakes

  1. How does this reflect on you personally when it comes up in leadership reviews?
  2. What's it costing you personally to manage around this every week?
  3. If this got fixed, what would that free you up to focus on?
  4. What would it mean for your own goals this year if this stopped being a fire?

Compounding/Scale Effects

  1. If this is already a problem at current volume, what happens at 2x scale?
  2. How does this compound when you add more people/products/regions?
  3. What's the cost of NOT fixing this before your next growth push?

Competitive Consequence

  1. If a competitor solved this and you didn't, what would that cost you?
  2. How long can you compete on service/speed if this stays as-is?
  3. What would your customers do if they found a faster alternative?

Confirming Severity

  1. On a scale of 1-10, how much is this actually costing you versus being a mild annoyance?
  2. Is this a "live with it" problem or a "this needs to change this year" problem?
  3. Who else in the business needs to understand how big this actually is?

Need-Payoff Questions (let the customer sell themselves)

Value Realization

  1. If [problem] were solved, what would that mean for your team hitting target?
  2. How would fixing this change the way you report results to leadership?
  3. What would it be worth to get that time back every week?
  4. If error rates dropped to near zero, what would that do for customer trust?
  5. How would solving this change your confidence going into next year's planning?

Speed/Efficiency Payoff

  1. If this process took half the time, what would your team do with the extra capacity?
  2. How much faster could you respond to customers if this bottleneck disappeared?
  3. What would it mean to finally have real-time visibility into this?

Financial Payoff

  1. What would it be worth in hard dollars to recover that lost revenue?
  2. If you redeployed that headcount to higher-value work, what would that be worth?
  3. How would this change your unit economics if solved?

Strategic/Growth Payoff

  1. If fixed, would that change your plans for expanding into [new market/segment]?
  2. How would solving this support the bigger initiative you mentioned earlier?
  3. What becomes possible for the team next year if this stops being a constraint?

Team/Morale Payoff

  1. What would it mean for retention on your team if this frustration went away?
  2. How would this change what your best people spend their time on?
  3. What would it do for morale to finally have a system that just works?

Risk/Confidence Payoff

  1. How much more confident would you feel walking into an audit if this were solved?
  2. What would it mean for your peace of mind heading into your busiest season?
  3. How would this change your answer next time leadership asks about this risk?

Getting Them to Sell Internally

  1. How would you describe the value of solving this to your own boss?
  2. If you had to justify this investment internally, what would you point to first?
  3. Who else in the business would be glad to hear this is finally getting solved?
  4. What would change about how this team is perceived internally if this were fixed?

Confirming and Closing the Loop

  1. So if I'm hearing you right, solving this is worth [X] to you — is that fair?
  2. Where would solving this rank against your other priorities this year?
  3. What would need to be true for you to move on this now rather than later?
  4. If we could deliver that outcome, what would the next step look like from your side?

Additional Situation Questions — Compliance/Regulatory Context

  1. How is [process] currently governed from a compliance/regulatory standpoint?
  2. What audits or certifications does this process need to pass?
  3. Who is accountable if this process fails a compliance review?
  4. How often are you asked to prove compliance around this?
  5. What documentation exists today to support an audit?
  6. Has this process ever failed an internal or external audit?
  7. What regulatory changes are on the horizon that could affect this?
  8. How confident is your legal/compliance team in the current setup?

Additional Problem Questions — Customer-Facing Symptoms

  1. What do customers say directly to your team when [process] breaks down?
  2. How many support tickets or complaints trace back to this in a typical month?
  3. Where does this show up in customer NPS or satisfaction scores?
  4. What's the most common customer-facing symptom of this internal problem?
  5. How often does this issue result in an escalation to leadership?
  6. What's the churn or downgrade impact when this goes wrong for a customer?
  7. How does your support team currently work around this on customer calls?
  8. What do your account teams tell customers when this comes up?

Additional Implication Questions — Board/Investor Level

  1. If this became a board-level concern, what would the conversation sound like?
  2. How does this issue factor into investors' or leadership's confidence in the plan?
  3. What would this cost you in a board meeting if asked to explain it plainly?
  4. How does this risk show up in your company's risk register, if at all?
  5. What would leadership's reaction be if they saw the real numbers behind this?
  6. How does this affect the story you tell the market about scalability?
  7. What's the reputational cost if this problem became publicly visible?
  8. How many quarters can this continue before it becomes strategic rather than operational?

Additional Need-Payoff Questions — Renewal/Expansion Framing

  1. If this were solved before renewal, how would that change the renewal conversation?
  2. What would justify expanding this to other teams/regions once it's working here?
  3. How would solving this change what you're willing to commit to next year?
  4. What would make this an easy "yes" to expand budget for next cycle?
  5. How would you frame the ROI of this to whoever approves next year's budget?
  6. What would need to be true for this to become a multi-year commitment rather than a pilot?
  7. How would proving this out here change your appetite to roll it out company-wide?
  8. What would this be worth to you across the next three years, not just this one?

Universal Closing Probes

  1. What would have to happen for this to become your top priority this quarter?
  2. If you could wave a wand and fix one thing about this today, what would it be?
  3. What does "good" look like for this, in your own words?
  4. Who internally would need to be convinced for this to move forward?
  5. What's stopped this from being fixed already?
  6. If we're still having this same conversation next year, what would that mean?
  7. What would you need to see from a partner to trust them with this?
  8. What haven't I asked that I should have?

Bonus: Follow-up probes to keep any question from going flat

  1. Can you say more about that?
  2. What does that look like in practice?
  3. How often does that happen?
  4. Who else feels that?
  5. What have you tried so far?
  6. What would "solved" actually look like?
  7. Why does that matter to you specifically?
  8. What's driving that?
  9. How long has that been true?
  10. What happens next if nothing changes?

How to use it

Keep this open in a second window or printed beside you during live calls; pull 3-5 questions per SPIN category per call rather than working through it top to bottom, and swap the [process]/[metric] brackets for the prospect's actual language.

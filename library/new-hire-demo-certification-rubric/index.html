New-Hire Demo Certification Rubric

The competency rubric enablement leaders use to formally certify new hires across 9 scored dimensions before anyone runs a live product demo for a real prospect. Includes the full certification process, scoring sheet, and sign-off form.

How to use it

Run every new hire through all three certification stages, score Stage 3 live against the 9 dimensions, and require a signed scoring sheet clearing the pass bar before they're allowed on a live prospect demo.

What's inside

  • 3-stage certification process (shadow, dry run, live-graded)
  • 9 scored competency dimensions with 1-4 level descriptors
  • Full scoring sheet with pass threshold and hard gates
  • Automatic-fail triggers (fabricated results, lost screen share, major overrun)
  • Sign-off form for raters and enablement lead
  • Remediation plan template for candidates who don't clear the bar
  • Re-certification triggers and 12-month cadence
  • Rater calibration protocol to prevent scoring drift

Purpose: No rep runs a live demo for a real prospect until they've been scored against this rubric and cleared the bar. This isn't a training checklist, it's a gate.

Certification Process (3 Stages)

Stage 1: Shadow (Week 1-2) New hire observes 2 live demos run by a certified rep (one discovery-stage, one late-stage). Debrief after each: what was the throughline? Where did proof points land? What would you have done differently?

Stage 2: Dry Run (Week 2-3) New hire records a solo demo (real product, dummy prospect persona assigned by manager) using the Demo Storyboard Template. Manager scores it on this rubric for feedback only, not pass/fail. Minimum one dry run, typically two.

Stage 3: Live-Graded Certification Demo New hire runs either (a) a real low-stakes prospect demo with a manager silently observing, or (b) a peer-panel roleplay with 2 trained raters. Scored live against the rubric below. Scores reconciled between raters within 0.5 points before results are shared.

Scoring Sheet

  • Total possible: 36 (9 dimensions × 4)
  • Certification bar: Average score ≥ 3.0 (27/36 or higher)
  • Hard gates (must each score ≥ 3, regardless of average): #7 Tech-Failure Recovery, #8 Time & Pacing Discipline, #9 Close & Next-Step Command
  • Automatic fail: no averaging around these:
  • Any dimension scores 1 (Not Yet)
  • Fabricates or exaggerates a customer result
  • Loses control of screen share for 30+ seconds with no recovery attempt
  • Goes over agreed time by more than 10 minutes without acknowledging it

Re-Certification Triggers

Certification is valid 12 months, and is re-triggered early by any of:

  • A major demo-flow or product UI change
  • Two consecutive deal losses where the loss-reason review cites the demo as a contributing factor
  • Promotion or transfer into a new segment (e.g., SMB rep moving to Enterprise)

Rater Calibration Note

Before any rater scores a cert demo solo for the first time, they should co-score 2 recorded demos alongside a senior rater and reconcile any score gap greater than 1 point. Re-calibrate the full rater pool every 6 months against a fixed reference recording so the bar doesn't drift up or down over time.

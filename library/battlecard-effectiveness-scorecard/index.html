Battlecard Effectiveness Scorecard

A 100-point rubric that scores every competitive battlecard on accuracy, usage, and measured win-rate impact — so retiring, rebuilding, or promoting a battlecard is a data call, not a guess.

What's inside

  • Accuracy scoring criteria (40 pts): positioning, pricing, features, rebuttal currency
  • Usage scoring criteria (30 pts): findability, CRM reference rate, update recency, rep confidence delta
  • Impact scoring criteria (30 pts): win-rate lift, cycle-time delta, anecdotal behavior change, loss-reason leak check
  • Four scoring bands — Promote / Maintain / Rebuild / Retire — with a required action for each
  • Fill-in scoring worksheet with an evidence-source column per criterion
  • Recommended quarterly cadence and a named scorer panel (enablement + reps + loss-review)
  • Decision log template for tracking retire/rebuild calls over time

Purpose: Score every competitive battlecard on a 100-point scale across Accuracy, Usage, and Impact so you know, with evidence, which battlecards to promote, refresh, or retire — instead of guessing from gut feel or how recently someone remembers updating one.

Cadence: Score every battlecard quarterly, or immediately after a competitor makes a material product/pricing move.

Who scores: Enablement/product marketing lead + 2 quota-carrying reps who've used the battlecard in a live deal in the last 90 days + 1 person who coded a recent loss involving that competitor.

BandScoreAction
Promote80-100Feature as the gold-standard template in enablement training; no changes needed this cycle
Maintain60-79Keep live; assign a light refresh (pricing/feature currency only) before next quarter
Rebuild40-59Structure is fine, content is stale or unused — assign an owner and a 2-week rebuild sprint
Retire0-39Archive immediately. If the competitor is still active in deals, replace the card entirely — do not cosmetically patch a card scoring this low

Rule of thumb: A battlecard nobody can find (low B1) will always score low on impact (C1-C4) regardless of how accurate it is — fix distribution before you rewrite content.

How to use it

Run this quarterly across every active competitive battlecard, scoring with an enablement lead plus reps who've used the card in a live deal; use the band and decision log to force an explicit retire/rebuild/maintain/promote call.

3-Day Sales Kickoff Agenda Builder

Hour-by-hour agenda blocks organized by Vision, Skill-Building, and Celebration days, so every session on the schedule earns its slot.

What's inside

  • Full hour-by-hour Day 1 (Vision & Direction) agenda, 7:30am to reception
  • Full hour-by-hour Day 2 (Skill-Building) agenda, including breakout tracks and roleplay labs
  • Full hour-by-hour Day 3 (Celebration) agenda, including a skill-growth award slot
  • 4 explicit design rules for building any SKO agenda (Vision/Skill/Celebration mapping, 90-minute break rule, practice-majority rule, growth-based recognition rule)

Hour-by-hour blocks for a 3-day SKO, organized so every session earns its slot: Day 1 sets Vision, Day 2 builds Skill, Day 3 is Celebration.

Day 1 — Vision & Direction

TimeSession
7:30–8:30Registration, breakfast, networking
8:30–9:00Welcome + year-in-review (CEO/CRO)
9:00–10:00Keynote: state of the market / strategic vision
10:00–10:15Break
10:15–11:15Product & portfolio roadmap
11:15–12:00This year's #1 company priority — and why it matters to every role in the room
12:00–1:00Lunch
1:00–2:00Customer voice panel (customer guest or CS panel)
2:00–3:15Breakouts by segment/region: territory & quota reveal, team-specific goals
3:15–3:30Break
3:30–4:30Cross-functional alignment: how Marketing/Product/CS support sales this year
4:30–5:00Day 1 recap + "commit card" exercise (write your #1 takeaway)
6:30–9:00Welcome dinner/reception

Day 2 — Skill-Building

TimeSession
7:30–8:30Breakfast
8:30–9:15Keynote: this year's named skill focus, and why mastering it matters for career, role, and results
9:15–10:30Breakout Track A (e.g., Discovery & Qualification)
10:30–10:45Break
10:45–12:00Breakout Track B (e.g., Objection Handling & Negotiation)
12:00–1:00Lunch + mixed-team peer roundtables
1:00–2:30Practice labs: roleplay with manager coaching + peer scoring
2:30–2:45Break
2:45–4:00New tools/process training (CRM changes, new plays, enablement content walkthrough)
4:00–5:00Manager huddles: turn today's skill-building into individual development commitments
7:00–9:00Optional evening activity / regional team dinners

Day 3 — Celebration

TimeSession
8:00–9:00Breakfast
9:00–10:00Closing keynote: tie the 3 days back to the vision (CEO)
10:00–10:15Break
10:15–11:15Awards & recognition — include a skill-growth award (e.g., "Most Improved," tied to competency-level movement), not only revenue awards
11:15–12:00Team commitment share-out: each team presents one commitment for the year
12:00–1:00Closing lunch
1:00–2:00Optional team-building competition / prize drawings
2:00Departures begin

Design rules behind this agenda

  • Every session should map to Vision, Skill, or Celebration — if you can't say which, cut it or move it.
  • No more than 90 minutes without a break or format change.
  • Skill-building day should be majority practice (labs/roleplay), not majority lecture.
  • Recognition on Day 3 should include how someone grew, not only what they closed.

How to use it

Copy the three day-tables into your own SKO planning doc, swap in your specific breakout topics and speakers, and check every session against the four design rules before finalizing.

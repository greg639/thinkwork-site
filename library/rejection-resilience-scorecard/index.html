Rejection Resilience Scorecard

A 5-marker rubric for scoring your own response to a lost deal — time-to-reset, attribution accuracy, next-action speed, pipeline behavior, and self-talk — built to catch a resilience spiral before it wrecks next week's pipeline.

What's inside

  • 5 resilience markers scored 1-5 with concrete descriptors at each anchor point
  • Deal-loss intake fields (deal name, size, stage, date)
  • Total score bands (strong / watch / active risk) with a recommended action per band
  • Repeatable resilience log table to track the trend across multiple losses, not just one bad day
  • Explicit trigger point for escalating to a manager

How to use this

Fill this out within 24 hours of losing a deal you cared about. Score yourself honestly on each of the 5 markers — this is about your response to the loss, not the loss itself. Track your scores over time in the log at the bottom; a downward trend across 2-3 consecutive losses is your earliest warning sign of a spiral, well before it shows up in next month's pipeline numbers.

The 5 Resilience Markers

1. Time-to-Reset

How long between hearing "no" and getting back to full-effort prospecting/pipeline activity?

  • 1 — More than 3 days; activity visibly stalled
  • 3 — Same day or next day, but noticeably lower energy/output
  • 5 — Same day, full effort resumed within hours

Your score: ___

2. Attribution Accuracy

Do you explain the loss with an accurate mix of controllable and external factors, or do you distort it?

  • 1 — Globalized ("I'm just bad at this") or fully externalized/blame-shifted ("it was rigged, nothing I could do")
  • 3 — Partially accurate but leans one way
  • 5 — Specific, accurate mix: names what you controlled and could improve, and what genuinely wasn't in your control

Your score: ___

3. Next-Action Speed

How fast do you take the next constructive action — a real debrief, a follow-up for future opportunity, logging the learning?

  • 1 — No debrief or follow-up happens; the deal is just closed and dropped
  • 3 — Debrief happens eventually, more than a week later
  • 5 — Debrief or learning captured within 48 hours, follow-up path for future opportunity identified

Your score: ___

4. Pipeline Behavior

Does your prospecting/pipeline-building activity in the following week dip measurably vs. your normal baseline?

  • 1 — Clear dip: fewer dials, fewer new opportunities created, visible pullback
  • 3 — Slight dip, recovers within the week
  • 5 — No measurable dip — activity holds at baseline or above

Your score: ___

5. Self-Talk / Narrative Language

How do you describe the loss to yourself and to others — specific/temporary/learnable, or permanent/pervasive/personal?

  • 1 — Permanent, pervasive, personal language ("I always lose these," "I'm not cut out for this")
  • 3 — Mixed language, some catastrophizing creeps in
  • 5 — Specific and temporary language ("this deal, for this reason, this time" — with a named adjustment for next time)

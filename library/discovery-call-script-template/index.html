Discovery Call Script Template

A full opening-to-close discovery call script with branching paths for three buyer personas, ready to adapt and run on your next call.

How to use it

Read the opening and context block verbatim on your next call, pick the branch matching who's on the line, and use the close script to lock a dated next step before you hang up.

What's inside

  • Opening (0–3 min): permission-based agenda-setting script
  • Context-setting questions (3–8 min) common to every persona
  • Branch A script: economic-buyer / budget-holder persona
  • Branch B script: technical-evaluator / champion persona
  • Branch C script: end-user / day-to-day-pain persona
  • Pain-quantification block used across all branches
  • Next-steps close script with mutual action plan language
  • Objection-recovery lines for 'just send me a deck' and 'we're just looking'

Opening (0–3 min)

Thanks for making time: before we dive in, I'll share what I'd love to cover, and I want to make sure it matches what you're hoping to get out of this call. Sound fair?

My plan: understand what's driving this conversation right now, what you've tried, who else is involved, and if it looks like a fit, agree on a clear next step. What would make this a good use of your 30 minutes?

Context questions (3–8 min): ask everyone

  • What prompted you to take this call now, versus six months ago?
  • Who else on your team is feeling this, and how?
  • What have you already tried to fix this?

Route from here based on their answer:

Branch A: Economic Buyer / Budget Holder

  • What's this problem costing the business today, in dollars or lost opportunity?
  • If you solved this, what changes at the P&L level?
  • What's already budgeted for this category this year?
  • Who else needs to sign off once we get to a number?

Branch B, Technical Evaluator / Champion

  • Walk me through how this fits into your current stack.
  • What are the non-negotiables for your evaluation criteria?
  • Who owns the final technical sign-off?
  • What's killed similar projects here before?

Branch C, End User / Day-to-Day Pain

  • Walk me through what this actually looks like in your week.
  • What's the workaround you're using today, and how much time does it eat?
  • If this got fixed, what would you stop doing that you hate doing now?

Pain quantification (use in every branch)

  • How often does this happen: daily, weekly, per deal?
  • What's the cost of one instance of this going wrong?
  • On a 1–10, how urgent is fixing this, what makes it that number and not lower?

Close (last 5 min)

Based on what you've shared, here's what I'm hearing: [restate pain, impact, and who's affected in their words]. Does that sound right, or am I missing something?

Here's what I'd suggest as a next step: [specific next step: demo, technical call, business case review] with [specific people] by [specific date]. Does that work, and is there anyone else who should be in the room?

Objection recovery

  • "Just send me a deck": Happy to, it'll actually be more useful if I tailor it to [their stated pain] rather than send the generic version. Can I ask two quick questions first?
  • "We're just looking": Totally fair, most people looking still have something specific that got them looking today. What was that for you?
